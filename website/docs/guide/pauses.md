# Pauses: Free time and Pause

At the end of the status line, two buttons put Sioul on hold, for two different moments. **Free time**, with a flower, is for a good moment you want to take: the weather is fine now, the energy is there now. **Pause**, apart from it at the very end, is for a moment that is too much. They look different and do different things, so that one is never taken for the other.

Sioul is not an emergency service. It does not watch you, and it acts only when you press one of them.

## Free time {#free-time}

Press **Free time** and now is leisure, whatever the hour, until you press it again.

- **Who reaches you**: your safe senders, at the times you ticked for them ([Accounts ▸ Senders](accounts.md#senders)); everyone else's mail waits. Dose reminders, the codes you just asked a site for and your events' own alarms still come; reminders before dates, sites' notifications and the rest wait. In the status line's menu, **Nothing at all** keeps even your safe senders away this time; doses and codes still come.
- **What is offered**: the Tasks page shows "Free time" and, under "If you want:", your own leisure items, in the order you made them. Nothing to finish, no next step, no timer. Movement and exercise are among them unless you turn **Offer movement and exercise** off in [Settings ▸ Pauses](settings.md#pauses); with **Even days** on (for an illness that limits your energy), they are off unless you turn them on.
- **A focus session** running stops when you press it, and counts; Sioul offers a line on where you stopped.
- **The night comes first**: Free time ends by itself when your night begins (or at midnight without one), and a nap in it is a nap.

### The end of the work day {#the-end-of-the-work-day}

When Free time takes some of your working hours, the end of today's work moves later by as much, and Sioul says it once as you come back: "Today, work runs until 19:00." Then:

- **It never goes past** an hour before you wind down for the night, your latest end of work (two hours after your usual end, unless you set it in [Settings ▸ Pauses](settings.md#pauses)), or the evening's time for you.
- **Only light steps** go after your usual end; the day holds no more than before: what no longer fits goes to the next days, as the plan always lays it.
- **Keep my usual end**, beside that sentence or in the status line's menu, leaves the end where it was, no reason asked. Turn **The end of work moves** off in the settings to make it always so.
- **Sioul ends it**: at the moved end, work rests, as after any work day. **Done for today** ends it earlier, as any day.
- Nothing is counted: no hours owed, no countdown, no record of your pauses.

## Pause {#pause}

### Set it up on a calm day {#set-it-up-on-a-calm-day}

In [Settings ▸ Pauses](settings.md#pauses), take your time:

- **Dose reminders still come**: on unless you turn it off; then they wait until you come back, and are reminded once then.
- **Starred contacts get through**, on the phone: calls and messages from your starred contacts, and anyone calling twice within 15 minutes. Off, no one.
- **What helps you**: in your own words, one thing a line. A line with a link or a file's path opens it from the pause: a playlist, a folder of photos, an app's page.
- **A line for the pause**: one line of yours, shown on the screen.
- **Breathing guide**: off unless you turn it on; then a slow shape to breathe with, shown only when you tap it, the breath out longer than the breath in, at the pace you set.
- **Coming back**: the rest of the day lighter (as a hazy day), no more work that day, or as planned.
- **Numbers for**: the country whose emergency number and crisis line show during a pause; unless you choose, the country of your phone numbers.
- **Try the pause screen** shows it as it will be. Nothing is held.

### Press it {#press-it}

**Pause** asks nothing. Everything Sioul shows is held, on all your devices: no notification but dose reminders (unless you chose otherwise) and the alarms you set on your events, nothing new on screen. A focus session running stops and counts. The work of the paused hours goes to later days, never into your evening. The phone itself stays free: calls, other apps, emergency calls.

On a phone, **Pause** is also among the quick settings, and at a long press on Sioul's icon.

### The screen {#the-screen}

Sioul's window is covered with a few lines: "Paused. Sioul is keeping everything on hold: mail, tasks, messages. Nothing new will show here until you come back. Sioul asks nothing of you until then." Under them: your line, **What helps you** (your list, folded until you open it), the breathing guide if you turned it on, and one quiet line with the emergency number and the crisis line of your country, one tap to call each; **Other numbers** unfolds the rest. One button, **Come back**: no code, no question.

### Coming back {#coming-back}

"You are back. Nothing was lost." The rest of the day is as you chose it, lighter unless you said otherwise, and you may make tomorrow lighter too with one button; doing nothing changes nothing. What was held comes back slowly: the Porch opens at your next admin hours, or when you open it. Nothing asks what happened, nothing rates it, and closing the day in the evening does not mention it.

For ten seconds the status line offers **Undo**: as if the pause had not been pressed.

Sioul keeps only when the last pause began and ended; **Forget the last pause**, in the settings, takes even that away.

## Telling them apart {#telling-them-apart}

| | Free time | Pause |
|---|---|---|
| For | a good moment you choose | a moment that is too much |
| Who reaches you | your safe senders, or no one | no one; dose reminders; on a phone, your starred contacts if you chose so |
| The window | as usual, leisure in view | covered, a few lines |
| Work | the end of the day moves later, within limits | goes to later days, never into the evening |
| It asks | nothing; one key keeps your usual end | nothing |

From Free time, **Keep my usual end** turns it into a pause that moves nothing: pressed at a bad moment, it never costs you an evening.

## Silencing the phone and the computer {#silencing-the-phone-and-the-computer}

During a pause Sioul keeps its own notifications for later, on all your devices. Each device also silences itself, as far as its system lets an application do it, as you set in the pause's settings; the pause's line says what was silenced and what could not be. A pause turns off only what it turned on: a do-not-disturb you set yourself stays as you set it.

- **Android** (10 and later): a "Pause" mode, and a "Free time" one, Sioul's own. Calls and messages from your starred contacts get through, and a second call from the same number within 15 minutes, unless you chose nobody. Alarms always ring, and dose reminders show unless you chose otherwise. What waits stays in the notification shade, silent. Sioul needs Android's "Do Not Disturb access" for this: **Open Android's page** in the pause's settings. Who is starred, you choose in your Contacts app (**Starred contacts**): Sioul never changes it.
    - Emergency services may call back from a number you do not know (in France, 0 800 112 112): a second call within 15 minutes gets through, and you can star that number.
    - **Pause** is also among the quick settings, and at a long press on Sioul's icon: they open Sioul on the pause, and never end it.
- **Plasma**: Plasma's do-not-disturb, shown "While Sioul is active (Pause)" among the notifications. Dose reminders show, unless Plasma is set to hide even critical notifications in do-not-disturb: Sioul then says so, and **Plasma's notification settings** opens the page where Sioul may "Show in do not disturb mode".
- **GNOME** keeps its Do Not Disturb for you, in the top bar. Tick **Switch GNOME's Do Not Disturb on with Sioul's, and off after**, and Sioul does just that; on already, it stays on.
- **Mac**: make two shortcuts in the Shortcuts app, "Sioul pause on", which turns on a Focus (Do Not Disturb, say), and "Sioul pause off", which turns it off: Sioul runs them.
- **Windows** does not let Sioul do it: its switch is in the notification centre (Windows key + N).

## Do not disturb on every device {#do-not-disturb-on-every-device}

Do not disturb is one switch for all your devices: turned on or off on one, it holds on all, as soon as each one hears of it through your sharing folder. Each device silences itself as during a pause, with a mode of its own, "Do not disturb (Sioul)": calls and messages from the people on your list get through, and a second call from the same number within 15 minutes; alarms and dose reminders too.

- **The switch** is in the status line, beside the sounds: a click turns it on until you turn it off; a click again turns it off, on every device. A right click, or a long press, chooses until when: for 30 minutes, an hour, two hours, until the hours you are in end, or until you turn it off. Its words say where it holds: "Do not disturb, on every device, until 15:00", or "here only" when your other devices have not followed; its menu says how each one follows.
- **What turns it on by itself**, each a setting in **Settings ▸ Do not disturb**: the pauses (on); a focus session (off), until half an hour past the time you chose, so that a session you forget never keeps you unreachable; your sleep (off), from winding down to waking.
- **The latest change wins**: turned off while you focus, it stays off until that session ends, and the next one turns it on again. Turned off during a pause, only your devices' own do-not-disturb is lifted: the pause still holds Sioul's own notifications.
- **Who may reach you during do-not-disturb**: one list for all your devices, in **Settings ▸ Do not disturb**, kept sealed in your sharing folder. Start from your safe senders, then add a contact or someone by hand. Their mail is notified; the others' waits, and is told when do-not-disturb ends. Your sites' notifications wait too; dose reminders, the codes you asked a site for, your events' reminders and your meals' and nights' notices come, silently where your device is silenced.
- **On a phone**, Android lets through only the contacts starred in your Contacts app. Sioul says who on your list is not starred on this phone, and opens their contact for you to star it, or Android's form to add them. It reads your contacts for that, and never changes them.
- **On a computer** there are no calls: the list's mail comes as a notification, which Plasma and GNOME show during their do-not-disturb, unless Plasma is set to hide even those; Sioul then says so, with Plasma's notification settings.
- **With Sioul closed on a phone**, Sioul keeps your devices in step in the background, with one quiet notification: do-not-disturb turned on at your computer follows within a few minutes, and new mail is told at its times. Turn it off in **Settings ▸ Do not disturb** (this phone only): changes then come when you open Sioul. There too, allow Sioul to run in the background, or Android may stop it to save the battery.

## What Sioul does not do {#what-sioul-does-not-do}

- It sends nothing: no message, no call, no location, to anyone.
- It does not watch you and cannot tell how you are: nothing starts a pause but your press.
- It is not a medical tool and not an emergency service. In danger, call the emergency number of your country; the pause's screen shows it.
