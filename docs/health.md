# Health and well-being

Kept on this device: `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health.toml` (what you enter), `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health-days.toml` (the days that differ from the usual one: below, "Each day its own") `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/health-state.toml` (doses marked taken, reminders sent, reminders that could not be shown, errands made, chat minutes) `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/health-doses.toml` (each dose that fell due, and the answers your devices captured: below, "Doses as records") and `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/health-back.toml` (the moves of doses taken back, a week: below, "Two kinds of medicines"). These files go nowhere, unless you share between your devices: then they travel sealed, through a folder your sync carries ([database.md](database.md)). The errands it makes are tasks in a list your phone has (below), and their titles name the medicine.

## Medicines
- **What it is**: its name, the brand on the box or your own word for it, which reminders, the day's list and a phone's card say; and, optional, for whoever prescribes or hands it out, the generic name of its molecule as you type it (the INN, "levothyroxine"; « DCI » in French) and its strength ("75 µg", "500 mg per tablet"), apart from a take's amount ("1 tablet"). With a generic name and no name, the generic name is its name. When it was first taken, if you know (**Taken since**, its own form only). In both forms; in a prescription's rows, beside the name on a computer, folded under it on a phone (a tap unfolds them; filled, the fold says them).
- **When**: at set times each day, each a take (below), every few days from a day ("every other day at 08:00, from Sunday 4 October"), or every few hours ("every 8 hours; next: today at 17:30"); until a day, or for as long as it goes; paused for now.
- **Takes**, at set times each day: rows in the medicine's form and in its prescription's (`qml/TakesEditor.qml`), each a time picked, not typed (an hour, then a minute every five, the minute set kept when it falls between: `qml/TimeField.qml`; Tab and the arrows reach both), and its own amount, only where it differs from the medicine's dose (1 tablet at 08:00, 2 at 20:00: the dose "1 tablet", the 20:00 row "2 tablets"). **Add a take** adds a row at a usual time not taken yet; each row can be taken out. No take, a time that does not read, or two takes at one time (one dose, whose amount would be lost) are said, and nothing is saved (`Medicine::set_takes`, `health::apply_medicine`).
- **On the page**, its content rather than its settings (`qml/MedicinesSection.qml`): under the day's list, or in a column of their own on a wide window (below, "The page"). Each medicine with its dose and its times ("07:30", "12:30 · 20:00"), or, when its takes' amounts differ, each take with its own ("08:00 · 1 tablet, 20:00 · 2 tablets"); every few days or hours in the words above; and until when; its name said precisely, with its generic name and strength ("Thyrolan — levothyroxine 75 µg"; the generic name once when it is the name: `Medicine::precise`); paused, or past its last day, quieter. **Add a medicine** and **Edit** open its form (`qml/MedicineDialog.qml`, made the first time, scrolling when its takes are many), and **Take it out** there asks once. Nothing of today's doses: they are in the day's list.
- **Each dose says its take's amount**: in the day's list, its reminder (desktop and phone), the Porch, the question on doses due while Sioul was closed, a phone's home screen card ("Iron · 2 tablets", `Health::doses`, by the time of day the take is set for, so that a dose a change of hour moves, 02:30 to 03:30, keeps its amount). The doses fall due as before: a take's amount never enters a dose's key (`<medicine>@<time>`), its marks, its record or its reminder's timing.
- **On the day**: each dose in the day's list and its timeline (below, "The page"); today's with "Taken". A dose taken says when; one click takes it back. Only today's: a dose of a day to come is refused by the window's Taken, Not taken, the question of when it was taken and the choice between differing answers (`health::may_answer`), since marked it would leave the alarms a phone is given and its reminder would not come; the alarms' own Taken keeps no such check, a reminder showing a minute before a dose due at midnight. Taken again, a dose already marked taken writes its mark again at this Taken's time and this device's own "taken" anew (`health::taken_again`), never a second move of its next doses (the move stays as the first Taken made it, which taking it back restores): a take back written earlier on another device then leaves the dose taken, since each is a newer word than that take back (marks and answers travel entry by entry, the later word winning; the check of e2324fe, must-fix 1). What stays in that race: a medicine taken every few hours keeps its next dose counted from the start the take back restored, and its reminder then says when it was last taken and from when the next may be (below, "A health file that does not read"). The late question asks the same: a dose marked already says "Already marked taken at 08:03" beside its Taken, which writes the mark again at the time typed (a time corrected so), no second move; a dose the schedule no longer has (moved by its own take) never moves the next ones again (`Health::taken_at`). A dose not marked is simply not marked: nothing counts what was missed, nothing turns red. Another day shows its doses plainly, without marks: the page keeps no record of the past.
- **Two kinds of medicines**: at set times of the day, they keep their times (being ready for something); every few hours, they keep the hours between two doses, which the body (the liver, the kidneys) needs to clear one before the next: each dose taken, on time, early or late, sets the next one that many hours after it (`Health::taken_at`, the schedule's start moved to the time taken plus the interval). A mark taken back puts the doses back where they were, unless they moved again since (`HealthState::moved`). A dose's time corrected (marked again at another time) moves its next doses from that time, as the first mark did from its own: the same minute is the same take; later, the one move is made again from where it started and the start it replaced put back everywhere; earlier, nothing moves (`taken_again`). The take backs travel in a file of their own (`health-back.toml`, `dose_backs::DoseBacks`, a week): each device follows every move from the medicine's start, the latest mark's, then puts back where a take back ends unless a move still marked ends there, until it stands still, so that taking back one dose never undoes another's move (`Health::mend_shifts`; the check of 1b6795e, H2 and H3: `a_take_back_never_undoes_another_dose_s_move`); and never before the end of a move still marked: two devices that each moved it, neither knowing of the other (the desk's Taken of 14:00, the phone's late Taken of the 08:00 dose), leave everywhere the medicine written last, maybe with the earlier end, and the start goes on to the later, so that no dose comes before the interval after a take (the A3 dose model: `no_start_before_a_marked_take_s_interval_whichever_version_won`). Two different times said for one dose, one on each device before either heard of the other, keep one mark, the one written last, and both answers, said as differing (below, "Doses as records"). The move lives in the medicine, which travels whole between devices; changed meanwhile on another device (its note, its dose's text), the medicine there, its start as it was, could win everywhere and bring the next dose early. The moves travel with the marks, apart: each minute, a medicine whose start is one a mark moved it from goes again where that mark moved it, its other changes kept, and that goes to the other devices (`Health::mend_shifts`; review of 5 October 2026, F23; `share::correctness_tests::a_late_dose_s_move_survives_an_edit_of_the_medicine_elsewhere`).
- **Taken late**: "Taken" pressed more than half an hour past the dose's time (on the page, in its notification, or for a dose due while Sioul was closed) asks when it was taken, now unless you say (`qml/DoseTaken.qml`), or that it was not; a medicine taken every few hours says its next dose comes that many hours after the time given.
- **Reminders**: one desktop notification per dose, within half an hour of its time, without sound, with "Taken" where the desktop's notifications take buttons (Linux; on Windows and macOS the notification has none, and the dose is marked on the page). Not repeated. Reminders come in quiet time too: they are yours; and while you sleep, unless you asked doses to stay silent then (below, "Do not disturb"). On a phone they come otherwise, with the phone's sound (below, "On a phone").
- **While Sioul was closed**: a dose of the last twelve hours, past its half hour, neither marked nor reminded anywhere, is asked about on the Porch at the next start, and above the day on this page ("Due while Sioul was closed, and marked nowhere Sioul can see: did you take them?", with **Taken…** and **Not taken**); a notification asks once a session, its "Answer" opening the Porch. A question on the past, never a reminder to take one now. Answered, it is not asked again.
- **What became of a reminder** (review of battery part C, the third to sixth reviews, 14 October 2026). **The rule**: a dose's reminder is plain only on the device that knows it is the dose's first reminder; every other reminder, on any device, says the doubt first ("Check first: …"). A device knows it is the first in two ways only, each resting on a word written after the device it answers opened, never on a wait: the computer that kept the doses at the dose's time (or at waking, or at a pause's end), at its first try then, once every phone has said in the folder, before that time, that it read this computer's current opening; or a phone's alarm that has read, from every other device that could have reminded the dose, that device's own word that it did not. Two phones, a phone off or flat, a stall of any length, a restart: each can at most add a "Check first", never a plain second reminder, but for one case no design can close (below, "What it cannot know").
  - **Each device's word** (`sioul_core::dose_reminders`, `health-reminders.toml`, a file of its own in the health part, each device's entry for a dose written by it alone and travelling whole): before each notice of a dose goes, the device writes that it is going (`maybe`; taken back only when the bus says no server took it, `ServiceUnknown`; any other refusal or a server gone with it may come after it showed, and keeps it); when one is shown (its server answered, or a phone's alarm showed it), `shown`; when its first try surely did not go out (nothing sent), `unshown`; and a promise, `quiet`: no notice of the dose from it until then (`NEVER`: none again). Each device takes only its own entries out, two days after their dose by its clock, never another's (a clock days ahead would else empty the others'); a device that stopped sharing has its entries taken out by every device that reads it so; any device's entries go a month after their dose (`STALE_DAYS`: a device forgotten, or reinstalled under a new id), forgetting being one device's view, counted not by the clock of the device that prunes alone but by the earlier of it and the newest dose the other devices' entries name, so that a clock a month ahead, there or on one other device, takes nothing of today's out (the ninth review's P1; a device's entries then stay while no device but the one that prunes names a dose a month newer). A file of its own because an older Sioul (0.0.3 to 0.0.5) does not know it and writes nothing in it, where a table of `health-state.toml` or a field of `health-doses.toml` would be dropped by its next save and taken out everywhere by the sharing; it keeps the file's changes waiting instead, forever, its quiet exchanges off (0.0.5) and its waiting list growing: **update every device at once** (docs/database.md). `health-state.toml` still says `reminded` and `unshown` for an older Sioul: a reminder not known shown is written there `unshown`, read by it as a dose not reminded, its alarm ringing plainly as it always did. A file that does not read is never read as "nothing done": a phone's alarm then says "what your other devices did to remind it could not be read here"; it is read again from every device's at the next exchange, and the doses due before are said not known for a day.
  - **The handshake** (`health::first_here`, `devices::Entry::acked`; review of battery part C, the sixth and seventh reviews): each phone says, in its own entry (sealed with the sharing's key and bound to its id, as the rest of the entry), the opening of each computer's entry it read, the latest it ever read (an older copy put back never lowers it: the eighth review's N3; an entry dated more than ten minutes ahead of the phone's clock is set aside while it reads so, `FUTURE`, so that a clock once far ahead holds nothing there once set right: the ninth review's F1), of each computer it counts now; a computer it stops counting (said off, forgotten, silent a week) goes from it. Only phones write it, only of computers. A computer is a dose's first only if it is alone (sharing off, or no other device in the folder), or if its opening is written into the folder (its entry), its claim and records written, it kept the doses since before the dose's time (`lease::Keeper::since`; kept alone, since its process began), and every phone in the folder that has not stopped sharing, that you have not forgotten on this computer, and that no other device says it replaces ([database.md](database.md#devices), `replaces`: a new phone in place of a dead one) (counted or not, said off or not, silent or not) says, in its entry, this very opening (a word naming another opening, later or impossible, says nothing), and was heard on this computer within two hours (a copy of its entry newer by its own times than any read here, `devices::written`: a living phone restates it each hour, `devices::RESTATED`; an older copy put back by whoever serves the files is no news of it, and the word is read from the newest copy, of a phone whose entry reads now, never from an older one: the ninth review's W1; and it stands only where the copy read now says it too: a copy that is no news (an older one put back, one written in the same second as the one before, one of a phone whose clock was set back) may take the word away, never give it, and a phone whose copy read now is set aside, its clock more than ten minutes ahead of this computer's (`FUTURE`), gives none (`newest_of`; the final review before 0.0.6, F1H); and of the two copies of its entry read here (the folder's, the one fetched beside it), a computer that either copy no longer names is withdrawn, whichever is dated later, while a copy naming an older opening of it is only behind and takes nothing away (`devices::chosen`; the re-check before 0.0.6, LOW-2); a time seen here ahead of this computer's clock now, set back since, is not within the two hours; past them, the phone may have taken its word back unheard, said this computer off or stopped counting it: `WORD_FRESH`, the eighth review's N1, N2), seen here before the dose's time less two minutes and the most a phone's clock was measured ahead (`word_lead`); a device whose entry does not say what it is (an older Sioul, one not read) never says it, and the computer is never the first while it is in the folder. A computer back after three minutes or more without a tick of its own (asleep, the machine frozen: `WAKE_GAP`) opens anew: its entry says a new start, and a phone's word on the old one says nothing of it. Without all that it is as a computer opened after the dose: while a phone is in the folder (counted or not; or the sharing's key not read, the others unknown), no notice until five minutes past that phone's alarm, promised (`QUIET_AFTER`), then the doubt first, never plain, each other device named ("the phone may have reminded you too, by its own alarm"; "another of your devices may have reminded you at 08:00" when none can be named); its key not read, or its claim or opening not written, it says so: "this computer could not tell your other devices it was open, and one of them may remind you of it too".
  - **The computer** (`health::plan`): its first reminder (the handshake holding, nothing of its own recorded for the dose) is plain within its window as the first (the wait for news: seven minutes while a phone may remind, `LATE_UNTIL`, ten without); the devices' doubts said first when there are any ("Knowing": what looks back, a record lost, a device apart, a line lost, counted from the dose's time, the moment a device may be opened for it from waking). Another device's notice sent and not known shown is said first, named: "desk may have shown its reminder at 08:00". Its first try fits in the tick's ten seconds (`notify::FIRST_TRY`: the connection, its two matches a second each, the server's two answers), and is recorded as it goes. Not gone out, it is tried again, five seconds apart for two minutes, then half a minute (each waiting as a slow bus needs, `notify::TRY_AGAIN`), always the doubt first: "Check first: Levothyroxine · 75 µg", "08:00. Sioul can't tell whether it was taken: its reminder, due at 08:00, could not be shown in time, and it may have been taken since without being marked. Check before taking it."; a late notice whose own record cannot be written goes all the same, the doubt first. While a phone may remind, none goes from seven minutes after the dose to five minutes after the phone's alarm, promised before that quiet begins; after it, the tries go on, the doubt first, whether the phone rang or not (off, flat, its alarms not set), until the dose is marked, answered or reminded anywhere this computer has read, or its half hour is over (then promised `NEVER`). The doses seen kept elsewhere a moment end the tries for now, nothing promised: back here within the half hour, the tick takes the dose up again, the doubt first. A notice sent and not answered in time is never sent again: its answer is listened for on its connection however late (a day at most), then it is recorded shown and said again in place, the doubt first, "its reminder, due at 08:00, was not confirmed in time, and it may have been taken since without being marked" (its "Taken" still counts, once; pressed before that answer, it is never said again over the press; a new number the server gives it, as GNOME does, is followed); or taken away, never recorded shown, when it is no longer to remind; refused late, or its server gone with it unanswered, it may have shown, and it is tried again, the doubt first ("its reminder, due at 08:00, was not confirmed in time"), while the half hour lasts. A notice still shown is taken away by its number once its dose is marked or answered anywhere this computer has read.
  - **The phone's alarm** (`dose_reminders::others`): a computer keeping the doses, it waits for news ten minutes (`WAIT_FOR_NEWS`, from waking for a dose that waited for it), then reads what each other device that counts and shares the doses did. Shown somewhere: silent. Plain only when each of them is known, by its own word, not to have reminded it: its promise of quiet covering this moment (its clock's lead and two minutes allowed) with nothing sent; or a computer closed cleanly, everything it wrote read here, until a few minutes after the alarm (`QUIET_AFTER`, clocks allowed), and only when its entry is the one this phone said it read, or a later one (an older copy put back reads as no word), this alarm's own pull from the server went through since the dose, and nothing that computer wrote after its close was read (a claim renewed while open, an entry of the reminders): opened since, it is never the first before this phone said it read that opening, and it keeps quiet past the alarm. A computer never takes another computer's "closed" as its word. Else it reminds, titled "Check first: …", each device said first: "desk may have shown its reminder at 08:00" (sent, not known shown), "desk could not show its reminder at 08:00, and may have shown it since" (not gone out, no promise read), "desk was in use and may have reminded you at 08:00" (in use, no word of its own read), "FP5 may have reminded you too, by its own alarm" (a device whose entry does not say what it is), "desk has its clock more than ten minutes ahead of this one's, so what it says is set aside here" (its entry set aside: its times and promises say nothing here, and the doubt says the same, once; never "an older Sioul there?", which its claims, dated ahead too, would else suggest: the final review's words), "desk closed at 23:00, and its news can be slow to come" (closed as last heard, not its word: no pull through, or something later read), "tablet may have reminded you too, by its own alarm" (another phone, always), "what your other devices did to remind it could not be read here" (the file not read); then the devices' doubts ("Knowing"). Kept by the phone itself (in use at the dose's time), it waits two minutes for the computers' promises (`FIRST_WORD`). Before it shows, it records its own word (`shown`) and `reminded`.

- **Knowing, before saying**: a dose taken twice can harm, so Sioul never says a dose was not taken when it cannot know (below, "Knowing").

## Doses as records
Each dose that falls due becomes a record your devices share (`sioul_core::doses`, `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/health-doses.toml`, in the sharing's health part), so that whether it was taken is an explicit state, not the absence of a mark:
- **Opened, not taken yet** (the canary): the device that sees it fall due (the window's minute, a phone's alarm) opens it, with the medicine, the time it was due, and itself (`opened.<device> = <when>`), for doses of the last twelve hours that no device answered. Two devices opening it before they see each other make one record, both openings kept, the earliest named.
- **Answered**: the first device to capture your answer turns it into taken (when you took it: earlier when you say so, "Taken…") or skipped ("Not taken"), noting itself and when (`answer.<device> = { state, at, noted, name, kind }`). Each device writes only its own opening and its own answer: the sharing, where the later word wins entry by entry, never has two devices fighting over one entry, and an answer travels whole.
- **Merged when read** (`doses::answered`): the same answer on several devices is that answer, the earliest captured; answers that differ are all kept and all said, the earliest first ("Marked taken on the phone at 08:02, skipped here at 08:10. Check which is right before taking it."), on the Porch and on this page, with **Taken** and **Not taken**: you choose (`health::choose`), and the answers saying otherwise go, on purpose. A merge never takes anything out. A mark taken back ("one click takes it back") takes every "taken" out, on purpose.
- **Kept** nine days after the dose (the page's week back, read only, and two days), then dropped by each device from its copy.
- **What it names**: the medicine and when the dose was due, never its amount: a take's amount changed afterwards changes no record.
- **The marks beside**: `taken` and `not_taken` in `health-state.toml` are still written with each answer, for an older Sioul, which reads them; and read as answers of a device not named. An older Sioul does not know the new file: its changes wait there, never written, never taken out.
- **Hardened** as the record of marks (below): never read as empty when it does not read, or is gone though it was written before (a witness file beside it); the sharing reads it again from every device's records, the broken one is kept aside, and the doses due before stay not known for a day.

## Knowing
Sioul may run on several devices that share through a folder another program syncs, late or not at all. Whether a dose was taken is said only when it is known; otherwise the doubt is said, with why and which device, and the person checks (`sioul_core::health::doubts_now`).
- **What each device says of itself** ([database.md](database.md), "Devices"): when it last started, when it last closed cleanly, whether it is working now (up from its start to the last step of a clean close, once an export read all it recorded: a crash, or a final export that failed, leaves it up), when it last read the others and when it last wrote its own, and how far its records went then.
- **Known not taken**: a dose due at T, answered nowhere this device has read, is known not taken when this device's record reads, and every other device that counts is either
  - **closed cleanly**, everything it wrote up to its last export read here: closed after T, or before T and not started since. Its last export holds every answer it captured, and it captures none until it starts again. Its close and its start are both on its own clock. Closed before T, it may be picked up right then to mark the dose, its sync slow to wake (a phone in a pocket); a phone is also opened by its own alarm, ten minutes after T when a computer keeps the doses (`ALARM_AFTER`), which may remind the dose or have it marked: from then on it is known only once the news of that opening had the time to come, counted from its alarm, never from T (review of battery part C, H2; `sioul_core::health::tests::a_phone_put_away_is_known_from_its_alarm_once_that_rang`). ## On a phone
Android stops an app it does not see: Sioul in the background on a phone cannot remind at the minute as the desktop does. So each coming dose (the next two days, those not marked) is given to Android's alarm clock (`crates/sioul-app/src/alarms.rs`, `android/`), again whenever a dose is marked or a medicine changes. At its time Android wakes a small part of Sioul, even when Sioul was stopped, which first asks the sync app to bring what your other devices marked and reads it, then decides as the window would:
- **taken, said not taken, or reminded already** somewhere: nothing;
- **not known the first**: another device's word missing or saying it may have reminded it (a notice sent, a first try not gone out without a promise of quiet read, a computer in use not heard on it, another phone's own alarm): after the wait for news, the reminder titled "Check first: …", each device said first (above, "What became of a reminder");
- **kept by a device you use now**: its turn first; asked again after the wait for news (from the dose's time; held by sleep or a pause, from waking);
- **not known** (a device in use not heard since the dose, one that stopped without closing, an older Sioul: "Knowing" above): after the wait for news, the reminder titled "Check first: …", with why;
- **otherwise**: the reminder, the medicine and its time, with **Taken** (more than half an hour late, Taken opens Sioul on when it was taken).
The reminders come on Android's "Doses" channel, at its default importance: the phone's notification sound, no banner, yours to change in Android's settings (`DoseAlarms.java`). A reminder tapped opens Sioul on its dose. Android must let Sioul set exact alarms (Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Sioul ▸ Alarms & reminders); without it, alarms may come late, up to an hour (`setAndAllowWhileIdle`), and the page says so.

## Kept as
`health.toml`, readable and editable by hand. A medicine at set times each day:

```toml
[[medicine]]
id = "iron"
name = "Iron"
dose = "1 tablet"
prescription = "thyroid-and-iron"
[medicine.schedule]
every = "day"
times = ["08:00", "20:00"]
```

When its takes' amounts differ, a field Sioul 0.0.3 does not know, `amounts`, holds every take's amount by its time, and `dose` holds them all in one line:

```toml
dose = "08:00 · 1 tablet, 20:00 · 2 tablets"
[medicine.schedule]
every = "day"
times = ["08:00", "20:00"]
[medicine.schedule.amounts]
"08:00" = "1 tablet"
"20:00" = "2 tablets"
```

- **The same amount at every take**: no `amounts`, `dose` that amount: the file is as before, field for field.
- **Generic name, strength, first day**: `generic = "levothyroxine"`, `strength = "75 µg"`, `since = 2025-08-04` on the medicine, each written only when given. `name` is always written, the generic name standing in when no name is given, as 0.0.3 needs one. None of them changes when a dose falls due, its key or its reminder's time.
- **What Sioul 0.0.3 drops**: it reads `generic`, `strength` and `since` as nothing, and leaves them out at its next save of `health.toml` (any change on its Health page), as it does `amounts`; the name, the dose, the times and every dose stay. Shared, the medicine's entry then travels without them, and your other devices lose them too: update every device together.
- **An older Sioul** (0.0.3) reads the times as before, so every dose falls due, reminds and is marked as before, with the same keys; it says the whole line with each dose ("Iron · 08:00 · 1 tablet, 20:00 · 2 tablets"), never one amount that is wrong for some takes. At its next save of `health.toml` (any change on its Health page) it drops `amounts`; the times stay, and so does the whole line, which this Sioul then says with each dose until the form sets the takes again. Tested on a copy of 0.0.3's own types (`an_older_sioul_reads_the_times_and_says_every_amount`).
- **Sharing**: a medicine travels whole, one entry by its id ([database.md](database.md)): the new field travels with it, and an older device keeps it in its file until it saves the file itself (above). Your devices are updated together.

## Meals, rest and sleep

- **Each day its own** (3, 21–23): on the page's day or week, a block is a tap away from what changes it, that day only, nothing asked: **15 min later** (as often as you like; the minutes are yours), **Move to…** a time, **Change its times…** (from, to; a meal's minutes to get it ready, a nap's to come back; the night's bedtime and waking), **Not today** (no notice that day, the time kept free all the same; **Today after all** takes it back), **Back to usual**, **Remove from today** (neither kept free nor said: its time goes back to the plan; the row stays, muted, with **Put back**); and **Add a meal or a rest…** for that day alone (removed with **Remove**); the night's **No alarm at 07:00** (below, "The alarm at waking"). On another day the words say "that day". All of it in `needs::Needs::change_day`.
- **Moved by hand**: on the page's timeline (`qml/HealthTimeline.qml`, `qml/TimeDrag.qml`), a meal with its getting ready, a nap with its coming back, or the night is dragged to another time on its day: whole, it keeps its length; by its bottom edge, a meal or a nap changes its length; the night's top edge is bedtime (its winding down before it, as long as it was), its bottom edge waking, in the next morning's column (tonight's waking is in tomorrow's: the next day, or the week). Where it would go is drawn while the hand moves, with a tag above it out of the finger's way: its times by whole five minutes ("12:45–13:35"), and "eating from 13:05" or "winding down, bed at 23:30". Let go, it is that day's change as **Move to…** and **Change its times…** make it (`health::drag_need`, then `Needs::change_day`), the plan goes around it at once, and "Undo" waits ten seconds in the status line, putting back what the drag wrote and only that (`Days::put_back`: its time, its waking, its length, the time kept before and after it, as read under the file's lock with the drag itself, `change_day_seen`): a choice made since or beside it on the same block, the night's alarm or "Not that day", from this device or another, stays (the check of 36002ea, low 21); let go where it was, nothing changes and nothing waits. A day past and a block over stay as they are; in the week a block moves within its own day; the night keeps its bedtime on its evening (noon to noon). With a mouse: press and drag (the wheel scrolls the hours). With a finger: a long press picks it up and the finger moves it; a plain swipe scrolls as before; a long press let go in place opens its menu. On a phone the whole day sits in a third of the screen, where a meal is a few pixels tall: a long press there opens the timeline **readable** (an hour 56 pixels tall, the page's height, the list behind it), the minute under the finger kept under it, and the block under the finger comes with it when there is no doubt which: the finger on it, or the only one within 20 pixels; two as close (a meal right after the night's end), none, and the next long press chooses. Readable, the ends that can be taken show a grip; **Done**, in a bar under the hours with how it works, or Back, folds it to the whole day. The keyboard has the menus (**Move to…**, **Change its times…**). One set of handlers for the whole timeline finds what lies under the pointer: the blocks carry none, and the page opens no slower.
- **Kept as times**: in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health-days.toml` (`needs::Days`), a table per day and block, readable and editable by hand: `[2026-10-07."meal:1"]` with `at = "13:30"`; `wake` for the night, `minutes`, `before`, `after` only where they differ from the usual ones; `quiet = true`, `off = true`; the night's `no_alarm = true`; one added has `kind` and `name`. So a usual time changed afterwards moves the usual days, never a day already changed; a meal or a nap taken out of the settings takes its changes with it, and those after it follow to their new place (`Days::removed_usual`). A past day stays as it was (`DayProblem::Past`): the page goes a week back at most, read-only; changes are dropped two weeks after their day. Today's moves an older Sioul kept in `needs-today.toml` are moved in once.
- **Pushed past events**: a meal that would fall in an event moves after it, the time to come back from it counted (its margins, [tasks.md](tasks.md)), by whole five minutes, on every day laid out, so that the page and the plan agree; not a meal whose time you set for that day, nor one added that day: you chose it with the day in view (`Needs::past_events_on`). Its notices, the plan and the day follow.
- **Travels**: a day's changes go to your own devices with the sharing's health part (`data/health-days.toml`, one entry per field), so that each plans the same day; a file rather than a table in `health.toml`, which an older Sioul would drop at its next save, and the sharing everywhere with it. The notices given stay on the device that gave them (`needs-today.toml`). None of it goes to anyone else (25).
- **Never** (16–19, 29–31): food, portions, numbers, colours, streaks, counts, praise, generated text; no record of what was eaten or slept, no "missed", no countdown during a meal.
- **Not built yet**: the heads-up at a breakpoint inside its window (8), a block tied to an event of yours rather than the clock (10), an if-then plan for the transition (28).

## The alarm at waking
On a phone, an alarm can ring when the night ends (`crates/sioul-app/src/wake.rs`, `needs::Needs::alarm_of`; Android's side in [android.md](android.md), "Waking").
- **Which mornings**: seven switches under the usual night in the page's settings, Monday to Sunday in the language's order, none at first. A switch is the morning it rings on, not the evening the night starts. Kept with the night in `health.toml` (`[needs.sleep] alarm = [true, true, true, true, true, false, false]`, Monday first), so set from any device; only a phone rings it. The setting says so ("Rings on your phone…"), and when it rings next.
- **When**: at the night's end as the page has it that day: the usual waking, or the night's own waking that day (**Change its times…**, **15 min later**); on the clock where the phone is, through the changes of hour (a waking the change skips, 02:30 on the last Sunday of March in Paris, rings at 03:30). A night removed that day: no alarm that morning. A night "Not today" still rings: that choice silences the night's notices, never the waking, which is asked for on its own.
- **No alarm that morning**: the night's row says "alarm at 07:00"; its menu has **No alarm at 07:00**, and back, **Alarm at 07:00 after all**, for that night only, even after midnight while it goes on (`no_alarm = true` on the night's own day in `health-days.toml`). **Back to usual** brings it back too.
- **The ring**: the phone's alarm sound, rising from very low to full over half a minute, until **Stop** or **10 min later**, over the lock screen, the time and those two buttons, nothing else. After **10 min later**, a quiet line says when it rings again, with **Stop**.
- **Never stopped silently**: the phone is given the wakings of the next eight days, again whenever the night, a day's change or the mornings change, here or from another device (the sharing). It asks Sioul again at each ring, as each night starts and ten minutes before each ring (another device may have changed the night while Android kept Sioul frozen), after a restart and a change of time or zone. When Sioul cannot answer (its file not readable, Sioul not opened for weeks), the phone goes on with what it has, then with the usual mornings, repeated.
- **Try the alarm**, on the phone, under the mornings (shown with the night off too, so that trying it changes nothing of the night): it rings ten seconds later, as a waking would, over the lock screen; nothing is written, and the next waking stays as it is. Its **10 min later** rings once more, then **Stop** alone. When Android refuses what it needs (exact alarms, notifications, the full screen), it does not ring and says why.
- **Not tried on a phone yet**: built and checked on a computer (Rust's tests; the Java compiled against `android-36`).

## Do not disturb
The night, from winding down to waking, and each nap with its minutes to come back, are **sleep**, one of the five times ([areas.md](areas.md)): what you set above makes them. While you sleep, almost no notification comes, as usual (the matrix's sleep column, `sioul_core::attention`, `crates/sioul-app/src/hours.rs`; [attention.md](attention.md)): no site, no reminder before a date, no pause to move, no meal or nap notice; the codes you just asked for come, unless their sleep cell says On the Porch only. The night's own notice at its time, the start of the wind-down, still comes: it says the night begins and, while that day's review is not given, has **Close the whole day** beside **Options…** (Linux): how the whole day felt, what it asked and gave in words, tomorrow's first step ([reviews.md](reviews.md)). What waits comes at waking when it still makes sense (a reminder before its date passes, a site's notification at the next gathered time); codes stay on the Porch. What the window shows is shown when you open it.
- **Doses come all the same**: you set their times, and a dose at 05:00 is meant to wake you. Settings ▸ What reaches you ▸ Sioul's own, the doses' sleep cell: At once (the default) or Later (`[attention] doses`; seeded once from `[reminders] doses_in_sleep`, [attention.md](attention.md) §7). Later, a dose due while you sleep is reminded at waking, once, its time said (taken more than half an hour late, "Taken" asks when), and the question on doses due while Sioul was closed, and the doses on the Porch, wait for waking too; on a phone, Android's alarm asks again at waking, and when a computer keeps the doses it waits for that computer's news from waking, as it does from a dose's time (the computer reminds the dose at waking too: the two never remind it together, both plainly; `health::news_from`, review of battery part C, the fourth round). Sioul never silences a dose without that choice.
- **During a pause** ([pauses.md](pauses.md)): as during sleep, nothing notifies but doses, unless you hold them there too (What reaches you ▸ Sioul's own, the doses' pause cell, `[attention] doses`; seeded once from `[pause] doses`; on a phone, Android's alarm asks again five minutes on, and once the pause is over waits for a computer's news from then, as at waking); held, they are reminded once on coming back. Alarms always ring. In Free time, doses come as always. Sioul never silences a dose without that choice.
- **The alarm at waking ends the sleep**: it rings at the night's end, which is no longer sleep (`quiet::Blocks::at`: the end left out), and the phone rings it without asking Sioul whether it may. Android's own do-not-disturb lets alarms through, unless set to total silence.
- **No night set**: no sleep at night, and nothing keeps notifications away. The Porch asks for it in a card, "Set my night", which opens this page at its meals and night, or "Leave as is".

## Prescriptions
- What it is for, who wrote it, until when it is valid, how many days the pharmacy gives at a time, when it was last fetched, a note: its form (`qml/PrescriptionDialog.qml`), from **Add a prescription** or **Edit** on the page.
- **On the page**, under it, its medicines a line each when one has a generic name or a strength ("Thyrolan — levothyroxine 75 µg · 07:30 · 1 tablet"); else, as before, the names its title does not say.
- **Its medicines, as rows in its form**: each its name, its dose and its takes (above, "Takes"); the medicines tied to it already (`Medicine::prescription`) come as rows. **Add a medicine** adds a row, at set times each day, one take at 08:00 to change; a row added and left empty is not kept. A row taken out goes at once when it was added there; a medicine saved before stays in the form, said in its row ("Iron is taken out when you save: no more reminders for it.", **Keep it** puts it back), and goes when the form is saved: no more reminders, its marks kept, as **Take it out** in its own form. A medicine taken every few days or hours shows when in words; its own form changes its schedule. The form is saved whole or not at all: a row without a name, or takes that cannot be set, are said with the medicine's name, and nothing changes (`health::apply_prescription`). A medicine made there is a medicine like any other: on the page, in reminders, in the doses.
- **On the page**, after the medicines: each with who wrote it, then in words the next visit to the pharmacy and the day to renew it by, the soonest first ("pharmacy from Tuesday 27 October · renew by Thursday 4 February 2027"; once that day has passed, "valid until …", never a word of lateness), the medicines tied to it when its title does not already name them ("for Levothyroxine and Magnesium"), and **Fetched today**, with a check once pressed that day (`health::prescription_rows`).
- **Errands**: two days before the medicines run out, a task "Pharmacy: …"; two weeks before the prescription ends, a task "Doctor: renew the prescription for …". Each is made once, tagged `health` (shown in quiet time), in the list chosen in the page's settings ("Errands go to"): else your usual task list, else the first list on a server, so your phone has them; a list on this device only when there is no other. Errands made on this device before move to that list once. "Fetched today" counts the next pickup from today.

## Moving
While a focus session runs, every 45 minutes (the Health page's settings), a pause to move and stretch is offered, never imposed: "Pause now" pauses the session and offers a line on where you stopped ("Where you stopped" in [tasks.md](tasks.md)); "Not now" goes on and asks again after as many minutes; unanswered, the session keeps counting, since a pause missed must not stop the time counted (something urgent may come first). "Back to it" goes on. The pause is counted again for each new session. Outside a focus session, while you are at this computer, a quiet notification says the same after as many minutes (ten at least).

## Chats
A limit a day, off by default: after the minutes chosen (counted while a chat is in front of you), chats are covered, blurred, muted and silent (their notifications wait), for the minutes chosen; then they come back by themselves.

## The page
The day at a glance, and what changes it (`qml/HealthPage.qml`, `qml/HealthTimeline.qml`; its view `health::page`, made off the window's thread, the week of the day shown at once). A header: ‹ Today, Tuesday 6 October ›, a day at a time (a week in the week); **Day** or **Week**; **Today** when elsewhere; ⚙.
- **The day**: a timeline, the same hours every day of the week (half an hour before the earliest waking or first thing, to half an hour after the latest bedtime or last thing), in three lanes: the day's events, and today's planned steps, faded, for context only; meals, naps and the night as blocks, getting ready, winding down and coming back lighter than the time itself, a quiet one lighter still; the doses as dots, hollow when due, filled when marked taken, warm when another device may know ("Check first": never "not taken"). A line at now, moved at each minute's turn by the window's one clock (`qml/Clock.qml`) and at once when the window comes back, alone: nothing is laid again for it. Beside it on a wide window, under it on a phone: the same as a list, in time order: each row its times (getting ready and winding down included, as the notices say), its name, how that day has it ("eating from 12:30", "changed for this day", "moved after an event", "this day only", "no notice that day"), and **15 min later** and **⋯** (the menu above), a dose **Taken**; whole-day events and a refill or a renewal falling that day on a line above; **Add a meal or a rest…** below; under it, how the day went in the words said then and their notes, and today **Close the whole day** in the evening and during the night ([reviews.md](reviews.md)). Today, under the list: what is not known of doses marked elsewhere, and where reminders come.
- **The medicines and the prescriptions**, after the day (above, "Medicines", "Prescriptions"): under its list, after the notes on doses, or, on a window that leaves the page 1400 pixels, in a column of their own on the right, as wide from the start (340 to 440 pixels), kept in the week: switching Day and Week moves nothing there, and a week being planned has the next pharmacy visit and renewal in view. On a narrower window or a phone, the week's seven columns take the page: the medicines are the same every day and their doses are its dots. On a phone they come below the day's first screen.
- **The week**: seven narrow columns, Monday to Sunday, today on a lighter ground with its line at now (both move at midnight, before the page is read again); the same blocks and dots, a tap on a block gives the same changes, for that day (someone who plans the coming week on Mondays fixes a day that will differ there); a day's title opens it.
- **Doses due while Sioul was closed**: the question (above, "Medicines"), above the day, only then.
- **A health file that does not read** (edited by hand, written by a newer Sioul), or **of no bytes** (a crash before its bytes reached the disk; Sioul never writes it empty, its tables always written), is never read as no medicines, nor written over (`Health::change` refuses: written empty, the sharing would take every medicine out on every device). It is written as the doses' records are (`write_witnessed`): its bytes on the disk before it takes its name, its witnesses made, so that a file of no bytes, or one gone though written here, reads as lost (`Health::read_bytes`). A Taken there makes its move on that copy and records it beside the mark (`HealthState::moved`), for every device whose file reads to make at its next minute; a take back keeps the move as taken back (`health-back.toml`, a week, a file of its own: a device on an earlier Sioul drops nothing of it, the check of 1b6795e, M2), put back here and on every device whose file reads, unless the doses moved again since; the window's minute keeps its mended copy when the write is refused. The reminders, a phone's alarms, the Porch, the home card and this page keep the doses as last known, each said so, to check before taking: "Your medicines could not be read here: the doses shown are as last known. Check before taking one.", above the day and the week alike; the question on doses due while Sioul was closed says it too; the night read so (a sleep keeping doses silent holds). The file is copied aside as `health.toml.unreadable`. With no copy either (none kept yet, or it does not read), this page, the Porch and the home card say "Your medicines could not be read here", and a phone is handed no list (`alarms_coming`: nothing), so that Android keeps the alarms it has, as with the wakings (`wake::schedule`); one of them that rings is shown as the alarm that reads shows it (recorded reminded, said to your other devices, once across restarts), titled Sioul, its time and that sentence said, unless the dose was marked, its time is past the Porch's twelve hours, a recorded move of its medicine replaced it, or your sleep keeps doses silent; a change that would leave it unreadable is held and said; a removal made in Sioul is marked and travels with its mark (`Health::removed`, a week), and is never held; removals that come with no mark and would take every medicine out here (two or more, none left) are held alone, the rest written (a medicine added there comes), said with "Take them out everywhere" on the device that sent them and "Take them out here too" on the one they reach, in Settings ▸ Your folder and sharing, until you say (`share::release_medicines`; ## Your watch
Sioul read a Garmin watch's own files (FIT) until 8 October 2026: the day's heart rate, stress, Body Battery, steps and sleep on this page, gentle offers of a pause or a walk between tasks, and a line on the Tasks page after a short night. That feature was taken out, because the watch's health figures are not shown to be accurate and Garmin has no official interface, only unofficial connectors that risk being blocked; the research on how a watch's data maps, and why its figures are doubtful, stays in [research/wearables.md](research/wearables.md).
- **Your files stay where they are**: Sioul no longer reads, writes or shares anything of a watch, and deletes nothing. The folder you chose for its files, the days it kept (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/watch/`) and the memory of its offers (`$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/watch-offers.json`) are yours to keep or delete.
- **An older configuration still loads**: `watch_folder` and `watch_offers` in `health.toml`, and a `watch` row under `[attention]` or `[notify]`, are read and left aside, as any key Sioul does not know; `health.toml` drops the two keys the next time Sioul writes it (`health::tests::the_watch_s_old_keys_are_read_and_left_aside`).
- **An older device**: the watch's days and offers that a device on an older Sioul still shares are left aside here, never written and never kept waiting, and the rest of the exchange goes on ([database.md](database.md), "Stores that leave, arrive or move").
