# Health and well-being

Kept on this device: `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health.toml` (what you enter), `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health-days.toml` (the days that differ from the usual one: below, "Each day its own") and `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/health-state.toml` (doses marked taken, reminders sent, errands made, chat minutes). These files go nowhere, unless you share between your devices: then they travel sealed, through a folder your sync carries ([database.md](database.md)). The errands it makes are tasks in a list your phone has (below), and their titles name the medicine.

## Medicines
- **When**: at set times each day ("12:00, 18:00"), every few days from a day ("every other day at 08:00, from Sunday 4 October"), or every few hours ("every 8 hours; next: today at 17:30"); until a day, or for as long as it goes; paused for now.
- **On the page**, its content rather than its settings (`qml/MedicinesSection.qml`): under the day's list, or in a column of their own on a wide window (below, "The page"). Each medicine with its dose and its times ("07:30", "12:30 · 20:00"; every few days or hours in the words above), and until when; paused, or past its last day, quieter. **Add a medicine** and **Edit** open its form (`qml/MedicineDialog.qml`, made the first time), and **Take it out** there asks once. Nothing of today's doses: they are in the day's list.
- **On the day**: each dose in the day's list and its timeline (below, "The page"); today's with "Taken". A dose taken says when; one click takes it back. A dose not marked is simply not marked: nothing counts what was missed, nothing turns red. Another day shows its doses plainly, without marks: the page keeps no record of the past.
- **Two kinds of medicines**: at set times of the day, they keep their times (being ready for something); every few hours, they keep the hours between two doses, which the body (the liver, the kidneys) needs to clear one before the next: each dose taken, on time, early or late, sets the next one that many hours after it (`Health::taken_at`, the schedule's start moved to the time taken plus the interval). A mark taken back puts the doses back where they were, unless they moved again since (`HealthState::moved`).
- **Taken late**: "Taken" pressed more than half an hour past the dose's time (on the page, in its notification, or for a dose due while Sioul was closed) asks when it was taken, now unless you say (`qml/DoseTaken.qml`), or that it was not; a medicine taken every few hours says its next dose comes that many hours after the time given.
- **Reminders**: one desktop notification per dose, within half an hour of its time, without sound, with "Taken" where the desktop's notifications take buttons (Linux; on Windows and macOS the notification has none, and the dose is marked on the page). Not repeated. Reminders come in quiet time too: they are yours; and while you sleep, unless you asked doses to stay silent then (below, "Do not disturb"). On a phone they come otherwise, with the phone's sound (below, "On a phone").
- **While Sioul was closed**: a dose of the last twelve hours, past its half hour, neither marked nor reminded anywhere, is asked about on the Porch at the next start, and above the day on this page ("Due while Sioul was closed on all your devices: did you take them?", with **Taken…** and **Not taken**); a notification asks once a session, its "Answer" opening the Porch. A question on the past, never a reminder to take one now. Answered, it is not asked again.
- **Knowing, before saying**: a dose taken twice can harm, so Sioul never says a dose was not taken when it cannot know (below, "Knowing").
- **One device reminds**: the one you are using (`sioul_sync::lease`: the device used most recently keeps the medicines' reminders, and acts once it has kept them a minute and a half, time for your other devices to know). A dose marked taken goes to your other devices at once (a sharing exchange right away), and the question on doses while Sioul was closed waits until the others were heard from, so a dose marked elsewhere comes first.
- **Sharing is not optional for this**: with medicines and Sioul on more than one device, share between them (Settings ▸ Sharing). Without it, the page says the doses are known to this device only; with it but your other devices not heard from for five minutes, it says since when, because a dose marked there may not show here yet. The doses' marks are always among what sharing carries (`state/health-state.toml`).

## Knowing
Sioul may run on several devices that share through a folder another program syncs, late or not at all. Whether a dose was taken is said only when it is known; otherwise the doubt is said, with why, and the person checks (`sioul_core::health::doubts`).
- **Known** means: this device's record of doses reads, and every other device sharing with it (heard in the last month) was heard after the dose was due, with everything it wrote until then read here; or it said it closed before, and its news usually comes within three minutes (had it opened again, that would be known by now).
- **How a device knows another**: each renews its claim on the doses every minute (`sioul_sync::lease`), saying how far it wrote its records (its round and the number of the last, `Claim::wrote`); the reader knows how far it read each one's records (`share::Heard`). Read that far: everything said until the claim is known. A device says it closed when Sioul quits, or when a phone puts it away (`share::closing`), after one last exchange, and opens again when it is back. How late each one's news comes is timed only while watching (a claim found after this one was closed is old, not late), over its last sixty claims, kept on this device only (`share/peers.json`).
- **Every device counts**: one that shares through the folder (it exchanged this last week) but whose claim does not read here yet (its seal not arrived, a damaged file) is a device never heard from: its doses are not known, said so.
- **Not known**: a device last heard before the dose was due ("your laptop was last heard on Monday 5 October at 07:52"); one closed whose news can come late (a phone's eDrive brings files every half hour); one that never said how far it wrote (an older Sioul); a line of another device's records that could not be read here, around the dose's time (never passed over in silence any more: `share-other-line`); this device's own record broken or gone.
- **What changes**: a reminder not known waits up to ten minutes for news, then comes all the same, titled "Check first: …", with why; a reminder is recorded as given only when it was shown (a desktop without a notification server, a phone before its alarms: another device still reminds it, and the question on doses due while closed still asks about it); the doses due while Sioul was closed, and today's doses not marked, show the doubt under them, on the Porch and this page. The page says what may be missing from the others.
- **The record, hardened**: never read as empty when it does not read, or is gone though it was written before (a hidden witness beside it says so): it is repaired instead. Sharing on, the sharing first forgets holding it and reads it again from every device's records, this one's too (`share::rebuild`), so an empty new record takes no mark out elsewhere and gets back what was written; then the broken one is kept aside (`health-state.toml.unreadable-<time>`) and a new one starts. The doses due before stay "not known" for a day (`health-doubt.toml`, this device only). The record is changed under a lock shared with the sharing (`sioul_core::filelock`): before, the minute's tick could write back an older copy over a dose another device had marked meanwhile, and the sharing then took it out everywhere.
- **What it cannot know**: a sync that stops without saying, while another device is closed and its news usually fast: had it opened again and marked a dose, this device could not know. Time between devices is their clocks'; a clock far off shortens the delays measured.
- **Tested**: `cargo test -p sioul-core health` (doubts; a record that cannot be trusted), `filelock`, `cargo test -p sioul-sync share` (what was read is known, a broken line said, a lost record rebuilt with nothing taken out elsewhere), `lease`, `cargo test -p sioul-app health` (what the claims teach).


## On a phone
Android stops an app it does not see: Sioul in the background on a phone cannot remind at the minute as the desktop does. So each coming dose (the next two days, those not marked) is given to Android's alarm clock (`crates/sioul-app/src/alarms.rs`, `android/`), again whenever a dose is marked or a medicine changes. At its time Android wakes a small part of Sioul, even when Sioul was stopped, which first asks the sync app to bring what your other devices marked and reads it, then decides as the window would:
- **taken, said not taken, or reminded already** somewhere: nothing;
- **kept by a device you use now**: its turn first; asked again after the wait for news;
- **not known** (a device not heard since): after the wait for news, the reminder titled "Check first: …", with why;
- **otherwise**: the reminder, the medicine and its time, with **Taken** (more than half an hour late, Taken opens Sioul on when it was taken).
The reminders come on Android's "Doses" channel, at its default importance: the phone's notification sound, no banner, yours to change in Android's settings (`DoseAlarms.java`). A reminder tapped opens Sioul on its dose. Android must let Sioul set exact alarms (Settings ▸ Apps ▸ Sioul ▸ Alarms & reminders); without it, alarms may come late, up to an hour (`setAndAllowWhileIdle`), and the page says so.
## Meals, rest and sleep
Set first, the work planned around them (`sioul_core::needs`). The usual ones are set once, in the page's settings (`qml/NeedsSection.qml` in `qml/HealthSettings.qml`); a day that differs is changed on the page itself, that day only (below, "Each day its own"). The research behind it: [research/meal-prompts.md](research/meal-prompts.md), whose criteria are numbered below.
- **Blocks**: meals (three when first turned on, one in the evening: Murphy et al. 2010; more in one step, "a meal or a snack"), naps (with minutes to come back after, as waking up takes a while: Hilditch et al. 2017), and the night (bedtime, waking, an hour to wind down: Harvey et al. 2021). Each is named as you like (none needed: "Breakfast", "Nap"…), at the time you set, on the weekdays you choose, its notices on or off (criteria 1, 4, 14, 23, 26, 27). Meals planned more than four hours apart are said where they are set, never in a notice (2).
- **Kept free**: a meal from getting it ready to its end, a nap with its minutes after, the night from winding down to waking. The plan lays no task in them (`plan::Settings::with_needs`), every day, as each day has them (`with_days`); the Tasks page's day shows meals and naps, a step going around them (3).
- **Two notices at most, each once** (5): a heads-up about the work, "No new big task", with the block's name and time, a quarter of an hour before, how long as you choose (criteria 4, 6, 9), then one at the time, its name and time. Each has one button, **Options…**, where the desktop's notifications take buttons (Linux; on Windows and macOS a notice is its name and time alone, and the same moves are on the Health page's day), which opens the question in the window (`qml/Interruption.qml`): a quarter of an hour later (you choose; as often as you like), **Move to** a time, or **Not today**, today only and without a word asked, and one line on where you stopped (7). A notice is never given twice for a block, moved or not: no nagging. Nothing else is said: none while an event goes on (the time stays kept: 22), none for a block not today (21), none when one passes (20). The same moves, and more, are on the page, for any day of its week (below). Only the block's name and time: safe on a screen someone else reads (24). Notices are said by the day a block starts on: a bedtime after midnight still has its own (before, it had none). From the computer you are at, as the doses. Not on a phone yet: there Android shows Sioul's notifications for the doses and the time running only ([android.md](android.md)); the blocks are kept free in the plan all the same.
- **Each day its own** (3, 21–23): on the page's day or week, a block is a tap away from what changes it, that day only, nothing asked: **15 min later** (as often as you like; the minutes are yours), **Move to…** a time, **Change its times…** (from, to; a meal's minutes to get it ready, a nap's to come back; the night's bedtime and waking), **Not today** (no notice that day, the time kept free all the same; **Today after all** takes it back), **Back to usual**, **Remove from today** (neither kept free nor said: its time goes back to the plan; the row stays, muted, with **Put back**); and **Add a meal or a rest…** for that day alone (removed with **Remove**); the night's **No alarm at 07:00** (below, "The alarm at waking"). On another day the words say "that day". All of it in `needs::Needs::change_day`.
- **Moved by hand**: on the page's timeline (`qml/HealthTimeline.qml`, `qml/TimeDrag.qml`), a meal with its getting ready, a nap with its coming back, or the night is dragged to another time on its day: whole, it keeps its length; by its bottom edge, a meal or a nap changes its length; the night's top edge is bedtime (its winding down before it, as long as it was), its bottom edge waking, in the next morning's column (tonight's waking is in tomorrow's: the next day, or the week). Where it would go is drawn while the hand moves, with a tag above it out of the finger's way: its times by whole five minutes ("12:45–13:35"), and "eating from 13:05" or "winding down, bed at 23:30". Let go, it is that day's change as **Move to…** and **Change its times…** make it (`health::drag_need`, then `Needs::change_day`), the plan goes around it at once, and "Undo" waits ten seconds in the status line, putting that day's block back as it was; let go where it was, nothing changes and nothing waits. A day past and a block over stay as they are; in the week a block moves within its own day; the night keeps its bedtime on its evening (noon to noon). With a mouse: press and drag (the wheel scrolls the hours). With a finger: a long press picks it up and the finger moves it; a plain swipe scrolls as before; a long press let go in place opens its menu. On a phone the whole day sits in a third of the screen, where a meal is a few pixels tall: a long press there opens the timeline **readable** (an hour 56 pixels tall, the page's height, the list behind it), the minute under the finger kept under it, and the block under the finger comes with it when there is no doubt which: the finger on it, or the only one within 20 pixels; two as close (a meal right after the night's end), none, and the next long press chooses. Readable, the ends that can be taken show a grip; **Done**, in a bar under the hours with how it works, or Back, folds it to the whole day. The keyboard has the menus (**Move to…**, **Change its times…**). One set of handlers for the whole timeline finds what lies under the pointer: the blocks carry none, and the page opens no slower.
- **Kept as times**: in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/health-days.toml` (`needs::Days`), a table per day and block, readable and editable by hand: `[2026-10-07."meal:1"]` with `at = "13:30"`; `wake` for the night, `minutes`, `before`, `after` only where they differ from the usual ones; `quiet = true`, `off = true`; the night's `no_alarm = true`; one added has `kind` and `name`. So a usual time changed afterwards moves the usual days, never a day already changed; a meal or a nap taken out of the settings takes its changes with it, and those after it follow to their new place (`Days::removed_usual`). A past day stays as it was (`DayProblem::Past`): the page goes a week back at most, read-only; changes are dropped two weeks after their day. Today's moves an older Sioul kept in `needs-today.toml` are moved in once.
- **Pushed past events**: a meal that would fall in an event moves after it, the time to come back from it counted (its margins, [tasks.md](tasks.md)), by whole five minutes, on every day laid out, so that the page and the plan agree; not a meal whose time you set for that day, nor one added that day: you chose it with the day in view (`Needs::past_events_on`). Its notices, the plan and the day follow.
- **Travels**: a day's changes go to your own devices with the sharing's health part (`data/health-days.toml`, one entry per field), so that each plans the same day; a file rather than a table in `health.toml`, which an older Sioul would drop at its next save, and the sharing everywhere with it. The notices given stay on the device that gave them (`needs-today.toml`). None of it goes to anyone else (25).
- **Never** (16–19, 29–31): food, portions, numbers, colours, streaks, counts, praise, generated text; no record of what was eaten or slept, no "missed", no countdown during a meal.
- **Not built yet**: the heads-up at a breakpoint inside its window (8), a block tied to an event of yours rather than the clock (10), an if-then plan for the transition (28).

## The alarm at waking
On a phone, an alarm can ring when the night ends (`crates/sioul-app/src/wake.rs`, `needs::Needs::alarm_of`; Android's side in [android.md](android.md), "Waking").
- **Which mornings**: seven switches under the usual night in the page's settings, Monday to Sunday in the language's order, none at first. A switch is the morning it rings on, not the evening the night starts. Kept with the night in `health.toml` (`[needs.sleep] alarm = [true, true, true, true, true, false, false]`, Monday first), so set from any device; only a phone rings it. The setting says so ("Rings on your phone…"), and when it rings next.
- **When**: at the night's end as the page has it that day: the usual waking, or the night's own waking that day (**Change its times…**, **15 min later**); on the clock where the phone is, through the changes of hour (a waking the change skips, 02:30 on the last Sunday of March in Paris, rings at 03:30). A night removed that day: no alarm that morning. A night "Not today" still rings: that choice silences the night's notices, never the waking, which is asked for on its own.
- **No alarm that morning**: the night's row says "alarm at 07:00"; its menu has **No alarm at 07:00**, and back, **Alarm at 07:00 after all**, for that night only, even after midnight while it goes on (`no_alarm = true` on the night's own day in `health-days.toml`). **Back to usual** brings it back too.
- **The ring**: the phone's alarm sound, rising from very low to full over half a minute, until **Stop** or **10 min later**, over the lock screen, the time and those two buttons, nothing else. After **10 min later**, a quiet line says when it rings again, with **Stop**.
- **Never stopped silently**: the phone is given the wakings of the next eight days, again whenever the night, a day's change or the mornings change, here or from another device (the sharing). It asks Sioul again at each ring, as each night starts and ten minutes before each ring (another device may have changed the night while Android kept Sioul frozen), after a restart and a change of time or zone. When Sioul cannot answer (its file not readable, Sioul not opened for weeks), the phone goes on with what it has, then with the usual mornings, repeated.
- **Try the alarm**, on the phone, under the mornings (shown with the night off too, so that trying it changes nothing of the night): it rings ten seconds later, as a waking would, over the lock screen; nothing is written, and the next waking stays as it is. Its **10 min later** rings once more, then **Stop** alone. When Android refuses what it needs (exact alarms, notifications, the full screen), it does not ring and says why.
- **Not tried on a phone yet**: built and checked on a computer (Rust's tests; the Java compiled against `android-36`).

## Do not disturb
The night, from winding down to waking, and each nap with its minutes to come back, are **sleep**, one of the five times ([areas.md](areas.md)): what you set above makes them. While you sleep, no notification comes (`sioul_core::quiet::may_notify`, `crates/sioul-app/src/hours.rs`): no code, no site, no reminder before a date, no pause to move, no meal or nap notice, nothing from the watch. The night's own notice at its time, the start of the wind-down, still comes: it says the night begins and, while that day's review is not given, has **Close the day** beside **Options…** (Linux): how the whole day felt, what it asked and gave in words, tomorrow's first step ([reviews.md](reviews.md)). What waits comes at waking when it still makes sense (a reminder before its date passes, a site's notification at the next gathered time); codes stay on the Porch. What the window shows is shown when you open it.
- **Doses come all the same**: you set their times, and a dose at 05:00 is meant to wake you. Settings ▸ Reminders and notifications ▸ **Doses during sleep**: Remind (the default) or Stay silent (`[reminders] doses_in_sleep`). Silent, a dose due while you sleep is reminded at waking, once, its time said (taken more than half an hour late, "Taken" asks when), and the question on doses due while Sioul was closed waits for waking too; on a phone, Android's alarm asks again at waking. Sioul never silences a dose without that choice.
- **The alarm at waking ends the sleep**: it rings at the night's end, which is no longer sleep (`quiet::Blocks::at`: the end left out), and the phone rings it without asking Sioul whether it may. Android's own do-not-disturb lets alarms through, unless set to total silence.
- **No night set**: no sleep at night, and nothing keeps notifications away. The Porch asks for it in a card, "Set my night", which opens this page at its meals and night, or "Leave as is".

## Prescriptions
- What it is for, who wrote it, until when it is valid, how many days the pharmacy gives at a time, when it was last fetched, a note: its form (`qml/PrescriptionDialog.qml`), from **Add a prescription** or **Edit** on the page.
- **On the page**, after the medicines: each with who wrote it, then in words the next visit to the pharmacy and the day to renew it by, the soonest first ("pharmacy from Tuesday 27 October · renew by Thursday 4 February 2027"; once that day has passed, "valid until …", never a word of lateness), the medicines tied to it when its title does not already name them ("for Levothyroxine and Magnesium"), and **Fetched today**, with a check once pressed that day (`health::prescription_rows`).
- **Errands**: two days before the medicines run out, a task "Pharmacy: …"; two weeks before the prescription ends, a task "Doctor: renew the prescription for …". Each is made once, tagged `health` (shown in quiet time), in the list chosen in the page's settings ("Errands go to"): else your usual task list, else the first list on a server, so your phone has them; a list on this device only when there is no other. Errands made on this device before move to that list once. "Fetched today" counts the next pickup from today.

## Moving
While a focus session runs, every 45 minutes (the Health page's settings), a pause to move and stretch is offered, never imposed: "Pause now" pauses the session and offers a line on where you stopped ("Where you stopped" in [tasks.md](tasks.md)); "Not now" goes on and asks again after as many minutes; unanswered, the session keeps counting, since a pause missed must not stop the time counted (something urgent may come first). "Back to it" goes on. The pause is counted again for each new session. Outside a focus session, while you are at this computer, a quiet notification says the same after as many minutes (ten at least).

## Chats
A limit a day, off by default: after the minutes chosen (counted while a chat is in front of you), chats are covered, blurred, muted and silent (their notifications wait), for the minutes chosen; then they come back by themselves.

## The page
The day at a glance, and what changes it (`qml/HealthPage.qml`, `qml/HealthTimeline.qml`; its view `health::page`, made off the window's thread, the week of the day shown at once). A header: ‹ Today, Tuesday 6 October ›, a day at a time (a week in the week); **Day** or **Week**; **Today** when elsewhere; ⚙.
- **The day**: a timeline, the same hours every day of the week (half an hour before the earliest waking or first thing, to half an hour after the latest bedtime or last thing), in three lanes: the day's events, and today's planned steps, faded, for context only; meals, naps and the night as blocks, getting ready, winding down and coming back lighter than the time itself, a quiet one lighter still; the doses as dots, hollow when due, filled when marked taken, warm when another device may know ("Check first": never "not taken"). A line at now. Beside it on a wide window, under it on a phone: the same as a list, in time order: each row its times (getting ready and winding down included, as the notices say), its name, how that day has it ("eating from 12:30", "changed for this day", "moved after an event", "this day only", "no notice that day"), and **15 min later** and **⋯** (the menu above), a dose **Taken**; whole-day events and a refill or a renewal falling that day on a line above; **Add a meal or a rest…** below; under it, how the day went in the words said then and their notes, and today **Close the day** in the evening and during the night ([reviews.md](reviews.md)). A row and its block light up together, under the pointer or chosen; a block tapped brings its row into view; the morning part of a night begun the day before, which is not in the day's list, opens that night's menu instead (after midnight, its alarm alone: the rest of a past day stays as it was). Today, under the list: what is not known of doses marked elsewhere, where reminders come, then what the watch says, once one is set up (made after the rest).
- **The medicines and the prescriptions**, after the day (above, "Medicines", "Prescriptions"): under its list, between the notes on doses and the watch (which waits for them, so that it does not move), or, on a window that leaves the page 1400 pixels, in a column of their own on the right, as wide from the start (340 to 440 pixels), kept in the week: switching Day and Week moves nothing there, and a week being planned has the next pharmacy visit and renewal in view. On a narrower window or a phone, the week's seven columns take the page: the medicines are the same every day and their doses are its dots. On a phone they come below the day's first screen.
- **The week**: seven narrow columns, Monday to Sunday, today on a lighter ground with its line at now; the same blocks and dots, a tap on a block gives the same changes, for that day (someone who plans the coming week on Mondays fixes a day that will differ there); a day's title opens it.
- **Doses due while Sioul was closed**: the question (above, "Medicines"), above the day, only then.
- **⚙** (`qml/HealthSettings.qml`, made the first time it opens; on the right, all of a phone's): what is set once: where the pharmacy and renewal errands go, the usual meals, naps and night, the watch's folder and offers, the pause to move, the limit on chats. The Porch's "Set my night" and Settings ▸ Hours open it at the meals and night (`showNeeds`).
- **Calm**: no red, no strikeout, no count, nothing about food; the rows keep their places as the day goes (one over keeps its row, without its buttons); on a phone the day's rows fit the screen with its timeline. Made at first use: the week, the settings, the menu, the forms; the list a moment after the first frame, the medicines after it.

## Your watch
What a Garmin watch measured, read from its own files (FIT), never through a Garmin account: nothing Garmin changes on its servers can break it, and nothing goes to a server, except sealed to your other devices when you share with them (`crates/sioul-core/src/wearable.rs`).
- **Where the files come from**:
  - a folder you choose behind the Health page's ⚙ ("Its files come to"): Gadgetbridge's exports (the open Android companion that can replace Garmin Connect, synced with Syncthing), Garmin's own export ZIPs (the full account export, or a day's wellness export), or the watch's folders copied by hand. ZIPs inside ZIPs are opened;
  - the watch itself, when the desktop shows its `GARMIN` folder: a watch in mass-storage mode (older models), GNOME's MTP mount (gvfs), or KDE's with kio-fuse. Recent watches speak MTP only; on KDE without kio-fuse, copy the watch's `GARMIN/Monitor`, `Sleep` and `HRVStatus` folders into the folder above (Dolphin shows them).
  - Looked at every quarter of an hour, and at once when a watch appears. Each file is read once (its serial and the time it was made), whichever way it came. Workouts (`Activity`) are left aside: their tracks are not needed here.
- **What is kept**, one small file a day (`~/.local/share/sioul/watch/<day>.json`): heart rate; stress as Garmin scores it (heart-rate variability while still, not a feeling); Body Battery (an undocumented field, kept only between 0 and 100); steps (the watch counts them up, Sioul keeps what each interval adds); resting heart rate; sleep levels and score; the night's HRV.
- **What the page shows**: when the watch last gave data; in words, the day's steps, the resting heart rate with its usual (the median of the last 14 mornings), last night's sleep as a length and its hours (no stages: watches judge them poorly), Body Battery, the week's averages; then today's curves, plain, without colours as grades. No goal, no streak, no score.
- **Gentle offers** (on unless you untick them), at a breakpoint only (a task done, a focus session ended), never in quiet time or once the day is done, 45 minutes apart at least, six a day at most, a declined rule resting longer each time (a nudge refused and repeated becomes noise):
  - five minutes on your feet after sitting 45 minutes (by the watch's steps);
  - a short pause when the watch scored stress high for most of the last half hour while you sat (the offer never names stress: the score tracks excitement as much as strain);
  - after 15:00, a low Body Battery: "Move the rest to tomorrow and call it a day?", with "Done for today";
  - midday to 16:00, room for a twenty-minute walk when Body Battery is high and steps are few.
