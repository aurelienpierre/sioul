# Invoices: the law, the standard and the countries

**The question**: what must the invoices Sioul issues say and do, first for a French entrepreneur individuel (a micro-entrepreneur under the VAT franchise, or liable for VAT), then for people elsewhere or in a company: their mentions, numbers, corrections, deposits and payment terms; the books they feed and the thresholds of 2026; the calendar of electronic invoices; the European standard, EN 16931, whose vocabulary one record can use for every country; and which countries a template can serve at all.

Researched on 16 November 2026, in two reports (French law; other countries and electronic invoices), trimmed here to what the design rests on. Findings 76 to 80 of [the findings](../research.md) come from it; what was built is in [projects.md](../projects.md) ("Invoices", "The books") and [accounting.md](../accounting.md) ("The declaration", "Payments on your invoices", "Invoices on one device"). Left out here: the Rust libraries that write and check the issued file and the validators (the issued file's own research, with its phase), payment QR codes, the artiste-auteur's case, and the approved platforms' offers.

**How evidence is graded**: each claim carries its source, official texts first (Légifrance, the BOFiP, impots.gouv.fr, service-public, URSSAF; EU law read on legislation.gov.uk and in the Official Journal, since EUR-Lex refuses automated reading; each country's administration), then the standards (EN 16931's rules as published for Peppol; Factur-X). **secondary** marks a claim resting on a specialist or commercial site, or on common practice; **UNVERIFIED** one not confirmed at a primary source during the research; *inference* Sioul's own reasoning. Nothing here is legal advice, and each figure says when it holds.

Abbreviations: CGI, Code général des impôts; ann. II, its annexe II; C. com., Code de commerce; BOI, a BOFiP reference; SP, an entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr sheet; LPF, Livre des procédures fiscales.

## 1. France: what an invoice says

### 1.1 The texts
- **C. com. L441-9** (every professional; version in force since 26 April 2019): every purchase of goods or services for a professional activity is invoiced, at delivery or completion; both parties keep a copy. It lists the commercial mentions: the parties, the dates, quantities and prices, discounts, the payment date, the escompte, the penalties, the €40 indemnity, the purchase order's number. A fine up to €75,000 for a natural person.
- **CGI art. 289 and ann. II art. 242 nonies A** (the tax mentions), in force since 1 January 2025 (décret 2024-1195 of 21 December 2024); its new wording (the SIREN, the delivery address, the nature of the operations, the option for debits) applies from 1 September 2026, and **from 1 September 2027 for micro-enterprises and SMEs**.
- **C. com. R123-237** (persons registered) and **R526-27** (an EI's name), as amended by décret 2022-725 (in force 15 May 2022).
- Official summaries: SP F31808, "Mentions obligatoires" (checked 11 August 2026), and SP F23208, "Tout savoir sur la facturation" (checked 7 August 2026).

### 1.2 The seller
- **« EI »**: an entrepreneur individuel's name carries "entrepreneur individuel" or "EI" immediately before or after it, nothing in between, on every professional document and correspondence; each bank account given to the activity carries it in its title (C. com. R526-27; also R123-237 9°). Example: "Camille Exemple EI". A micro-entrepreneur is an EI.
- **The address** (L441-9; R123-237 3°) and **the SIREN** (R123-237 1°; 242 nonies A I-1°); the SIRET is accepted in practice, as it holds the SIREN; while it is being given, "SIRET en cours d'attribution" (SP F31808).
- **The register**: a trader registered at the RCS writes "RCS" and the city of its court registry (R123-237 2°); a liberal profession (on the RNE only) writes none. An artisan's RNE mention on invoices: wording **UNVERIFIED** (the old "RM" mention ended with the Répertoire des métiers on 1 January 2023; common practice is "Immatriculée au RNE", or nothing beyond the SIREN). A company's form, capital and register are printed as the design asks; the research read R123-237 for sole traders only.
- **The VAT number**: not required under the franchise (ann. II art. 242 nonies A II; BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §55), except for services to a business in another member state (§10).
- **Insurance**: an artisan whose trade requires it prints the insurance, the insurer's contact and the geographic coverage (Code de l'artisanat L132-1, cited by SP F31808; the fields beyond these three **secondary**); the building trades attach their décennale certificate (Code des assurances L243-2, **secondary**).
- Other mentions of particular trades (the legal conformity guarantee on listed consumer goods, the eco-participation, the members of an approved management body: SP F31808) are not modelled: they are the person's own lines.

### 1.3 The client
- Name and address, and the billing address when different (L441-9; 242 nonies A I-1°); a consumer may object to their address being printed (SP F31808).
- A business's **SIREN** (242 nonies A I-1°, new wording), from 1 September 2027 for micro-enterprises (SP F31808; the impots.gouv.fr table of invoice data, August 2026).
- A client liable for VAT: its **VAT number** (I-4°), which may be left out at €150 excluding tax or less, except across borders (SP F31808).
- The **delivery address** of goods when different (I-7° bis), from 1 September 2027 for micro-enterprises.
- The client's **purchase order** number, when there is one (L441-9 I).

### 1.4 The document
- **The issue date** (I-6°) and **a unique number** (I-7°; §2 below).
- **The date of the sale or the service**, or of a deposit, when it differs from the issue date (I-10°; L441-9). A period ("prestations du 1er au 31 octobre") is common practice (**secondary**); the texts say "date".
- **Each line**: quantity, precise description, unit price excluding tax, VAT rate or exemption (I-8°; L441-9); for services, the materials and the labour apart (SP F31808).
- **The nature of the operations**: goods, services or both (I-8° bis; impots.gouv.fr's codes LB, PS, LBPS), from 1 September 2027 for micro-enterprises. A franchise beneficiary with mixed activities already shows the goods and the services apart, on invoices and in the receipts book (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §300).
- **Discounts** acquired and quantifiable at the time of the sale (I-9°; L441-9).
- **Totals**: per rate the total excluding tax and the tax, and the tax due (I-11°); the totals excluding and including tax (SP F31808).
- **The currency** (a data item of electronic invoices; §10) and **the language** (free; §10).

### 1.5 VAT: the franchise, or liable
- **The franchise (art. 293 B CGI)**: no VAT may appear (art. 293 E); the invoice says "**TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI**" (or "article 293 B bis" for the EU franchise, or art. 284 of directive 2006/112/EC) (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §50–53, version of 1 July 2026). VAT shown anyway is owed by the mere fact of invoicing it (art. 283-3 CGI), with no deduction for the client (§53, §60). A franchise beneficiary's invoice may leave out its own VAT number and the exemption reference whatever the amount (242 nonies A II; §55), never the mention (§50).
- **Recodification**: VAT law moves from the CGI into the Code des impositions sur les biens et services (CIBS) on **1 January 2027** (ordonnance 2025-1247 of 17 December 2025, its date moved by ordonnance 2026-671 of 27 July 2026, art. 17); the CGI's references stay usable until **30 June 2028**, and doctrine stays opposable (BOI-RES-TVA-000253, 7 October 2026). Two impots.gouv.fr FAQs, both modified on 14 October 2026, give "TVA non applicable, article L. 2x3-3 du Code des impositions sur les biens et services" for use from 1 January 2027, but disagree on the number: L. 223-3 in one, L. 233-3 in the other (maxcompta.com, **secondary**, cites L. 233-3). The exact article: **UNVERIFIED**. Hence a mention the person can edit, the CGI's wording its default until the article is settled.
- **Liable for VAT** (an EI past the thresholds, or by option, who stays a micro-entrepreneur for URSSAF up to the micro ceilings): per rate, the total excluding tax and the tax; the rate of each line; the total tax; its own VAT number, and the client's between businesses (I-2°, I-4°); the exemption's reference on an exempt line (I-12°); "Autoliquidation" when the client owes the tax (I-13°); "**Option pour le paiement de la taxe d'après les débits**" when a provider of services opted for it (I-11° bis, from 1 September 2027 for micro-enterprises and SMEs). From 1 January 2027 the simplified VAT regime (RSI) ends: the réel normal only (impots.gouv.fr FAQ, 14 October 2026).
- **Another currency, under VAT**: the VAT is also given in euros, at the ECB's rate of the day it becomes due (art. 289 IV; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §380–400).
- The VAT rates themselves (20 %, 10 %, 5.5 %, 2.1 %) were not part of this research: Sioul ships them as they were known in mid-2026, to be checked.

### 1.6 Businesses, public bodies and consumers
- **A business** (a professional, or a legal person): an invoice for every sale or service (L441-9; art. 289 CGI); a franchise beneficiary invoices taxable persons and non-taxable legal persons like any taxable person (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §50). **A public body**: an invoice through Chorus Pro (SP F23208).
- **A consumer**: VAT law asks no invoice (§50, §55), except for distance sales of goods and exempt intra-EU deliveries (SP F23208). For services, a **note** is due from €25 including tax, on request below, and the provider displays that it may be asked (arrêté 83-50/A of 3 October 1983, threshold raised to €25 on 15 July 2010; SP F23208 and F38350; DGCCRF); given once the service is done and before payment, in two copies, with the date, the provider's name and address, the client's name unless they object, the date and place of the service, each service with its quantity and price, and the totals. One invoice document with these fields covers it (*inference*).
- **The payment block** of businesses (§4) is never printed for a consumer.
- **When**: at delivery or completion; a monthly recap per client is allowed, issued by the end of the month; by the 15th of the next month for exempt intra-EU deliveries and business services taxed in another member state (SP F23208).
- **Sanctions**: €15 per missing or wrong mention, capped at a quarter of the invoice's amount (art. 1737 CGI), the first offence in four years forgiven when corrected; failing to invoice, up to €75,000 for an EI (L441-9 II).

## 2. Numbers
- **The rule**: each invoice carries a unique number from a chronological and continuous sequence (242 nonies A I-7°; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §90, in force since 18 October 2013, current).
- **Several series** only "lorsque les conditions d'exercice de l'activité de l'assujetti le justifient", used as first justified; each numbered in the order of issue, continuous, and two invoices of the same year never with one number; a distinct prefix per series is advised (§90). Accepted (§100): several billing sites; several client categories with different rules (taxable persons and private persons); several issuing methods (self-billing, a third party, electronic, paper). Not on its own (§110): one series per client, or per country of destination. A mandatary keeps a sequence per principal (§90).
- **Starting again each year**: not said by the BOFiP; its "same year" wording lets a sequence restart each year when the year is in the number ("2026-0001"), which keeps numbers unique (**secondary**: Legalstart, Abby, Obat, lecoindesentrepreneurs). A monthly restart without the month in the number would break uniqueness.
- **Gaps**: the sequence is continuous, so none; an issued invoice is never deleted but corrected (§3). The continuity is primary; the "no deletion" consequence **secondary** (commercial guides).
- **Drafts**: the law numbers issued invoices; a draft or a quote takes no number from the sequence (*inference* from the rule; secondary guides agree). Hence the number given at issue, in the same step as the record, and a draft named by an id of its own.
- **Credit notes**: an invoice in the law's eyes (art. 289 I-5 CGI), so numbered in a chronological, continuous sequence: the invoices' own, or a series of their own ("A2026-0001"), which secondary guides recommend. Whether doctrine expressly admits a series for credit notes: **UNVERIFIED**; the invoices' sequence is always safe.
- **Order**: an invoice's date is its delivery's, or its issue's when sent electronically (§140); within a series, numbers rise with dates.
- **Elsewhere**: a sequential number "based on one or more series" (Directive 2006/112/EC art. 226(2)); Spain requires a separate series for corrective invoices (Real Decreto 1619/2012 art. 6); a yearly reset is common practice, not a rule in the texts read.

## 3. Corrections and deposits

### 3.1 Credit notes and corrective invoices
- A document or message that changes an invoice and refers to it specifically and unambiguously is an invoice (art. 289 I-5 CGI), with every mandatory mention except the seller's VAT number and the exemption reference, whatever its amount (BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-20 §100, version of 19 January 2022, current).
- **Two forms** (§100–110): a new invoice cancelling and replacing the first, citing it exactly and saying so; or a **credit note** citing the invoice's **number and date**, stating the reduction excluding tax and its VAT, the parties' names and addresses, and the totals due after the reduction. A year-end rebate may cite a set of invoices, or the contract, with their period.
- **As data**: the corrected invoice's number is part of an electronic invoice's data from 1 September 2026, its date from 1 September 2027 (impots.gouv.fr's table; ann. IV art. 41 septies D CGI); EU-wide, the corrected invoice's number becomes a mandatory detail on 1 July 2030 (§12).
- **No other way**: changing or deleting an issued invoice is not provided for (art. 289 I-5; the BOFiP above). Hence issued invoices never change, and "editing" one means a credit note, then a new invoice.
- **Related cases**: losing the franchise in the year, corrective invoices go to taxable clients for the operations from the day of crossing, deposits included (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §170); under VAT, an invoice never paid recovers its VAT with a duplicate bearing the art. 272 mention (BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-20 §320–340).

### 3.2 Deposits
- A deposit paid before the operation is invoiced (art. 289 I-1-c CGI; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-20 §160); a deposit invoice may leave out what is not known yet (the exchange rate, the exact date of the operation, a quantity or a price still variable) (§160–170), and gives the deposit's date when it differs from the issue date (242 nonies A I-10°). A franchise beneficiary does so for its business clients like anyone (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §50).
- The final invoice deducts the deposits invoiced (practice; **secondary**): it lists them by number and date with their amounts, and gives the balance due.

### 3.3 Quotes (later)
- Not mandatory in general; mandatory before repairs and upkeep in building and household equipment, whatever the amount (arrêté of 24 January 2017; INC, a semi-official body; the text itself not re-read), and, per **secondary** sources, for personal services from €100 a month, removals and a few other trades. A quote is no invoice and takes no number from the invoices' sequence.

## 4. Payment terms

### 4.1 Between businesses
C. com. L441-10, version in force since 26 April 2019 (ordonnance 2019-359), read on Légifrance on 16 November 2026.
- **By default**: 30 days after the goods are received or the service performed (L441-10 I).
- **At most**: 60 days from the invoice's date, or 45 days end of month when expressly agreed and not abusive (L441-10 I); periodic invoices, 45 days.
- **Penalties** (L441-10 II), due the day after the due date, without a reminder: by default the ECB's rate of its latest main refinancing operation **plus 10 points**; a contractual rate, never below **three times the legal interest rate**. The ECB's rate is the one in force on 1 January for the first half-year, on 1 July for the second.
  - The ECB's rate: 2.15 % from 11 June 2025, 2.40 % from 17 June 2026, 2.65 % from 16 September 2026 (ecb.europa.eu). So the default penalty rate is **12.15 %** in the first half of 2026 and **12.40 %** in the second; 12.65 % in the first half of 2027 if unchanged.
  - The legal interest rate for professional creditors: **2.75 %** in the second half of 2026 (arrêté of 26 June 2026; SP A15398), 2.62 % in the first (**secondary**); the floor of a contractual rate, **8.25 %** in the second half of 2026.
  - Penalties are computed on the amount including tax, and bear no VAT (**secondary**).
- **The €40 flat indemnity** for recovery costs, owed by a professional client paying late (C. com. D441-5; SP F31808; justice.fr), more on proof of higher costs (L441-10 II).
- **Wording**: L441-9 asks for the due date, the escompte's conditions, the penalty rate and the indemnity; the phrasing is free. "Escompte pour paiement anticipé : néant." is the usual wording (SP F31808). A suggested text: "En cas de retard de paiement, pénalités exigibles dès le lendemain de la date d'échéance, sans rappel, au taux de 12,40 % (taux BCE + 10 points) ; indemnité forfaitaire pour frais de recouvrement : 40 € (art. L441-10 et D441-5 du code de commerce)." The rate used is kept with each invoice, since it changes each half-year.
- Exceeding the legal terms: up to €75,000 for a natural person (L441-16; **secondary**, not re-read).

### 4.2 Consumers
- L441-9 and L441-10 cover purchases for a professional activity: no legal maximum term, no €40 (SP F31808: only a professional client owes it), no penalties by right; late interest at the legal rate (2.75 % in the second half of 2026) from a formal notice (Code civil 1231-6; **secondary**). Hence a due date and the means of payment only.

## 5. Keeping invoices
- **10 years** (C. com. L123-22, cited by SP F23208), **6 years** for tax (LPF L102 B; BOI-CF-COM-10-10-30): keep them 10.
- **In the format issued**: an electronic invoice is kept as it was issued and sent (art. 289 V CGI); a converted copy may be kept beside it (BOI-CF-COM-10-10-30 §150–160). Invoices made by software and sent on paper: two copies printed, or the PDF or PDF/A-3 file secured so that it reproduces identically (same BOI).
- **Authenticity, integrity, legibility**, from issue to the end of retention (art. 289 V), by a reliable audit trail (VII-1°), a qualified electronic signature or seal, or EDI (BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-30-20 §1–40). A PDF its issuer mails is a valid invoice backed by an audit trail (§30); for a very small business, comparing by hand the quote, order, delivery, invoice, credit notes and payments, traceable both ways, can suffice (§70).
- This stays the rule for consumers and clients abroad; for domestic invoices between businesses, the PDF stops being an invoice once the electronic obligation applies (§9).
- Hence issued invoices that never change, the exact bytes sent kept with a hash (*inference*: no text asks for a hash), and a trail from the invoice to its payments and credit notes.

## 6. The books of a micro-entrepreneur
- **The texts**: C. com. L123-28 (a book recording chronologically the amount and the origin of receipts; a purchases register summarised by year when the main business sells goods, food or lodging; exact wording **secondary**); CGI art. 50-0 (micro-BIC: a livre-journal day by day, backed by invoices; BOI-BIC-DECLA-30-30 §10–40); art. 102 ter (micro-BNC: the daily detail of receipts with the identity the client declared, the amount, the date and the form of payment; BOI-BNC-DECLA-20-30 §1–10); art. 286 II-1 (the franchise: the same, with quarterly and yearly totals, goods and services apart for mixed activities; BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §290–300). Official summary: SP F36018 (checked 21 February 2026).
- **The receipts book**, entry by entry **by the date of collection**, not the invoice's (BOI-BIC-DECLA-30-30 §20): the amount; the client and the nature of the sale or service; the means of payment, cash told apart from the others; the invoice's reference; goods and services in columns for a mixed activity; totals at the end of each quarter and each year. Consumer receipts of €76 or less may be recorded as one total per day (art. 286 I-3°; micro-BNC: cash paid on the spot only).
- **The purchases register**: for goods, food and lodging only (§40); pure services are excluded.
- **Form**: kept by software when the register is identified and dated with means "offrant toute garantie en matière de preuve" (SP F36018; C. com. R123-172 and after); 10 years. Hence the book made from the payments, each tied to its invoice (*inference*).
- **What Sioul does** (phase 4, [projects.md](../projects.md#the-books)): the receipts book made from the payments written on the invoices, a row per payment on its day of collection, with the invoice's client, the nature, the invoice, the means (cash apart), the amount and, under VAT, the amount excluding it; goods and services in columns for a mixed activity; each quarter's and each year's totals; as CSV and as a page printed to PDF; the receipts no invoice describes from the work budgets that declare, as before. Consumer receipts of €76 or less are not grouped per day (each is a row of its own), and the purchases register waits.

## 7. URSSAF declarations and contributions, 2026
- **What is declared**: the turnover **actually collected** in the period, excluding tax ("Seules les factures encaissées doivent être déclarées"), monthly or quarterly, **zero periods too**, online only (autoentrepreneur.urssaf.fr, "L'essentiel du statut", read 16 November 2026, rates from 1 January 2026).
- **When**: monthly, by the last day of the next month; quarterly, by 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January; moved to the next working day after a weekend or a holiday. The first declaration gathers the start's period and the ones after it: monthly, the start month and the three after, due the last day of the fourth month after the start month (a start in January, 31 May; in March, 31 July); quarterly, the start quarter and the next (a start in the first quarter, 31 July; on 12 April 2026, 31 October 2026) (URSSAF's tables; justice.fr, "Micro-entrepreneur : déclarer son chiffre d'affaires", a start on 6 March 2026 first declared by 31 July 2026; propulsebyca.fr's monthly table; the review of phase 4, P4-1, corrected the earlier reading "at least 90 days after the start").
- **Categories**, one amount each: sales of goods (BIC); commercial or craft services (BIC); other services (BNC); regulated liberal professions (CIPAV); classified tourist lettings.
- **Rates from 1 January 2026**, metropolitan France:

| Category | Contributions | Versement libératoire |
|---|---|---|
| Sales of goods (BIC) | 12.3 % | 1 % |
| Commercial or craft services (BIC) | 21.2 % | 1.7 % |
| Other services (BNC) | 25.6 % | 2.2 % |
| Liberal professions, CIPAV | 23.2 % | 2.2 % |

  Secondary sources announced 26.1 % for BNC in 2026; URSSAF says 25.6 %. ACRE lowers the contributions for a first year (by a quarter for a start from 1 July 2026, by half before).
- **The CFP** (vocational training), on turnover: 0.1 % for traders, 0.3 % for artisans, 0.2 % for liberal professions (URSSAF). **The chambers' tax**, by activity, is not due at €5,000 of turnover or less (justice.fr; **secondary**-level confidence, not re-read in the CGI).
- **The versement libératoire**: for a household whose reference tax income of year N-2 is under a ceiling per part (€29,315 for 2026); chosen at registration or within three months, then each year by 30 September for the next.
- **The yearly return**: the 2042-C-PRO, with flat allowances of 71 %, 50 % and 34 %; its boxes (5KO, 5KP, 5HQ; 5TA, 5TB, 5TE with the versement libératoire) **secondary**, to be checked on each year's form.
- **What Sioul does** (phase 4): a declaration per period (each quarter, or each month as set) and per activity, the four above (classified tourist lettings left out), zero periods said, none before the books begin (the start of activity, else the first thing Sioul knows of); its day the last day of the next month, moved off a weekend (holidays not counted, so a day said may come a day or two before URSSAF's, never after); the first declaration gathering the start's period and the next three months, or the next quarter; each invoice line in its activity, apart from its nature (a meal or a night's lodging a sale, though a service for VAT); nothing below zero declared, what was given back beyond what came in said, since the form takes no amount below zero and no source says how a refund carries over; the contributions estimated at the rates of each payment's day, the versement libératoire when chosen, the CFP by trade; ACRE as a rate of the person's own; the chambers' tax and the yearly return's boxes left out. The rates are the French preset's dated data, each overridable.

## 8. Thresholds in 2026

### 8.1 The micro regime (art. 50-0 and 102 ter CGI)
- **€203,100** for sales of goods, food and lodging; **€83,600** for services, BIC and BNC, CIPAV included; a mixed activity, both (SP F32353, checked 21 February 2026; URSSAF).
- Counted on turnover **collected** in the calendar year, excluding tax; the regime ends only after **two consecutive years** above, on 1 January of the next; the first year prorated to its days of activity (a start on 1 March 2026, services: 83,600 × 306 / 365 = €70,087).
- **What Sioul does** (phase 4): the ceilings watched under the micro regime only, on the receipts book's turnover; prorated in the first year, to the euro; 80 % and the crossing said once each, and the regime's end once the year before was above too, said again on the first start of the year it takes effect, to do until the regime changes. The ceilings of 2022 to 2025 are in the preset (service-public, A16230, 5 January 2023: €188,700 and €77,700 from 2023, €176,200 and €72,600 in 2022), so a second year above is seen in 2026 too.

### 8.2 The VAT franchise (art. 293 B CGI), unchanged in 2026

| Activity | Base (year N-1) | Higher (year N) |
|---|---|---|
| Goods, food on site, lodging | €85,000 | €93,500 |
| Other services, liberal professions | €37,500 | €41,250 |
| Lawyers, regulated activities | €50,000 | €55,000 |
| Lawyers, other activities | €35,000 | €38,500 |
| Authors, their works and rights | €50,000 | €55,000 |
| Authors, other activities | €35,000 | €38,500 |

SP F21746 (checked 1 January 2026: "les seuils … restent inchangés"); BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 §130–140 (1 July 2026). Software and architecture are not an author's work for these thresholds (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-30 §130): a software author uses the services' thresholds.
- **Counted**: turnover excluding VAT of operations **located in France** whose VAT became due: goods at delivery, services at payment; a service to a business in another member state, taxed there, does not count (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 §180–210). A mixed activity: both conditions (§160); several businesses of one person are added up (§300).
- **The higher threshold crossed in year N**: VAT on operations **from the day it is crossed** (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §160; SP F21746), and corrective invoices to taxable clients from that day (§170); payments received later for services performed before stay untaxed (§160). URSSAF's page still says "the first day of the month of crossing", an older wording: the BOFiP is followed.
- **The base threshold crossed, not the higher one**: the franchise holds for year N, VAT from **1 January N+1** (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 §130). The old tolerance of two years no longer exists for VAT; it still does for the micro regime (§8.1).
- **Below again**: the franchise from the next 1 January (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-20 §190). An option for VAT at any time, for two years at least (art. 293 F; §230–240).
- **The first year**: the thresholds prorated to the days of activity, the higher ones in the year of creation (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 §285–295; SP F21746 annualises the turnover, which is equivalent). **Conflict**: the impots.gouv.fr FAQ of 14 October 2026 applies the higher thresholds unprorated in the year of creation; the BOFiP is opposable and stricter, and is followed.
- **What Sioul does** (phase 4): the thresholds watched while the franchise holds, counted as above (goods on their invoice's day, services on each payment's, the reverse charge and what is outside the scope left out, refunds taken off), the goods' thresholds on the whole turnover of a mixed activity; prorated in the first year as the BOFiP says, its figures reproduced (a start on 12 June: €52,001 and €22,942); 80 % of the base threshold and its crossing said once each, to know, and on the first start of the next year "From today, VAT applies", to do; the higher one's crossing to do, with the corrective invoices to make, alone when one payment crosses both; one to do stays until the tax scheme says VAT from its day, told again at each start until then. A bank line tied to an invoice counts even with no payment written; a deposit counts once. Lawyers' and authors' own thresholds are not in the preset: a threshold of the person's own stands for them. Other countries' thresholds are not watched until researched (Germany's § 19 UStG among them).

### 8.3 The €25,000 threshold that was not
- The loi de finances 2025 made a single franchise threshold of €25,000 (higher €27,500); it was suspended, then repealed by loi 2025-1044 of 3 November 2025, art. 1, which restored the thresholds above for good (the BOFiP's news of 1 July 2026; SP F21746). Its article in the loi de finances 2025 (art. 30): **secondary**. Whether a narrower threshold survived in the loi de finances 2026: **UNVERIFIED**; the official pages of 2026 show none.

## 9. Electronic invoices in France
- **The calendar**: since **1 September 2026**, every business subject to VAT, under the franchise too, must be able to **receive** electronic invoices through an approved platform (plateforme agréée, PA); large and mid-sized companies issue them and report. From **1 September 2027**, SMEs and **micro-enterprises issue** them and send their reporting data (impots.gouv.fr FAQs; the FAQ "Je découvre la facturation électronique", version of 1 September 2026, §8.1). Before then, issuing them is voluntary, and a client must accept a compliant one (§5.2–5.3).
- **The franchise is in scope**: "La réforme s'applique à tous les assujettis à la TVA, qu'ils soient redevables ou non (par exemple le franchisé en base)" (impots.gouv.fr FAQ, modified 28 September 2026).
- **Which flow**: a taxable business established in France, an electronic invoice through a PA; a private person, or a business abroad, the transaction's data reported (e-reporting), the invoice itself sent by any channel, PDF or paper (impots.gouv.fr FAQs; FAQ §9.1; SP F23208). A franchise beneficiary reports every two calendar months, by the 25th to 30th of the next month (impots.gouv.fr's table of frequencies, August 2026); whether payment data are expected from it ("éventuellement"): **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Formats**: structured data; "un PDF simple envoyé par voie électronique n'est pas une facture électronique au sens du dispositif" (FAQ §3.1). The base: UBL, UN/CEFACT CII and Factur-X (a PDF/A-3 carrying its CII XML), set by AFNOR XP Z12-012 (formats and profiles), XP Z12-013 (the API between software and a PA), XP Z12-014 (business cases); external specifications v3.2 of 30 April 2026 (impots.gouv.fr). The article that lists them: **UNVERIFIED**. The semantic model is EN 16931 (§14).
- **Data**: from 2026, both SIRENs and countries, the operation's category (LB, PS, LBPS), the issue date, the number, totals and tax per rate, the currency, and when relevant the VAT numbers, the corrected invoice's number, the option for debits, and the exemption's reason, which "vise aussi le régime de la franchise en base"; from **1 September 2027**, each line's description, quantity and unit price, discounts, the delivery address, the corrected invoice's date, the escompte (ann. IV art. 41 septies D CGI; impots.gouv.fr's table, August 2026).
- **How to send**: through a PA ("par l'intermédiaire obligatoire d'une plateforme agréée", FAQ §3.1). Software connected to at least one PA by its API (AFNOR XP Z12-013) is a "solution compatible" (§2.3); software that only exports files is not, but its user stays compliant uploading the file to their own PA, where the PA offers it (§9.1; that many PAs take Factur-X uploads: **secondary**).
- **Sanctions** (SP F23208, citing art. 1737 and 1788 D CGI): €50 per invoice not issued electronically when it should be, capped at €15,000 a year; €500 per missing transmission of reporting data, same cap; the first offence in four years forgiven when corrected within 30 days.

## 10. Abroad, currency and language, from France
- **A service to a business in another member state**: taxed there (art. 259-1° CGI, **secondary** as a citation; art. 44 and 196 of directive 2006/112/EC), so not located in France: outside the French franchise and its thresholds (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10 §180). The invoice says "**Autoliquidation**" (242 nonies A I-13°; citing the article is not required: BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §520–540), with **both VAT numbers**, even under the franchise (242 nonies A II c; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-20 §50–60). Never the 293 B mention as its reason: the franchise does not govern an operation located abroad (*inference* from the BOFiP; some guides print it). A micro-entrepreneur asks its tax office for an intra-EU VAT number for such services, whatever their amount, keeping the franchise for French operations (impots.gouv.fr FAQ, 14 October 2026; art. 286 ter CGI). The invoice by the 15th of the next month (SP F23208); a European declaration of services (DES) each month by the 10th working day, on paper (cerfa 13694) allowed under the franchise (douane.gouv.fr, 7 February 2025).
- **A service to a consumer in the EU**: French rules, the franchise applies (art. 259-2°; **secondary**), except electronic services past €10,000 a year across the EU, taxed in the consumer's state (art. 259 D; **secondary**).
- **Outside the EU**: a business client's service is outside French VAT; "Autoliquidation" is only for operations located in another member state (BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §540); "TVA non applicable – article 259-1 du CGI" is practice (**secondary**).
- **Goods**: under the franchise, a delivery to an EU business stays under the franchise, with no exempt intra-EU delivery (BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-50 §1–20; art. 262 ter I CGI); liable, the exempt delivery cites its exemption and both VAT numbers.
- **Currency**: any, the tax due being set in euros (art. 289 IV; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §380); under the franchise there is no VAT, so no question, but the books and the declarations count euros (the payment day's rate: practice, **secondary**).
- **Language**: free; a sworn translation may be asked on request only (BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-20-20-10 §420–430). That the loi Toubon asks French on documents given to French consumers: **secondary / UNVERIFIED**.
- **What Sioul does** (phase 4): the DES of each month's reverse-charged services (category `AE`), by each invoice's service day, since the sources do not give apart the month a service's VAT becomes due in the client's state; said by the 10th working day of the next month (weekends counted out, holidays not), with each invoice's client and VAT number; a deposit and its final invoice once; a credit note in the month it is issued, below zero, as the online service enters a decrease, and said when it credits another month's invoice (the manual of the online service, DGDDI, September 2019, gives the minus sign, rounding to the euro and "the calendar month in which VAT became due", not a credit note's month); an invoice in another currency listed, left out of the total, and said; a payment in another currency counts in the books at what reached the person in euros, as said, never at a rate of Sioul's own; the VAT summary names an invoice in another currency and does not count it.

## 11. Software that records consumers' payments
- A taxable person who sells to private customers and records their payments in software must use software meeting conditions of inalterability, security, conservation and archiving, proven by a certificate from an accredited body or an attestation from its editor (art. 286 I 3° bis CGI; BOI-TVA-DECLA-30-10-30, version of 25 March 2026). Invoicing software that follows consumers' payments counts, its cash function only (§30–40).
- **Not under the franchise** (art. 286 II-2 CGI; §25): a micro-entrepreneur under the franchise needs no certificate and no attestation, even when its software records consumers' payments.
- The editor's attestation, abolished from 16 February 2025 (loi 2025-127, art. 43), is back since 21 February 2026 (loi 2026-103, art. 125; BOI §200–205). For **free software**, the user is treated as its editor and obtains the attestation for the version used (BOI, "Exemple 4 : Logiciel libre"). The fine (art. 1770 duodecies CGI), €7,500 per software: **UNVERIFIED**.
- Hence what the French preset says: liable for VAT, with consumers' payments recorded, Sioul has no certificate and no attestation.

## 12. The European frame
- **When an invoice is due** (Directive 2006/112/EC art. 220–222): supplies to taxable persons and to non-taxable legal persons, distance sales, intra-EU supplies of goods, advance payments before them; Member States may ask more (France does, for consumers' services). Cross-border reverse-charged services: by the 15th of the next month, 10 days from 1 July 2030.
- **What it says** (art. 226, as amended by Directive 2010/45/EU): the issue date; a sequential number "based on one or more series"; the supplier's VAT number; the customer's when it owes the VAT or for intra-EU goods; names and addresses; the quantity and nature of the goods or services; the date of supply or of the advance when it differs; per rate or exemption the taxable amount, the unit price and the discounts; the rate; the VAT; for an exemption, a reference to its provision; the words "Reverse charge" when the customer owes the VAT; the margin schemes' words. Simplified invoices: up to €100, for corrective documents, and for small enterprises under art. 284 (art. 220a, 226b).
- **Currency** (art. 230): any, the VAT due given in the national currency. **Language** (art. 248a): no general requirement of a translation may be imposed.
- **Small enterprises** (Directive 2020/285, from 1 January 2025): a domestic exemption up to €85,000 (art. 284(1)); in other member states too, for a Union turnover up to €100,000, with an individual "EX" number (art. 284(2)–(3); the Commission's Explanatory Notes, 2024); what happens on crossing a threshold depends on each state's choice (art. 288a).
- **ViDA** (Directive (EU) 2025/516, in force April 2025): member states may impose domestic electronic invoices without asking for a derogation, the ground of the French, Belgian and German mandates; from **1 July 2030** an electronic invoice is structured data conforming to EN 16931, and two details become mandatory: a corrected invoice's number, and the account to pay into (art. 226(16), (17)).

## 13. Other countries: what the presets rest on
- **Germany**: § 14(4) UStG (the parties' names and addresses; the Steuernummer or the USt-IdNr.; the issue date; a unique number, in one or more series; the goods or services; the date of supply; the consideration by rate and exemption; the rate and the tax, or a note that an exemption applies). The **Kleinunternehmer** (§ 19 UStG, reformed on 1 January 2025): turnover up to €25,000 the year before and €100,000 the current year, the supplies now exempt; invoices by § 34a UStDV: names and addresses, the Steuernummer, the USt-IdNr. or the Kleinunternehmer-Identifikationsnummer (the German EX number), the issue date, the goods or services, the consideration as one sum, and a note that the small-business exemption applies; no number listed. Its wording is free; "Gemäß § 19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer berechnet" is common practice (**UNVERIFIED** as an official wording). A Kleinunternehmer may always send a plain invoice. Electronic invoices: received by all since 1 January 2025; paper or PDF still allowed for 2025 and 2026, in 2027 only up to €800,000 of turnover, for nobody from 2028 (§ 27(38) UStG; BMF letters of 15 October 2024, 2025 and 2026); ZUGFeRD from 2.0.1 and XRechnung admitted, their MINIMUM and BASIC-WL profiles not (the 2024 letter, as reported by EY and Baker Tilly; the letter's text not read). The late-payment wording (§ 288 BGB) was not researched.
- **Belgium**: content as art. 226 (VAT Code art. 53 §2, Royal Decree no. 1 art. 5: **UNVERIFIED** in detail). The franchise (VAT Code art. 56bis) up to €25,000 (**secondary**; **UNVERIFIED** at the FPS Finance); its mention: two wordings in use ("Régime particulier de franchise des petites entreprises", and "Petite entreprise soumise au régime de la franchise de taxe. TVA non applicable."), which one the Royal Decree prescribes **UNVERIFIED**; a franchise enterprise keeps a Belgian VAT number. From **1 January 2026**, invoices between Belgian businesses liable to VAT are structured electronic invoices sent through **Peppol**, Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 at least; a PDF by mail no longer suffices; consumers are left out; even a business selling only to consumers must be able to receive them (einvoice.belgium.be; a tolerance in the first quarter of 2026). Whether franchise enterprises must issue them: **UNVERIFIED**. Peppol is reached through a certified access point, which a desktop program cannot be.
- **Spain**: content by Real Decreto 1619/2012 art. 6 (correlative numbers in each series, a separate series for corrective invoices; the NIF; "inversión del sujeto pasivo"); professionals invoicing Spanish businesses deduct an IRPF withholding of 15 % (7 % at the start). **VERI\*FACTU** (Real Decreto 1007/2023; Orden HAC/1177/2024): any invoicing software a Spanish taxpayer uses keeps a hash-chained record of each invoice and prints a QR code (30 to 40 mm) and the words "VERI\*FACTU", and its producer signs a declaration kept readable inside the program; companies from 1 January 2027, sole traders from 1 July 2027 (Real Decreto-ley 15/2025; **secondary**). A template cannot meet this.
- **Italy**: invoices between Italian parties, businesses and consumers alike, go through the **Sistema di Interscambio (SdI)** in FatturaPA XML (fatturapa.gov.it, schema 1.2.3); the regime forfettario too since 1 January 2024 (DL 36/2022 art. 18; **secondary**). A PDF is no domestic invoice there.
- **Switzerland**: liable for VAT from CHF 100,000 of worldwide turnover (estv.admin.ch); an invoice gives the supplier's name, address and VAT number (the UID followed by MWST, TVA or IVA; its format **UNVERIFIED**), the recipient's name and address (not on receipts up to CHF 400), the date or period, the description, the price, the rate and the VAT, 8.1 % standard (kmu.admin.ch). Not registered: no VAT and no mention; whoever states a tax on an invoice owes it (art. 27 MWSTG, **UNVERIFIED**, not read). No numbering rule found (**UNVERIFIED**). The QR-bill is the national payment slip (later).
- **The United Kingdom**: any business invoice (gov.uk, "Invoicing and taking payment from customers"): a unique number; your name, address and contact details; the customer's name and address; a description; the supply and invoice dates; the amounts; VAT if any; the total owed; a sole trader using a business name adds their own name. Registration for VAT above £90,000 of taxable turnover (gov.uk). A full VAT invoice (HMRC VAT Notice 700 §16.3.1) gives the total VAT in sterling. Not registered: no mention, no VAT line. VAT invoices become electronic from 1 April 2029 (gov.uk, 28 October 2026).
- **The United States and Canada**: no federal rule on an invoice's content was found (**UNVERIFIED** as a negative); Canada's content follows what the buyer needs for its input tax credits, by bands of $100 and $500 (canada.ca); sales taxes are a state's or a province's. Elsewhere serves them: identifiers and lines of the person's own.
- **The patterns**: identifiers vary in number and name, so a list of (kind, value) is the only general shape. "No VAT" takes four shapes: a franchise with a mention (France, Belgium, Germany, Italy), not registered with none (the UK, Switzerland, Canada, the US), an exemption with a reference, the reverse charge with its words. Withholding taxes on the invoice (Spain, Italy) are a third kind of line, not modelled. Mandates that need certified software or the state's channel (Spain, Italy, from 2026 to 2028 France, Belgium and Germany) cannot be met by a PDF.

## 14. EN 16931: the vocabulary of the record
- **What it is**: the European semantic model of an invoice, each element a business term (BT-n) or group (BG-n), with business rules that check them (BR-…); two syntaxes, UN/CEFACT CII (Factur-X, ZUGFeRD) and UBL 2.1 (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0, "a Core Invoice Usage Specification" of it). Factur-X 1.09.2, the same as ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 (published 4 August 2026, in force 1 September 2026; fnfe-mpe.org), in its EN 16931 profile (`urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017`), serves France and Germany alike; the MINIMUM and BASIC WL profiles are no invoices in Germany and stop counting in France (Factur-X 1.07.2, read in full; the BMF's letter).
- **The terms the record uses**: BT-1 the number; BT-2 the issue date; BT-3 the kind (UNTDID 1001: 380 an invoice, 381 a credit note, 384 a corrective invoice, 386 a prepayment invoice, 389 a self-billed invoice); BT-5 the currency (ISO 4217); BT-9 the due date and BT-20 the terms; BT-13 the purchase order; BG-3 the preceding invoice a credit note cites; BG-4 and BG-7 the seller and the buyer, with their identifiers (BT-30 a legal registration, such as a SIREN under scheme 0002; BT-31 the VAT number; BT-32 another tax registration; BT-47 and BT-48 the buyer's); BG-5, BG-8 and BG-15 the addresses, structured, with the ISO 3166-1 country; BG-14 the invoicing period. Lines (BG-25): BT-153 the name and BT-154 its details; BT-129 the quantity and BT-130 its unit, UN/ECE Recommendation 20's code (`HUR` hour, `MIN` minute, `DAY` day, `C62` one, `MON` month, `KMT` kilometre, `LS` lump sum); BT-146 the net price, for BT-149 units; BT-136 a line's allowance; BT-151 and BT-152 the VAT category and rate; BT-131 the net amount. Totals (BG-22): BT-106 the lines' sum; BT-109 without VAT; BT-110 the VAT; BT-112 with VAT; BT-113 paid in advance; BT-115 the amount due. The VAT breakdown (BG-23), per category and rate: BT-116 the base, BT-117 the VAT, BT-118 the category, BT-119 the rate, BT-120 and BT-121 the exemption's reason and code. Notes (BG-1), each with its subject code (UNTDID 4451: `AAB`, `PMD`, `PMT`, `REG`…).
- **Rounding** (Factur-X 1.07.2 §7.1.8): money to two decimals, **half away from zero** (13.455 → 13.46, −13.455 → −13.46); the totals satisfy BR-CO-10 to BR-CO-16 exactly (the lines' sum, the bases and the VAT of the breakdown, without and with VAT, the amount due). Money in decimals or integer cents, never floating point.
- **VAT categories** (UNTDID 5305, BT-151 and BT-118): `S` standard rate, `Z` zero-rated, `E` exempt (a reason's text or code required, BR-E-10), `AE` reverse charge, `K` intra-EU supply of goods, `G` export outside the EU, `O` outside the scope of VAT (then no other category on the invoice, BR-O-11 to 14).
- **Exemption codes** (the VATEX list, EN 16931's code lists v15, used from 15 May 2025): the French franchise is **`E`** with **`VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE`** ("France domestic VAT franchise in base"), its mention as BT-120, a rate and a VAT of 0 (BR-E-05, BR-E-09); the reverse charge **`AE`** with **`VATEX-EU-AE`**, the seller's VAT identifier and the buyer's VAT number or legal registration required (BR-AE-02 to 04), and `VATEX-FR-AE` for a French domestic reverse charge; `K` with `VATEX-EU-IC`, `G` with `VATEX-EU-G`, `O` with `VATEX-EU-O`. No code exists for the German Kleinunternehmer (its text alone satisfies BR-E-10) nor for the EU's cross-border small-enterprise scheme. Not `O` for the franchise: its seller stays a taxable person, and `O` forbids the buyer's VAT number (BR-O-02 to 04). A secondary source quoting XP Z12-012 gives the same pair, `E` with `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE`, as French rule BR-FR-CO-16 (**UNVERIFIED** in the norm).
- **An open point for the issued file**: category `E` asks for the seller's VAT number, another tax registration (BT-32) or a tax representative (BR-E-02 to 04), and most French micro-entrepreneurs have no VAT number. What XP Z12-012 puts there (perhaps the SIREN as BT-32) must be read by a person: AFNOR's copy of the norm forbids text and data mining, so it was not read.

## 15. What Sioul takes from it
- **One record in EN 16931's terms** (§14): kinds 380, 381 and 386; lines with UN/ECE units, a price per base quantity (an hour's rate over 60 minutes), a category and a rate; totals in integer cents, rounded half away from zero; the status read from payments and credit notes, never typed. Built in the invoicing's phase 1.
- **A number given at issue, never twice, never changed after** (§2, §3, §5): drafts without a number; issuing all or nothing; a journal of each series' last number beside the records, carried by each device's claim; a credit note citing the invoice's number and date, in the same series by default; a final invoice citing the deposits it deducts. Built in phase 1, deposit and final invoices in the core only until the editor of phase 3 makes them; the issued file kept with its hash in phase 2.
- **What a French invoice says depends on the client, the legal form and the VAT scheme** (§1, §4): three settings apart, the scheme dated; « EI » printed by Sioul; the business lines (escompte, penalties at the rate kept with the invoice, €40) for business clients only, a public body's late payments following rules not read here; the franchise's mention editable, the CGI's wording by default; the client's SIREN said as missing before September 2027, required after. Built in phase 1, as the French preset.
- **Countries as data** (§12, §13): presets for France, Germany, Belgium, the UK, Switzerland and Elsewhere; Spain and Italy named, not supported, with why; what was left unverified (Belgium's franchise mention, an artisan's RNE mention, Germany's late-payment wording, the CIBS article) left to the person, never printed as law. Built in phase 1.
- **The books follow the money received** (§6, §7, §8, §10): payments written on invoices, from the bank's matching or by hand, in phase 1. Built in phase 4 ([projects.md](../projects.md#the-books)): the receipts book from them, read in one place, a refund on a credit note negative on its own day; the declarations per period and activity, each with its day moved off a weekend, a zero one too, the first gathered as URSSAF's tables and justice.fr give it, and the contributions estimated; every euro of a bank line once; for an issuer liable to VAT, the VAT summary per period and rate, which files nothing; the franchise's thresholds and the micro ceilings, counted as the texts say, prorated in the first year, each step said once, one to do until the setting follows, what takes effect on 1 January said then; the DES each month; a payment in another currency counted at what reached the person in euros, as said. All as the French preset's dated data, a rate or a threshold of the person's own over any of them; holidays, ACRE, the chambers' tax, the VAT return's day, a service's DES month and a credit note's left as the sources leave them, and said (finding 80).
- **What Sioul does not file**: the declarations, the DES and the VAT return stay the person's, on URSSAF's, the customs' and the tax office's own sites; the books prepare them.
- **Electronic invoices** (§9, §14): Factur-X in its EN 16931 profile inside each issued PDF/A-3 in phase 2; sending through the person's PA before September 2027, later; Peppol for Belgium, not reached.
- **What Sioul cannot do**, said in the guide: Spain and Italy; Peppol; the certificate for consumers' payments under VAT (§11).
