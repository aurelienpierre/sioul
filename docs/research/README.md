## How to read them
- Each note opens with its question and how its evidence is graded: from meta-analyses and trials down to user reports and vendors' own claims for the behavioural notes; primary sources (documentation, licence texts, source code) for the technical ones.
- Every finding carries its citation, with its DOI or URL beside it or under its section, as the search found it. "(unverified)" marks what could not be confirmed at a primary source.
- "Built", "next", "not built" and "refused, because…" say where Sioul stood on 4 October 2026; the feature notes (for example [tasks.md](../tasks.md) or [health.md](../health.md)) say how it works now.
- The searches ran on 3 October 2026. 32 of the behavioural notes' citations were checked against Crossref, arXiv and PubMed on 4 October 2026: each resolved, with matching authors, year and venue.
