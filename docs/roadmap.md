# Roadmap

Slices, each useful on its own. Built for one user first.

1. **The Porch** ([porch.md](porch.md)). *Working*: accounts found from their address, passwords in the keyring, IMAP sync with IDLE, code notifications, the Porch sorted and summarised in English and French, "Done for now"; from the command line and in a first Qt window. Senders checked by Sioul itself (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, ARC, reverse DNS), borrowed names set aside, blocking, account priority, HTML mail made safe, attachments opened after the antivirus. *Next*: the background service on its own, one card at a time, portals inside Sioul.
2. **The Outbox**: Markdown drafts become mail with attachments; "waits for" links; sending by a deliberate key.
3. **Next** ([tasks.md](tasks.md)). *Working*: CalDAV tasks with RFC 9253 links, edited line by line; the solver (topological order, loops said, the one next step with its reason, days with room, gaps after what was done); Now, list, board, timeline; capture in one line; the focus window and time spent; the day's weather; joy and someday; notes as a vault; links both ways between mail, tasks, events, contacts, notes, drafts and budget lines; import for agents; reminders before dates, once each, with the window closed too ([reminders.md](reminders.md)); what a day holds ([capacity.md](capacity.md)): four costs and a gain, said before and after, budgets learned from your days, estimates corrected by your record, the day's free time, heavy then light, the days around a heavy appointment lighter, even days, two silent slots of time for you. *Next*: splitting by AI, company, cues from events, admin windows as capacity. *Open, waiting for research*: your own good hours for the hardest and most anxious steps (criterion 31 of [capacity-budget.md](research/capacity-budget.md)). Who declares them and how they move with an illness and its treatment, the time of day against fatigue rising through it, and hard first against a small easy start for ADHD are unsettled; working hours are not taken for good hours meanwhile.
4. **Portals** ([sites.md](sites.md)). *Working*: secure mailboxes, chats and other sites pinned in one persistent profile, logged in once; notifications accepted and kept for the Porch (or at once in real time); calls with microphone, camera and a chosen screen; sounds silenced per site; a link from the mail that announces a site; logins from Bitwarden, read by Sioul itself (no other program). *Next*: "file this page" into a case.
5. **Calendar**: invitations by mail, `.ics` previews, deadlines found in letters.
6. **Contacts**: CardDAV. *Working*: the map (OpenStreetMap; addresses placed once each by Nominatim, when you allow it, kept locally), a pin in each card, the map of everyone, pins opening cards. *Next*: deduplication.
7. **Virtual Secretary, mode B** ([virtual-secretary.md](virtual-secretary.md)). Mode A comes with slice 1's sync.
8. **Trust, the rest**: Spamhaus, lookalike domains, the registry of genuine senders. Your own spam filter is built ([spam-filter.md](spam-filter.md)).
9. **Accounting** ([accounting.md](accounting.md), [projects.md](projects.md)). *Working*: budgets, presets, reserves and their cover rules; the verdict at the period's pace, with a realistic end and, from three past periods, a range; a budget's ledger and its balance by day, week, month or year; movements added by hand, once or recurring; amounts read from mail and added as lines; projects, time and invoices; the papers wallet, with renewals reminded and planned ([papers.md](papers.md)). *Next*: bank statements, currency conversion, the French reports.
10. **AI**: companion, triage, drafts, MCP server ([ai.md](ai.md)). The command line and the Markdown drafts serve agents from slice 1 on.
11. **Elsewhere**. *Working*: Google calendars, contacts and tasks, signed in with your own key, what Google does not keep greyed ([google.md](google.md)); GitHub's issues and pull requests as tasks, off unless asked ([github.md](github.md)). Several devices sharing what only Sioul keeps, through a folder your sync carries, sealed with a passphrase ([database.md](database.md)). *Next*: a database server as an option; phones.
12. **Android**, then Windows. *Working* (0.0.2, an APK on the releases page): the window on a phone, the doses reminded, the sharing, do-not-disturb with your other devices. New, not tried on a phone yet: calls and other apps' notifications screened ([android.md](android.md)).

More languages come with each slice ([i18n.md](i18n.md)).
