# Roadmap

Slices, each useful on its own. Built for one user first.

1. **The Porch** ([porch.md](porch.md)). *Working*: accounts found from their address, passwords in the keyring, IMAP sync with IDLE, code notifications, the Porch sorted and summarised in English and French, "Done for now"; from the command line and in a first Qt window. Senders checked by Sioul itself (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, ARC, reverse DNS), borrowed names set aside, blocking, account priority, HTML mail made safe, attachments opened after the antivirus; one mailbox for every address, topics for mail, OpenPGP with your key on a security key, Microsoft's mail ([client.md](client.md), [topics.md](topics.md), [microsoft.md](microsoft.md)). On a desktop with a tray, Sioul goes on behind a closed window. *Next*: a service that watches once Sioul has quit, one card at a time, portals inside Sioul.
2. **The Outbox**: Markdown drafts become mail with attachments; "waits for" links; sending by a deliberate key.
3. **Next** ([tasks.md](tasks.md)). *Working*: CalDAV tasks with RFC 9253 links, edited line by line; the solver (topological order, loops said, the one next step with its reason, days with room, gaps after what was done); Now, list, board, calendar; capture in one line; the focus window and time spent; the day's weather; joy and someday; notes as a vault; links both ways between mail, tasks, events, contacts, notes, drafts and budget lines; import for agents; reminders before dates, once each, with the window closed too ([reminders.md](reminders.md)); tasks with their steps in order and their checklists, a date asked with its hour, a task before an appointment or one time of a repeating event, with the rides and walks around it and the reminder to leave; one search for tasks and events, and several changed together; what a day holds ([capacity.md](capacity.md)): four costs and a gain, said before and after, budgets learned from your days, estimates corrected by your record, the day's free time, heavy then light, the days around a heavy appointment lighter, even days, two silent slots of time for you. *Next*: splitting by AI, company, cues from events. Admin hours already hold your admin tasks in the plan. *Open, waiting for research*: your own good hours for the hardest and most anxious steps (criterion 31 of [capacity-budget.md](research/capacity-budget.md)). Who declares them and how they move with an illness and its treatment, the time of day against fatigue rising through it, and hard first against a small easy start for ADHD are unsettled; working hours are not taken for good hours meanwhile.
4. **Portals** ([sites.md](sites.md)). *Working*: secure mailboxes, chats and other sites pinned in one persistent profile, logged in once; notifications accepted and kept for the Porch (or at once in real time); calls with microphone, camera and a chosen screen; sounds silenced per site; a link from the mail that announces a site; logins from Bitwarden, read by Sioul itself (no other program). *Next*: "file this page" into a project.
5. **Calendar** ([client.md](client.md)). *Working*: one agenda for everything with a time (Day, Week, Month, What comes, with no horizon); invitations by mail answered, and sent for meetings you organise, by the server or a draft, never both; `.ics` files opened and imported; time zones; agendas made, renamed and shared on Nextcloud and Google; your availability shared as busy times; a child's events taken by one parent; dates found in paper letters. *Next*: changing one time's title or place, exporting `.ics` files, subscribing by address.
6. **Contacts**: CardDAV. *Working*: the map (OpenStreetMap; addresses placed once each by Nominatim, else the Base Adresse Nationale or Photon, when you allow it, kept locally), a pin in each card, the map of everyone, pins opening cards; duplicates, on one card and between two cards, cleaned and merged, each change undone with one click for thirty days ([client.md](client.md), "Categories and duplicates"); the people of your household as one object, with their papers, letters and budgets ([persons.md](persons.md)). *Next*: changing a contact's photo here.
7. **Virtual Secretary, mode B** ([virtual-secretary.md](virtual-secretary.md)). Mode A comes with slice 1's sync.
8. **Trust, the rest**: Spamhaus, lookalike domains, the registry of genuine senders. Your own spam filter is built ([spam-filter.md](spam-filter.md)).
9. **Accounting** ([accounting.md](accounting.md), [projects.md](projects.md)). *Working*: budgets, presets, reserves and their cover rules; the verdict at the period's pace, with a realistic end and, from three past periods, a range; a budget's ledger and its balance by day, week, month or year; movements added by hand, once or recurring; amounts read from mail and added as lines; your bank's own exports read and matched with what mail said was paid; money movements made, changed and removed with Undo, in Overview, Budgets, Movements and From mail; projects, time and invoices; the papers wallet, with renewals reminded and planned ([papers.md](papers.md)). *Next*: bank statements as PDF, currency conversion, the French reports.
10. **AI** ([ai.md](ai.md)). *Working*: the MCP server ([mcp.md](mcp.md)): agents read what you open to them, project by project, and write tasks, events, notes, ties, drafts and money records, each change of an event or a task said in the window with Undo; nothing sent, no mail deleted, no money moved. The command line and the Markdown drafts serve agents from slice 1 on. *Next*: the companion, triage.
11. **Elsewhere**. *Working*: Google calendars, contacts and tasks, signed in with your own key, what Google does not keep greyed ([google.md](google.md)); Microsoft's mail, Outlook.com and Microsoft 365, signed in on Microsoft's page ([microsoft.md](microsoft.md)); GitHub's issues and pull requests as tasks, off unless asked ([github.md](github.md)). Several devices sharing what only Sioul keeps, through a folder your sync carries, sealed with a passphrase, or kept in step with a Nextcloud by Sioul itself ([database.md](database.md)); plain files and a log were chosen over a database server ([database.md](database.md), "Why plain files and a log"). *Next*: the gaps listed there under "Not built".
12. **Android, Windows and macOS**. *Working* on Android since 0.0.2 (an APK on the releases page): the window on a phone, the doses reminded, the sharing, do-not-disturb with your other devices; other apps' notifications screened (tried on a phone on 8 October 2026); calls screened, not tried on a phone yet ([android.md](android.md)). Windows and macOS: built and tested by GitHub at each push, packaged with each version, little tried yet ([building.md](building.md)).

More languages come with each slice ([i18n.md](i18n.md)).
