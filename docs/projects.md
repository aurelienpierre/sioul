# Projects, time and invoices

A project is any matter you follow, one list for all of them (`sioul-projects.toml` in your notes folder, [notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md)): the umbrella over its tasks (REFID), events, mail (its routes), notes, time and budget lines. Projects were once "cases" beside "projects"; they are one thing, with one name, and "for a client" is a choice in its form. What was written under the old name still reads ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#its-first-name)). Made, renamed, given its routes and taken out from the Projects page (its form, "Its mail"); taken out, its tasks, notes, mail and time stay where they are. The Porch's settings only choose which projects have a lane there (Porch ⚙, "Projects shown here"). A project **for a client** (`kind = "project"`) has a client, an hourly rate and the budget its invoices are expected in, and its time can be billed.

```toml
[[project]]
id = "lumen"
title = "Studio Lumen — website"
kind = "project"
client = "Studio Lumen"        # or "sioul:contact/<UID>": its client profile, else the card's postal address
rate = 60.0                    # else [invoice] rate
budget = "work"                # invoices expected there until paid
```

## A project's page (Projects, Ctrl+8)
- **One line of time**: what is coming first (dates asked of its open tasks, events), then what happened, newest first (tasks done, mail by its routes or tied by hand, notes of its record or tied to it, time noted, invoices). Mail comes by its routes on sender and subject (matched again each time), by being tied to the project (when it arrived, or by hand), and by conversation: the whole of a conversation one of its messages belongs to ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#routes)). Each message is checked as the Porch checks it, each time the page is shown (`porch::Gate`, which reads the file and asks `porch::admission`, the function the Porch's own lanes use): what the Porch sets aside (a blocked sender, forged, a borrowed name, spam no route protects, hostile) never shows, whether a route, a tie Sioul made or a conversation brings it, and a conversation is followed through the messages let in only, so a forged reply joins nothing; mail nothing authenticates comes by no route on its sender's address or domain, and says "whose address could not be verified"; a message you tied by hand stays, as you tied it. The agents' `list_projects` reads the same page.
- **Its tasks** as a board, a list or a calendar: the Tasks page, filtered to the project.
- **"Note time"**: a meeting, a call, work away from the timer; for a project alone or a task.
- **"Make the invoice"**, when billable time waits: a draft of that time, issued in the same click, or refused with what is missing ("Invoices", below).

## Time (Ctrl+9)
A week, a month or a year: a bar per day (per month over a year) stacked by project, each project's hours and what is left to bill, in hours and in money at its rate, then each stretch of time, newest first.
- **Billed or not, task by task**: a task's form says "Billed": as its project says (work for a client is billed), its time is billed, or not billed (`X-SIOUL-BILLABLE` in the task, kept by CalDAV servers; greyed on Google Tasks).
- **As a spreadsheet**: the export button on the Time page writes a project's billable time as CSV: what (a line per task, or per note), hours, hourly rate, amount, then the total; this week, last week, this month, last month, all time, or from one day to another. In French, `;` between fields and a decimal comma, as French spreadsheets read them. Nothing else goes in: the full invoice is the invoice's (below). Time comes from the focus timer (a task's time counts for its project: the first of its projects that is for a client, else its first project) and from what is noted by hand (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/time/<month>.toml`, sessions with `project`, `unbilled`, `invoice`: the number that billed it, a mark made again from the issued records before each issue, "Invoices" below). Any stretch not billed, timed or noted by hand, changes with a click: its task, its project, its day, from when to when, what it was (`qml/TimeDialog.qml`; moved to another month, it is written there first); its right click takes it out. Billed time stays as it was billed.

## Invoices
Invoices, credit notes and deposit invoices you issue (`crates/sioul-core/src/invoice/`: `record.rs`, `life.rs`, `series.rs`, `totals.rs`, `presets.rs`, `ids.rs`, `html.rs`; the issued file in `pdf.rs`, `pdfa.rs`, `cii.rs`, `epc.rs` and `kept.rs`; client profiles in `clients.rs`). The invoicing's phase 1 (November 2026) built the model and its life, its phase 2 the issued file, both described here. Later: the window's Money ▸ Invoices, the editor and Settings ▸ Invoices by country (phase 3); the books, the receipts book from invoice payments, the declarations and the thresholds (phase 4). What the design rests on: [research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), findings 76 to 80 of [research.md](research.md).

- **One record, in EN 16931's terms** (`record.rs`, `format = 2`): each field is one of the European standard's business terms (BT-xx), so that one record prints its page in any language and becomes an electronic invoice without translation. Its kind is UNTDID 1001's code (BT-3): 380 an invoice, 381 a credit note, 386 a deposit invoice. It holds its series and number, its issue day, the service's day or period (`period_from`, `period_to`: the first and last days of the time billed, the days given, else the day of issue, and none on a deposit invoice, whose service is still to come; service-public.gouv.fr, sheet F31808, and ann. II CGI art. 242 nonies A I-10°), its due day and terms, its currency (ISO 4217) and language, its project, its client as chosen (`client_ref`), the issuer and the client frozen as they were at issue, its lines, its totals, the invoice it corrects (BG-3), the deposit invoices it deducts, the client's purchase order (BT-13), its notes with their subject codes (UNTDID 4451) and the late-payment rate it printed (in basis points); `file` and `file_sha256`, the issued file kept beside it and its SHA-256 ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)); `record_sha256` stays empty: how a record's own hash is read, so that a record changed by hand is said on its row, comes with the window (phase 3). Its payments and the day it was sent are not in it: they are its life, kept apart ("Its life, outside the record", below).
- **Where it stands is read, never typed** (`Invoice::status`, `left`, `credited`, `left_to_credit`): draft, issued, sent, partly paid, paid, credited, from its number, the day it was sent, its payments and the credit notes that cite it. A credit note has a status of its own, "credits invoice 2026-005" (`Status::CreditNote`): it is never paid nor unpaid; while it has money to pay back, it reads "to pay back" (`Status::ToPayBack`, "credits invoice 2026-005 · €120.00 to pay back": "Credit notes", below). Partly paid needs a payment: an invoice credited in part and not paid is issued (or sent), with less left to pay. An invoice whose deposits cover it all, nothing left due, reads paid. Nothing is "overdue": what is unpaid is "not paid yet".
- **Lines** (BG-25): a description and details; a quantity in thousandths of a UN/ECE unit (Recommendation 20: `HUR`, `MIN`, `DAY`, `C62`, `MON`, `KMT`, `LS`); a unit price in cents for `base` units (BT-149); a discount; a VAT category (UNTDID 5305: `S`, `Z`, `E`, `AE`, `K`, `G`, `O`) and its rate in basis points; an exemption's reason; goods or a service; the amount; the time it bills, by session. **Lines from time** come as before, one per task (or per note, for time noted without a task), in the order first worked on, as minutes at the project's hourly price with a base of 60, so that 25 minutes at €60 an hour make €25.00 exactly, as format 1 computed it. **The time billed is what the issued records list** (`life::billed`, `with_billing`): a session an invoice lists, and no credit note freed since, is billed by it whatever its own mark says; the sessions' `invoice` marks are a cache, made again from the records before each issue (`life::reconcile_marks`), so that a crash between a record and its marks never lets the same time be billed twice.
- **Totals** (`totals.rs`), computed once in integer cents as EN 16931 checks them (BR-CO-10 to 17): each line to the cent, half away from zero (Factur-X §7.1.8); per category and rate its base and its tax; then without tax, the tax, with tax, the deposits deducted (BT-113) and the amount due (BT-115). No float in a sum: quantities are thousandths, rates basis points; a project's rate, still a float in `sioul-projects.toml`, becomes cents at that boundary.
- **Drafts** in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/invoices/drafts/<id>.toml`, beside the records, so that they travel with them: a draft has no number, and its id is when it was made and six letters (`20261116-143005-k3x9qa`), never a number: its file's name, letters, digits and dashes only, never what the file says (`draft_id_ok`). It changes freely (`life::change_draft`, which the editor of phase 3 calls): `life::refresh` takes the issuer as the settings say it, each line's tax where it is due ("Where VAT is due", below), the legal lines the preset prints for this client, the late-payment rate, the due day and the totals, and, when nothing says the service's day (no time billed, no day given), the day of issue, shown on the draft and printed (`period_default`; issued, the issue's own day), never on a deposit invoice; your own notes stay. Its client is made again from its profile, or its contact's card, until it is issued (`life::refresh_client`: at issue, and in `sioul invoices show`), so that a profile changed meanwhile counts; a client written as a name alone, or whose profile and card are gone, stays as the draft has it, and a credit note keeps the credited invoice's client as it was. A sync app's conflicted copy of a draft ("<id> (conflicted copy …)") is listed as a draft of its own, its id made of its file's name, and says which draft it copies (`Invoice::copy_of`), never hidden; saved, it keeps that id, and its file of before goes. `sioul invoices discard <draft>` takes a draft out. A draft is made from a project's time not billed yet (`life::from_time`), for a client (`life::for_client`), blank, as a credit note (`life::credit`), or, in the core only for now, as a deposit invoice (`life::deposit`).
- **Checked before issue** (`life::checks`): what the preset asks and the draft lacks, in plain words and in the invoice's language; and what EN 16931 refuses in the issued file's data: a unit price below zero (BR-27: a discount or a credit note says less), a unit outside Sioul's Recommendation 20 codes (a word such as "hrs" or "jours" is read as its code, by the agents' `draft_invoice` too), a country that is none (your own, the client's, the delivery's: by its code or its name, in the one table of countries, `clients::country`), characters the font cannot print and right-to-left writing. The window's one click says the notes, what is recommended and not required (no Factur-X data, an IBAN whose key fails…), in the status line as it issues; the editor of phase 3 says them before. Required items refuse the issue; recommended ones are said only ("The client's SIREN: asked on invoices to French businesses from September 2027."). Identifiers are checked by their keys (`ids.rs`: a SIREN and a SIRET by Luhn, La Poste's own rule too; a VAT number by its own country, the two letters it starts with, never by the issuer's: a French one by its key, another member state's, the United Kingdom's and a Swiss UID by their form, an unknown prefix taken as written, `ids::vat_valid`). An identifier counts toward what is required only when the invoice prints it (`printed`, true unless set otherwise). « en cours d'attribution » as the SIRET, or an Other identifier saying « SIRET en cours d'attribution », stands for it while it is being given (service-public F31808), and prints « SIRET en cours d'attribution ». The checks take the issued records too: a credit note is checked against what is left to credit ("Credit notes", below). A table that issued invoices before may now be refused where its preset requires what it lacks (your SIREN; the address of a French business client, which every client without a profile now is; the country of a client whose address names none Sioul knows; your legal form, "Older tables" below): a change of behaviour, said here.
- **Issued all or nothing** (`life::issue`): refused, in words, on a device whose sharing is on but leaves out the `drafts` part, which carries the invoices ("Turn on Drafts and invoices in Sharing to issue invoices on this device, or issue them on the device that has it."), since the others' numbers may not be known there; refused, the draft taken out, when an issued record names that draft already (a crash between the record and the draft's removal: `invoice-draft-issued`), and refused for a sync app's copy of a draft issued since, kept for you to read (`invoice-draft-copy-issued`); its client made again from its profile or card (`life::refresh_client`, through `Steps::clients`; a credit note's is the credited invoice's, copied as it was), then checked; refused when some of its time was billed meanwhile, as the records say it (the sessions' marks made again from them first). Then, under the numbering's lock: numbered, its issuer frozen as the settings say it that day and its client as made then, its file made and kept as `invoices/<number>.pdf` (never over a file already there), the record kept as `invoices/<number>.toml` naming that file with its SHA-256 (never over another file: of two numbers that make one file name, "F:1" and "F/1", the second is refused), this device's series journal written; its time marked (a credit note's cancelled time freed); for an invoice in euros, the ledger's money, a planned line in the project's budget, tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>` and due on its due day (an invoice in another currency is expected in no budget, and no bank line in euros ever joins it); for a credit note, the credited invoice's planned line lowered by its amount instead, taken out when nothing is left of it (`budget::lower_planned`; a line already paid stays as it is); the draft taken out. A step that fails undoes those before it, a lowered line put back too (`budget::restore_lowered`), and its number taken out of this device's journal, under the numbering's lock, only while the journal still says it (`life::forget_number`): a number another process gave meanwhile stays, and the gap is said. **Make the invoice** on the Projects page stays one click: a draft of the project's time, issued at once; refused, it says what is missing and leaves no draft behind (`crates/sioul-app/src/projects.rs`, `make_invoice`).
- **Numbers** (`series.rs`): a pattern and its reset make a sequence. A pattern holds `{YYYY}`, `{YY}`, `{MM}`, and one counter, `{N}` or `{N:k}` (k digits at least), the rest as written; `reset = "yearly"` (then the year is in the pattern) or `"never"`. `[invoice] pattern` when set, else the older `prefix`: empty, `{YYYY}-{N:3}` from 1 again each January (2026-001); a prefix of your own and three digits, going on from the last whatever the year (F-041, F-042). Each number is one more than the largest of the series journal, the records (those that no longer read too) and every device's claim (`series::next`). The journal keeps each sequence's last number and its day, each device in its own file, `invoices/series/<device>.toml`, which no other device writes (so the sync never makes a conflicted copy of it, and an older Sioul, which lists only the folder's top files, never takes it for an invoice), read as the largest of every device's (the single `invoices/series.toml` of phase 1's first builds is read too); written in the same step as the record, under the numbering's lock: a record deleted by hand no longer frees its number, and a number of the journal's range with no record here is said ("2026-004 has no record on this device"; when the sequence's numbers hold a month, or a year it never restarts on, by its counter, "F2026{MM}-{N:3}: number 4 has no record on this device", `series::missing`), never given again. The lease's claim carries each sequence's last number too (`sioul_sync::lease::Claim::series`, from the journals at each renewal, right after each issue, the window's as the command line's, and when the window closes, `lease::close_with`; [database.md](database.md#leases-one-computer-at-a-time)), so that a device whose records have not come yet never gives one of them again. The window refuses to number when sharing is set up but its key cannot be read (a keyring locked: the others' claims cannot be read either), as the command line does ("Your sharing folder cannot be written now…", `invoice-unchecked`). Records list in their natural order, 2026-999 before 2026-1000.
- **Credit notes** (`life::credit`): a draft citing the invoice's number and day (BG-3), with all its lines (to cancel it) or some (to correct them), each line keeping the tax it was billed with, its exemption's words too (`Line::own_tax`), should the scheme have changed since; in the invoices' series, unless `credit_pattern` gives them one of their own; no payment terms; a business's credit note carries the payment lines too (escompte, penalties, the €40 indemnity), as France's rules ask of every invoice (BR-FR-05). Never beyond what is left to credit: a credit note for more than the invoice's amount less the credit notes issued for it is refused before issue, naming them (`Invoice::left_to_credit`, a required check), so that an invoice is never credited twice. Issued, it settles what it credits: an invoice credited whole reads "credited", one credited in part has the rest left to pay. It reads "credits invoice 2026-005", never paid nor unpaid. When the invoice it credits was paid beyond what it now asks (its amount less every credit note), the credit note has that money to pay back (`Invoice::pay_back_share`: what was paid beyond, shared among the invoice's credit notes in their numbers' order, each up to its own amount; `to_pay_back`, less what it paid back): it reads "to pay back €120.00" (`Status::ToPayBack`) and takes **Paid back**, a box on its row in the project's page (`InvoiceRow::pay_back`, `pay_rest`), or amounts paid back (`sioul invoices pay <credit note> --amount`, `add_payment`), never more than is left to pay back; with nothing to pay back, its row has no box, and a payment on it is refused in words (`invoice-nothing-to-pay-back`). Money paid back is a negative receipt on its day (`life::received`, "Payments", below). It frees for billing again the time of each line it cancels whole, and all of the invoice's time when it credits the whole invoice (a format-1 invoice, whose lines hold no time of their own: all its time when credited whole; for a line credited alone, the sessions of that line's name among the time noted, which `life::credit` takes). Issuing it lowers the credited invoice's planned budget line by its amount, and takes the line out when nothing is left of it (above, "Issued all or nothing"), so that the client's next transfer is never paired with a credited invoice.
- **Deposits and final invoices** (`life::deposit`, `cite_deposits`), in the core only for now: a deposit invoice (386) bills an amount up front, excluding tax; a final invoice cites the deposit invoices it deducts, by number, day and amount with tax, and its amount due is what is left. A deposit invoice prints no service day: the service is still to come. A deposit is deducted by one final invoice at a time: a final invoice citing a deposit that another final invoice, not cancelled whole, deducts already is refused by a required check (`invoice-check-deposit-taken`); a credit note that cancels a final invoice whole deducts its deposits as the invoice did, and so gives them back, to be deducted again. No command, agent's tool or window makes either yet: they come with the editor's "Deposit…" (phase 3).
- **Payments**, as read (`Payment`: `{id, date, amount, means, line, fee}`; `line` the key of the bank line it came from, or that was found for it when it was said by hand, `movements::line_key`; `fee` what a processor kept of it), each counted once, whatever the devices did ("Its life, outside the record", below). A payment with an amount is never more than is left to pay, "Paid" aside (`life::add_payment` refuses it, in words); on a credit note, it is money paid back ("Credit notes", above). **Paid**, on the Projects page or the invoice's card in the agenda, says "Paid" on that day, by transfer (`life::pay_rest`, a `[[settled]]` entry): it covers what the other payments leave, read each time, so that Paid said on two devices, or before or after the bank's line comes, counts once; its budget line follows ("Budget", below); **Undo** takes it back; **Not paid** takes back everything said by hand (payments with an amount, "Paid", a format-1 record's own `paid`), never what the bank's lines paid (`life::unpay`). **From the bank**: a bank line that the matching joins to the planned lines of invoices, when an export is taken in, pays them (`movements::acts::resolve`, `life::take_bank_line`): one invoice, or several when one transfer pays them together, split across them, the oldest first, each up to what the bank's lines and the payments said in cash left it, by transfer, and what remains is said as paid beyond what they asked; when read, a payment said by hand with room for it takes the line in as its evidence ("Its life, outside the record", below). What the client paid counts, on the day it was paid: when a processor's export says them, the movement's gross on its day paid at the processor (`Movement::gross_or_net`, `paid_day`), its fees being the payment's fee; when only the net is known, a line through a processor (PayPal, Stripe: the account's kind, else the line's label) short of what is owed by at most the processor's usual fee (its published rule, `Processor::about_fee`) settles the invoices, the difference said as its fee in the status line ("€2.15 kept by the processor, as its fee.", `invoice-paid-fee`) and kept with the payment, never written into the money store. Nothing is written on a credit note, on an invoice with nothing left to pay, nor on an invoice in another currency than the bank line's, which is then not kept as joined to it either. Once per bank line and invoice, whatever the device: the same line taken in again, here or on another device, writes nothing more, and a line taken back from an invoice never pays it again. The window says it in the status line with Undo, which takes those payments back and keeps the bank line apart from the invoices' line from then on (`movements::acts::not_same`), and nothing asks to confirm it ([accounting.md](accounting.md#payments-on-your-invoices)). **Not the same payment**, in a movement's menu on the Budgets page, does both for a line found wrong later (`acts::not_same_paid`, which takes back what that line paid on any invoice, by its key: `life::take_back_line`); an agent's `same_movement` with `same = false` keeps the money store apart only ([mcp.md](mcp.md)). Payments add up: one smaller than what is left makes the invoice partly paid. `life::received` and `received_without_tax` give the turnover received from one day to another, excluding VAT, by the nature of what was sold, money paid back on a credit note being a negative receipt on its day, for phase 4's books.
- **Its life, outside the record** (`invoice::lives`): an issued record is never written again, so that its file (and phase 2's kept PDF, with their hashes) stays as it was issued. What happens to it later is written in `invoices/life/<device>.toml` (`format = 1`), each device's own file, which no other device writes, in entries that never name one another, so that nothing dangles when one is taken back: `[[payment]]`, money received with its amount (id, invoice, date, amount, means, line, fee), either said by hand, its id made of what it says (`hand:<invoice>/<day>/<amount>/<means>/<n>`, `n` its rank among payments alike said on this device), or a bank line's share for an invoice, its id made of the line's key, the same on every device that takes the line in (`bank:<line>/<invoice>`; `movements::line_key`: its account, day, amount and a digest of its label); `[[settled]]`, "Paid" said on a day (`settled:<invoice>/<n>`), its amount read each time as what the payments with an amount leave; `[[removed]]`, an entry taken back by its id (an Undo, "Not paid", "Not the same payment"), whichever device wrote it; and `[[sent]]`, the day it was sent. Reading merges every device's file by union, each id once, a removal winning (`lives::Lives::load`; `all_in` gives each record its life), so that Paid said on two devices, the same export taken in on two, and the same payment typed on two before they exchange each count once; a bank line split across invoices on two devices at once counts as the first file split it. Then each invoice's payments are read (`Lives::apply`): the bank's first, each taken in by a payment said by hand with room for it, of its very amount first, else the nearest in days, never one said in cash, which keeps its own day and amount (the line is its evidence, never a second payment), what is left of the line beyond them counting as its own; then "Paid", a format-1 record's own word among them, covers what is left of what the invoice asked on the first day it was said (its amount less the credit notes issued by then), on that day. "Paid" taken back on one device while another takes the bank's line in leaves the bank's payment. Since each file has one writer, the sync never makes a conflicted copy of a record or of its life. A life file or a series journal of a newer format is read where it can be, never written over (`change_own` and `Journal::save` refuse, asking to update Sioul on this device), and said (`Lives::newer`, `Journal::newer`, in `sioul invoices list`); a record of a newer format is read as format 2 where it reads so, and said (`Invoice::newer`). A second file holding a number another holds is one record when it is the same invoice (an older Sioul's Paid aside, which counts if either says it); otherwise two devices gave that number at once (`invoice::twice_in`, `Twice`): the file its number names is the record read, the other is kept, and both are said, by `sioul invoices list` ("2026-014 was given twice, to Studio Lumen and to Atelier Exemple (2026-014 (conflicted copy).toml): issue a credit note for one, then invoice it again.") and by one calm row for all such numbers on the Porch's Money lane, in work's hours, until it is dismissed or none is left (`notices::invoices_twice`, `money:invoices-twice/<numbers>`). Life writers take the device, the sharing's own id (`Numbering::device`, `add_payment`, `take_back`, `pay_rest`, `unpay`, `take_bank_line`, `take_back_line`). Records, their issued files, drafts, life files and journals are written whole through Sioul's shared writer (`filelock::replace`: a hidden file renamed over the old one, tried again on Windows while another program holds it a moment).
- **Format-1 records** (before November 2026, no `format` key) read as issued invoices of kind 380 with time lines (`record::V1`): their SIRET an identifier, their VAT line the franchise's mention (category `E`), or no VAT (`O`) without one. They are never written: their own `paid` reads as one more "Paid", said on their due day (`older:<number>`), which covers only what the other payments leave, which "Not paid" or an Undo takes back with a removal entry, and which an older Sioul ticking Paid changes; their payments and the day they were sent go into the life files as any record's. They print as they always did, in the window's language. A record that no longer reads still holds its number, and is never written over. An older Sioul never reads a format-2 record as its own (`record::tests::an_older_sioul_never_reads_a_format_2_record_as_its_own`), so that its Paid never writes one back without its lines, its taxes and its client; to it, such a record is a number it never gives again, which is why every device that shares invoices is updated together (`packaging/release-notes.md`).
- **Its own language and currency**: an invoice keeps its language (`en` or `fr`: the client profile's, else `[invoice] language`, else the window's) and its currency (the profile's, the settings', the preset's, else euros), prints in them whatever the window speaks, and shows its amounts in its currency in every view (the project's page, the agenda's dated card, `sioul invoices`, the agents), where a euro sign showed before whatever the currency.
- **The page** (`html.rs`) prints the issuer with its mark and identifiers, the client with its identifiers and delivery address, each party's country when the two differ, by its name in the invoice's language ("Belgique" on an invoice in French, `Address::text`), the nature of the operation, the date or period of the service, each line's quantity, unit and unit price, the VAT per rate whenever the invoice carries VAT (liable, or a credit note of an invoice from when you were, whatever your scheme on its day), the totals, the deposits deducted, the legal lines and how to pay: a draft's preview (`sioul invoices export --html`; the editor's, in phase 3), and the issued file says the same words ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)). A format-1 record keeps no file: the window prints its page to an A4 PDF through Qt's text document and PDF writer, as it always did, in `[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices` (Factures in French).
- **Budget**: the amount due of an invoice in euros, the ledger's money, is expected in the project's budget, a planned line on the due day (the terms: the client profile's, the settings', the preset's, else 30 days), tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>` (an invoice in another currency is expected in no budget, since the ledger does not convert yet); a credit note lowers the credited invoice's line, or takes it out ("Credit notes", above); ticking "Paid" dates it today and makes it real, with "Undo" for ten seconds; unticked, it is planned again on the day it had, its due day when that is not known (`budget::unsettle_line`), where it was in the ledger, so that Paid then not paid leaves the budget as it was.
- **From the command line** (`crates/sioul-cli/src/invoices.rs`): `sioul invoices [list] [--client <words>] [--year <year>] [--status <status>]` (drafts first, a sync app's copy of a draft said so, then the invoices, newest first, each with where it stands; `--status` draft, issued, sent, partly-paid, paid, credited, credit-note or to-pay-back; a record that no longer reads, a number with no record here, a number given twice, and a file written by a newer Sioul, said), `show <number or draft>` (its lines, totals and payments, its client and whether it is billed as a business, a person or a public body, "a business, as no client profile says otherwise; its path), `draft <project>` (a draft of the project's time not billed yet; nothing numbered), `issue <draft>` (you at the keyboard, under the window's rules: one device numbers at a time, never one whose sharing leaves invoices out or whose sharing key cannot be read; with sharing on, only when this computer's window may number, as the window itself asks it (`sioul_sync::lease::may_number`: this computer's claim alive, renewed within two minutes and never closed, the keeper's, settled, no other computer's closed claim unread, the others heard from lately), else "Open Sioul on this computer first…" (`invoice-cli-open-sioul`) or the window's own words, since a claim of the command line's own would lapse unseen when it ends; right after issuing, it renews that claim with the journal's last numbers (`claim_numbers`), never reopening one the window closed meanwhile, whose numbers `lease::close_with` raises instead; the draft's client made again from its profile or card; its notes said, its file copied where invoices go and its path said, a copy that cannot be written said apart, the invoice issued all the same), `credit <number> [--line <n>]…` (a draft credit note, all lines or those given, never beyond what is left to credit), `discard <draft>` (a draft taken out: nothing was numbered), `pay <number> [--day <day>] [--amount <amount>] [--means transfer|card|cash|cheque|direct-debit|other]` (a payment received, or, on a credit note, money paid back; what is left when no amount is given, never more).
- **Agents**: `invoices` reads drafts and invoices, `draft_invoice` writes drafts only, when you ask; neither issues, sends nor records a payment ([mcp.md](mcp.md)).

### The issued file
Issued, an invoice is a file, written once and kept beside its record ([research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), §15; finding 80 of [research.md](research.md)).

- **A PDF/A-3b, written in Rust** (`pdf.rs`, krilla 0.8.2): A4, 18 mm margins; Liberation Sans regular and bold, embedded and subset (`crates/sioul-core/data/fonts/`: version 2.1.5, byte for byte as released, SIL Open Font License 1.1, its licence beside and in every package with `NOTICES.md`); its colours in sRGB, with sRGB's output intent; its creation day the day of issue, so that the same record makes the same bytes. It says what the page says (`html::current`), word for word, in the invoice's language and currency: a test holds what the layout draws to the page's words, and every word inside its page (`pdf::tests::the_pdf_says_what_the_page_says`). A long invoice goes on as many pages as it takes, the table's head again on each, each page saying which it is; a line longer than a page goes on to the next under the head again, its figures on its first part; a column's head too wide for its column goes on two lines, and a total wider than its column reaches left, its label ending before it; a test holds every word drawn to no other on its line. A character the font lacks (a script other than Latin, Greek or Cyrillic) holds the issue back, said before (`pdf::unprintable`), since a PDF/A never draws a missing glyph; right-to-left writing (Hebrew, Arabic) too (`pdf::right_to_left`), which would print backwards without Unicode's bidirectional ordering; a non-breaking hyphen prints as a hyphen, a narrow no-break space as a no-break space.
- **Its Factur-X XML** (`cii.rs`): Factur-X 1.09.2 / ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 in the EN 16931 profile (`urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017`), UN/CEFACT CII D22B written by Sioul's own serializer on quick-xml, element by element in the schema's order, from the record's own terms, nothing computed again. Attached as `factur-x.xml` (`text/xml`, the relationship *Alternative*: the PDF and the XML say the same invoice, the one relationship Germany accepts), listed in the catalog's `/AF`. The VAT categories: `S` with its rate; `E` with its reason (the French franchise: `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE` and its mention); `AE`, the reverse charge, with both VAT numbers; `O`, not registered, with `VATEX-EU-O`, no rate and neither side's VAT number; `K`, goods to a business in another member state, with `VATEX-EU-IC`, the delivery country and day; `G`, an export, with `VATEX-EU-G`; `Z`. The identifiers: a French SIREN (a SIRET's first nine digits) as the legal registration (scheme 0002), the VAT number (`VA`), a Steuernummer, a UTR or a tax number elsewhere as a tax registration (`FC`), another identifier as the seller's own when it has none of these; France's business process (BT-23: `B1` goods, `S1` services, `M1` both); the account to pay into, the one the QR code names (the first whose IBAN's key holds, else the first): a SEPA transfer (58) when its key holds and the invoice is in euros, else a credit transfer (30) with the number as written (`ProprietaryID`, after the holder's name, in the schema's order), the invoice's number as the payment's reference; the day the supply was completed (BT-72): the last day of the period billed, else the day of issue, none on a deposit invoice, which bills what is not supplied yet (its period, when it says one, as BG-14); the invoices it cites (BG-3, unbounded in the profile's schema): the one a credit note corrects, the deposit invoices a final one deducts; the countries as ISO 3166-1 codes (`clients::country`, the one table of countries, `data/countries.tsv`: UK as GB, EL as GR, a name in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese or its own languages as its code); the units as UN/ECE Recommendation 20's (`units.rs`).
- **Its XMP** (`pdfa.rs`): the Factur-X properties (`fx:DocumentType` INVOICE, `fx:DocumentFileName`, `fx:Version` 1.0, `fx:ConformanceLevel` EN 16931) and their schema, among the PDF/A extension schemas krilla declares (one `pdfaExtension:schemas` only: a property twice, and no reader takes the XMP). krilla 0.8.2 takes no XMP property of one's own (its issue #209), so the metadata stream krilla wrote is written again in place, its length with it, the cross-reference offsets after it moved by as much: one object changes, the file stays one revision. lopdf, which the design named for this, would rewrite the whole file and bring some twenty crates (ciphers, hashes, a random generator) for one stream; this takes none, and a test reads the table back.
- **Without its XML** (`cii::without`), said before issue as a note, never as a reason not to issue: under the French franchise without a VAT number, since EN 16931's exempt category asks the seller's VAT or tax number (BR-E-02) and the French rule that says which number to give instead, BR-FR-CO-16 of AFNOR's XP Z12-012, is still to be read (design question 1); and an invoice whose rules lack your VAT or tax number, the client's VAT number (a reverse charge, a delivery to another member state), an identifier of yours, your country or the client's as a code, a line's unit or price the standard takes, or (a deposit for goods to another member state) the period it covers; lines outside the scope of VAT beside others, which EN 16931 keeps apart. The PDF is then a PDF/A-3 without attachment: a complete invoice on paper, as French law asks of small businesses until 1 September 2027.
- **The EPC QR code** (`epc.rs`; EPC069-12 version 3.1): on euro invoices with an IBAN whose key holds, never on a credit note: version `002`, UTF-8, `SCT`, the BIC when given, the account holder's name (else yours, at most 70 characters), the IBAN, the amount due, the invoice's number as the remittance; error correction M, version 13 at most, 331 bytes at most; drawn as squares (qrcodegen's modules, a run of dark ones one rectangle), 25 mm with its quiet zone, at the right of the payment details, "Scan to pay by transfer" under it. `[invoice] qr = false` leaves it out. An IBAN whose key fails is said before issue.
- **Kept** (`kept.rs`): written in the same step as the record and its number, under the numbering's lock. The record is the commit: the file is written first under a name the sharing leaves alone (`<number>.pdf.new`), takes its name once the record is written, and is taken away when a step after it fails. An issue stopped halfway (a crash, a phone closing the app) never blocks the next number: before numbering, the device that numbers gives its name to a waiting file whose record came with its SHA-256, and sets aside in `invoices/leftovers/` any other waiting file and any kept file no record holds, said once in the status line (`kept::tidy`). The record names its file (`file`, which must be its number's, `<number>.pdf`, else nothing is handed out) with its SHA-256 (`file_sha256`). It travels with the records, in the sharing's part for drafts and invoices ([database.md](database.md)). **Print again**, issuing in the window or the terminal, and `sioul invoices export --pdf` copy it byte for byte where invoices go (`[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices`), so that a PDF reader saving into its copy never changes the file kept; a copy there already the same is left as it is. A kept file gone or changed is made again from the frozen record (the QR code on or off, as it was) and kept again when this Sioul makes the bytes issued, a changed one first set aside in `leftovers/`; when it cannot (another version of Sioul made them), nothing is handed out, and the sentence says where the record and the file are (`kept::issued`). When the copy where invoices go cannot be written, the invoice is issued all the same, and the status line says so ("Print again once that folder is there"). Format-1 records keep no file: their page is printed through Qt as before.
- **Checked in CI** (`.github/workflows/e-invoices.yml`, on pushes to main touching what goes into an invoice's file, and by hand, as the other workflows run): sioul-core's ignored test `invoice::samples::write_fixtures` writes one invoice per preset and VAT scheme, a credit note, a deposit invoice and the final one, an account whose IBAN's key fails, three pages of lines, a line longer than a page (`valid/`), one made wrong on purpose with the rule it breaks beside it (its totals a cent off, BR-CO-15, `invalid/`), and the French franchise without a VAT number (`without-xml/`). Mustang CLI 2.26.0 (its jar's SHA-256 pinned) reads each with veraPDF, the EN 16931 schematron and France's rules of XP Z12-012 inside, and `tools/einvoice/validate.sh` expects each part of its report valid and no word from the French rules on a good fixture, the broken one invalid for its own rule, and the PDF/A-3b of the third valid; it lists every warning. The same runs offline with Mustang's jar and Java (the script's comment says how). No invoice goes to an online validator.

### Countries: presets as data

- **Three settings apart**: the legal form (France: an entrepreneur individuel, micro-entrepreneur included, or EURL, SASU, SARL, SAS, SA; the other presets: a sole trader or a company), the income regime for the books (France: micro or réel), and the VAT scheme, dated (`none`, not registered; `franchise`, the small-business exemption with its mention; `liable`), since none implies another: an EI micro-entrepreneur liable for VAT prints « EI » and VAT per rate; a company under the franchise prints the franchise's mention, its form and its capital. Sioul prints « EI » after a French entrepreneur individuel's name (C. com. R526-27), whatever the VAT, unless the name says it already, at its start, at its end, or in words (`life::marked_name`). A French company's capital ("SASU au capital de 1 000 €") and its RCS city are required; an EI registered as a trader (activity `goods-bic` or `services-bic`) is advised to give its RCS city. A VAT scheme the preset does not offer (`none` under France, where every business is under the franchise or liable) is refused by a required check, never taken as said.
- **Where VAT is due, as data** (`life::placed`, at each `refresh`; `default_tax` for a new line): the preset's `[[tax.place]]` rules, each for some VAT schemes, client zones (domestic, another EU member state, abroad), client kinds and natures (goods or a service), give a line its category, its reason code and its words; the first that applies wins (the review of phase 1, F4). ## Budgets at their pace
A budget's verdict no longer compares a projection made only of what is scheduled with its target, which said "short" on the 3rd of every month. What is known in advance (presets, planned lines) counts in full; the rest, what simply happens, is weighed against the part of the period gone: ten days into thirty, a third of what the period still needs should have come in (or no more than a third of what it may spend should be spent). Within the one tolerance of every verdict, 5 % of what the period moves, it is on track ([accounting.md](accounting.md), "On track, without assuming money flows evenly").

The end of the period is estimated:
- with fewer than three past periods: the difference of today carried to the end (no extrapolation of three days into a month);
- with three or more: what is known, plus the median past period's unscheduled movements for the part left; the worst and best past periods give a range (the 10th and 90th percentiles from ten periods on).
