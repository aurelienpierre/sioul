# Projects, time and invoices

A project is any matter you follow, one list for all of them (`sioul-projects.toml` in your notes folder, [notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md)): the umbrella over its tasks (REFID), events, mail (its routes), notes, time and budget lines. Projects were once "cases" beside "projects"; they are one thing, with one name, and "for a client" is a choice in its form. What was written under the old name still reads ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#its-first-name)). Made, renamed, given its routes and taken out from the Projects page (its form, "Its mail"); taken out, its tasks, notes, mail and time stay where they are. The Porch's settings only choose which projects have a lane there (Porch ⚙, "Projects shown here"). A project **for a client** (`kind = "project"`) has a client, an hourly rate and the budget its invoices are expected in, and its time can be billed.

```toml
[[project]]
id = "lumen"
title = "Studio Lumen — website"
kind = "project"
client = "Studio Lumen"        # or "sioul:contact/<UID>": its client profile, else the card's postal address
rate = 60.0                    # else [invoice] rate
budget = "work"                # invoices expected there until paid
```

## A project's page (Projects, Ctrl+8)
- **One line of time**: what is coming first (dates asked of its open tasks, events), then what happened, newest first (tasks done, mail by its routes or tied by hand, notes of its record or tied to it, time noted, invoices). Mail comes by its routes on sender and subject (matched again each time), by being tied to the project (when it arrived, or by hand), and by conversation: the whole of a conversation one of its messages belongs to ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#routes)). Each message is checked as the Porch checks it, each time the page is shown (`porch::Gate`, which reads the file and asks `porch::admission`, the function the Porch's own lanes use): what the Porch sets aside (a blocked sender, forged, a borrowed name, spam no route protects, hostile) never shows, whether a route, a tie Sioul made or a conversation brings it, and a conversation is followed through the messages let in only, so a forged reply joins nothing; mail nothing authenticates comes by no route on its sender's address or domain, and says "whose address could not be verified"; a message you tied by hand stays, as you tied it. The agents' `list_projects` reads the same page.
- **Its tasks** as a board, a list or a calendar: the Tasks page, filtered to the project.
- **"Note time"**: a meeting, a call, work away from the timer; for a project alone or a task.
- **"Make the invoice"**, when billable time waits: a draft of that time, issued in the same click, or refused with what is missing ("Invoices", below).

## Time (Ctrl+9)
A week, a month or a year: a bar per day (per month over a year) stacked by project, each project's hours and what is left to bill, in hours and in money at its rate, then each stretch of time, newest first.
- **Billed or not, task by task**: a task's form says "Billed": as its project says (work for a client is billed), its time is billed, or not billed (`X-SIOUL-BILLABLE` in the task, kept by CalDAV servers; greyed on Google Tasks).
- **As a spreadsheet**: the export button on the Time page writes a project's billable time as CSV: what (a line per task, or per note), hours, hourly rate, amount, then the total; this week, last week, this month, last month, all time, or from one day to another. In French, `;` between fields and a decimal comma, as French spreadsheets read them. Nothing else goes in: the full invoice is the invoice's (below). Time comes from the focus timer (a task's time counts for its project: the first of its projects that is for a client, else its first project) and from what is noted by hand (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/time/<month>.toml`, sessions with `project`, `unbilled`, `invoice`). Any stretch not billed, timed or noted by hand, changes with a click: its task, its project, its day, from when to when, what it was (`qml/TimeDialog.qml`; moved to another month, it is written there first); its right click takes it out. Billed time stays as it was billed.

## Invoices
Invoices, credit notes and deposit invoices you issue (`crates/sioul-core/src/invoice/`: `record.rs`, `life.rs`, `series.rs`, `totals.rs`, `presets.rs`, `ids.rs`, `html.rs`; the issued file in `pdf.rs`, `pdfa.rs`, `cii.rs`, `epc.rs` and `kept.rs`; client profiles in `clients.rs`). The invoicing's phase 1 (November 2026) built the model and its life, its phase 2 the issued file, both described here. Later: the window's Money ▸ Invoices, the editor and Settings ▸ Invoices by country (phase 3); the books, the receipts book from invoice payments, the declarations and the thresholds (phase 4). What the design rests on: [research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), findings 76 to 80 of [research.md](research.md).

- **One record, in EN 16931's terms** (`record.rs`, `format = 2`): each field is one of the European standard's business terms (BT-xx), so that one record prints its page in any language and becomes an electronic invoice without translation. Its kind is UNTDID 1001's code (BT-3): 380 an invoice, 381 a credit note, 386 a deposit invoice. It holds its series and number, its issue day, the service's day or period (`period_from`, `period_to`: the first and last days billed; service-public.gouv.fr, sheet F31808), its due day and terms, its currency (ISO 4217) and language, its project, its client as chosen (`client_ref`), the issuer and the client frozen as they were at issue, its lines, its totals, the invoice it corrects (BG-3), the deposit invoices it deducts, the client's purchase order (BT-13), its notes with their subject codes (UNTDID 4451), the late-payment rate it printed (in basis points), its payments and the day it was sent; `file` and `file_sha256`, the issued file kept beside it and its SHA-256 ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)). `record_sha256` stays empty: how a record's own hash is read, so that a record changed by hand is said on its row, comes with the window (phase 3).
- **Where it stands is read, never typed** (`Invoice::status`, `left`, `credited`): draft, issued, sent, partly paid, paid, credited, from its number, the day it was sent, its payments and the credit notes that cite it. Nothing is "overdue": what is unpaid is "not paid yet".
- **Lines** (BG-25): a description and details; a quantity in thousandths of a UN/ECE unit (Recommendation 20: `HUR`, `MIN`, `DAY`, `C62`, `MON`, `KMT`, `LS`); a unit price in cents for `base` units (BT-149); a discount; a VAT category (UNTDID 5305: `S`, `Z`, `E`, `AE`, `K`, `G`, `O`) and its rate in basis points; an exemption's reason; goods or a service; the amount; the time it bills, by session. **Lines from time** come as before, one per task (or per note, for time noted without a task), in the order first worked on, as minutes at the project's hourly price with a base of 60, so that 25 minutes at €60 an hour make €25.00 exactly, as format 1 computed it.
- **Totals** (`totals.rs`), computed once in integer cents as EN 16931 checks them (BR-CO-10 to 17): each line to the cent, half away from zero (Factur-X §7.1.8); per category and rate its base and its tax; then without tax, the tax, with tax, the deposits deducted (BT-113) and the amount due (BT-115). No float in a sum: quantities are thousandths, rates basis points; a project's rate, still a float in `sioul-projects.toml`, becomes cents at that boundary.
- **Drafts** in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/invoices/drafts/<id>.toml`, beside the records, so that they travel with them: a draft has no number, and its id is when it was made and six letters (`20261116-143005-k3x9qa`), never a number. It changes freely (`life::change_draft`, which the editor of phase 3 calls): `life::refresh` takes the issuer as the settings say it, each line's tax as the scheme asks, the legal lines the preset prints for this client, the late-payment rate, the due day and the totals; your own notes stay. A draft is made from a project's time not billed yet (`life::from_time`), for a client (`life::for_client`), blank, as a credit note or a deposit invoice.
- **Checked before issue** (`life::checks`): what the preset asks and the draft lacks, in plain words and in the invoice's language; and what EN 16931 refuses in the issued file's data: a unit price below zero (BR-27: a discount or a credit note says less), a unit outside Sioul's Recommendation 20 codes (a word such as "hrs" or "jours" is read as its code, by the agents' `draft_invoice` too), a country that is none (your own, the client's, the delivery's), characters the font cannot print and right-to-left writing. The window's one click says the notes, what is recommended and not required (no Factur-X data, an IBAN whose key fails…), in the status line as it issues; the editor of phase 3 says them before. Required items refuse the issue; recommended ones are said only ("The client's SIREN: asked on invoices to French businesses from September 2027."). Identifiers are checked by their keys (`ids.rs`: a SIREN and a SIRET by Luhn, La Poste's own rule too; a French VAT number by its key). A table that issued invoices before may now be refused where its preset requires what it lacks (your SIREN, a French business client's address): a change of behaviour, said here.
- **Issued all or nothing** (`life::issue`): checked first; refused, in words, on a device whose sharing is on but leaves out the `drafts` part, which carries the invoices ("Turn on Drafts and invoices in Sharing to issue invoices on this device, or issue them on the device that has it."), since the others' numbers may not be known there; refused when some of its time was billed meanwhile. Then, under the series journal's lock: numbered, its issuer and client frozen, its file made and kept as `invoices/<number>.pdf` (never over a file already there), the record kept as `invoices/<number>.toml` naming that file with its SHA-256 (never over another file: of two numbers that make one file name, "F:1" and "F/1", the second is refused), the journal written; its time marked (a credit note's cancelled time freed); a planned line in the project's budget, tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>` and due on its due day (none for a credit note); the draft taken out. A step that fails undoes those before it. **Make the invoice** on the Projects page stays one click: a draft of the project's time, issued at once; refused, it says what is missing and leaves no draft behind (`crates/sioul-app/src/projects.rs`, `make_invoice`).
- **Numbers** (`series.rs`): a pattern and its reset make a sequence. A pattern holds `{YYYY}`, `{YY}`, `{MM}`, and one counter, `{N}` or `{N:k}` (k digits at least), the rest as written; `reset = "yearly"` (then the year is in the pattern) or `"never"`. `[invoice] pattern` when set, else the older `prefix`: empty, `{YYYY}-{N:3}` from 1 again each January (2026-001); a prefix of your own and three digits, going on from the last whatever the year (F-041, F-042). Each number is one more than the largest of the series journal, the records (those that no longer read too) and every device's claim (`series::next`). The journal, `invoices/series.toml`, keeps each sequence's last number and its day, written in the same step as the record, under its lock: a record deleted by hand no longer frees its number, and a number of the journal's range with no record here is said ("2026-004 has no record on this device", `series::missing`), never given again. The lease's claim carries each sequence's last number too (`sioul_sync::lease::Claim::series`, from the journal at each renewal; [database.md](database.md#leases-one-computer-at-a-time)), so that a device whose records have not come yet never gives one of them again. Records list in their natural order, 2026-999 before 2026-1000.
- **Credit notes** (`life::credit`): a draft citing the invoice's number and day (BG-3), with all its lines (to cancel it) or some (to correct them); in the invoices' series, unless `credit_pattern` gives them one of their own; no payment terms; a business's credit note carries the payment lines too (escompte, penalties, the €40 indemnity), as France's rules ask of every invoice (BR-FR-05). Issued, it settles what it credits: an invoice credited whole reads "credited", one credited in part has the rest left to pay. It frees for billing again the time of each line it cancels whole, and all of the invoice's time when it credits the whole invoice (a format-1 invoice credited whole: all its time). The budget line is not changed by a credit note.
- **Deposits and final invoices** (`life::deposit`, `cite_deposits`): a deposit invoice (386) bills an amount up front, excluding tax; a final invoice cites the deposit invoices it deducts, by number, day and amount with tax, and its amount due is what is left.
- **Payments**: each `{date, amount, means, movement}`, `movement` the address of the bank line's movement it was matched with, empty when said by hand. **Paid**, on the Projects page or the invoice's card in the agenda, writes a payment of what is left, by transfer, dated today (`life::pay_rest`), and its budget line follows ("Budget", below); **Undo** takes that payment off again; **Not paid** takes off the payments said by hand, never those the bank's matching wrote. **From the bank**: a bank line that the matching joins to an invoice's planned line, when an export is taken in, writes a payment on that invoice (`movements::acts::resolve`: the bank's day and amount, by transfer, the movement's address), once per movement; the window says it in the status line with Undo, which takes it off again, and nothing asks to confirm it ([accounting.md](accounting.md#payments-on-your-invoices)). Payments add up: one smaller than what is left makes the invoice partly paid.
- **Its life written in place** (`invoice::write_life`): an issued record is never written whole again; its payments and the day it was sent are written into its file with `toml_edit`, every other line kept as it is.
- **Format-1 records** (before November 2026, no `format` key) read as issued invoices of kind 380 with time lines (`record::V1`): their SIRET an identifier, their VAT line the franchise's mention (category `E`), or no VAT (`O`) without one. They are never rewritten: their payments and `sent` are written in place, with `paid` for an older Sioul, and they print as they always did, in the window's language. A record that no longer reads still holds its number, and is never written over.
- **Its own language and currency**: an invoice keeps its language (`en` or `fr`: the client profile's, else `[invoice] language`, else the window's) and its currency (the profile's, the settings', the preset's, else euros), prints in them whatever the window speaks, and shows its amounts in its currency in every view (the project's page, the agenda's dated card, `sioul invoices`, the agents), where a euro sign showed before whatever the currency.
- **The page** (`html.rs`) prints the issuer with its mark and identifiers, the client with its identifiers and delivery address, the nature of the operation, the date or period of the service, each line's quantity, unit and unit price, the VAT per rate when liable, the totals, the deposits deducted, the legal lines and how to pay: a draft's preview (`sioul invoices export --html`; the editor's, in phase 3), and the issued file says the same words ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)). A format-1 record keeps no file: the window prints its page to an A4 PDF through Qt's text document and PDF writer, as it always did, in `[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices` (Factures in French).
- **Budget**: the amount due is expected in the project's budget, a planned line on the due day (the terms: the client profile's, the settings', the preset's, else 30 days), tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>`; ticking "Paid" dates it today and makes it real, with "Undo" for ten seconds; unticked, it is planned again on the day it had, its due day when that is not known (`budget::unsettle_line`), where it was in the ledger, so that Paid then not paid leaves the budget as it was.
- **From the command line** (`crates/sioul-cli/src/invoices.rs`): `sioul invoices [list] [--client <words>] [--year <year>] [--status <status>]` (drafts first, then the invoices, newest first, each with where it stands; a record that no longer reads, and a number with no record here, said), `show <number or draft>` (its lines, totals and payments, and for a draft what it still lacks), `export [--year <year>]` (every invoice as CSV, in French with `;` and decimal commas), `export --html <number>` (its page to print) or `export --pdf <number>` (its issued file, copied where invoices go, made again first when gone or changed and this Sioul makes the same bytes; its path), `draft <project>` (a draft of the project's time not billed yet; nothing numbered), `issue <draft>` (you at the keyboard, under the window's rules: one device numbers at a time, never one whose sharing leaves invoices out; its notes said, its file copied where invoices go and its path said, a copy that cannot be written said apart, the invoice issued all the same), `credit <number> [--line <n>]…` (a draft credit note, all lines or those given), `pay <number> [--day <day>] [--amount <amount>] [--means transfer|card|cash|cheque|direct-debit|other]` (a payment received; what is left when no amount is given).
- **Agents**: `invoices` reads drafts and invoices, `draft_invoice` writes drafts only, when you ask; neither issues, sends nor records a payment ([mcp.md](mcp.md)).

### The issued file
Issued, an invoice is a file, written once and kept beside its record ([research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), §15; finding 80 of [research.md](research.md)).

- **A PDF/A-3b, written in Rust** (`pdf.rs`, krilla 0.8.2): A4, 18 mm margins; Liberation Sans regular and bold, embedded and subset (`crates/sioul-core/data/fonts/`: version 2.1.5, byte for byte as released, SIL Open Font License 1.1, its licence beside and in every package with `NOTICES.md`); its colours in sRGB, with sRGB's output intent; its creation day the day of issue, so that the same record makes the same bytes. It says what the page says (`html::current`), word for word, in the invoice's language and currency: a test holds what the layout draws to the page's words, and every word inside its page (`pdf::tests::the_pdf_says_what_the_page_says`). A long invoice goes on as many pages as it takes, the table's head again on each, each page saying which it is; a line longer than a page goes on to the next under the head again, its figures on its first part; a column's head too wide for its column goes on two lines, and a total wider than its column reaches left, its label ending before it; a test holds every word drawn to no other on its line. A character the font lacks (a script other than Latin, Greek or Cyrillic) holds the issue back, said before (`pdf::unprintable`), since a PDF/A never draws a missing glyph; right-to-left writing (Hebrew, Arabic) too (`pdf::right_to_left`), which would print backwards without Unicode's bidirectional ordering; a non-breaking hyphen prints as a hyphen, a narrow no-break space as a no-break space.
- **Its Factur-X XML** (`cii.rs`): Factur-X 1.09.2 / ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 in the EN 16931 profile (`urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017`), UN/CEFACT CII D22B written by Sioul's own serializer on quick-xml, element by element in the schema's order, from the record's own terms, nothing computed again. Attached as `factur-x.xml` (`text/xml`, the relationship *Alternative*: the PDF and the XML say the same invoice, the one relationship Germany accepts), listed in the catalog's `/AF`. The VAT categories: `S` with its rate; `E` with its reason (the French franchise: `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE` and its mention); `AE`, the reverse charge, with both VAT numbers; `O`, not registered, with `VATEX-EU-O`, no rate and neither side's VAT number; `K`, goods to a business in another member state, with `VATEX-EU-IC`, the delivery country and day; `G`, an export, with `VATEX-EU-G`; `Z`. The identifiers: a French SIREN (a SIRET's first nine digits) as the legal registration (scheme 0002), the VAT number (`VA`), a Steuernummer, a UTR or a tax number elsewhere as a tax registration (`FC`), another identifier as the seller's own when it has none of these; France's business process (BT-23: `B1` goods, `S1` services, `M1` both); the account to pay into, the one the QR code names (the first whose IBAN's key holds, else the first): a SEPA transfer (58) when its key holds and the invoice is in euros, else a credit transfer (30) with the number as written (`ProprietaryID`, after the holder's name, in the schema's order), the invoice's number as the payment's reference; the day the supply was completed (BT-72): the last day of the period billed, else the day of issue, none on a deposit invoice, which bills what is not supplied yet (its period, when it says one, as BG-14); the invoices it cites (BG-3, unbounded in the profile's schema): the one a credit note corrects, the deposit invoices a final one deducts; the countries as ISO 3166-1 codes (`countries.rs`: UK as GB, EL as GR, a name in English, French or its own language as its code); the units as UN/ECE Recommendation 20's (`units.rs`).
- **Its XMP** (`pdfa.rs`): the Factur-X properties (`fx:DocumentType` INVOICE, `fx:DocumentFileName`, `fx:Version` 1.0, `fx:ConformanceLevel` EN 16931) and their schema, among the PDF/A extension schemas krilla declares (one `pdfaExtension:schemas` only: a property twice, and no reader takes the XMP). krilla 0.8.2 takes no XMP property of one's own (its issue #209), so the metadata stream krilla wrote is written again in place, its length with it, the cross-reference offsets after it moved by as much: one object changes, the file stays one revision. lopdf, which the design named for this, would rewrite the whole file and bring some twenty crates (ciphers, hashes, a random generator) for one stream; this takes none, and a test reads the table back.
- **Without its XML** (`cii::without`), said before issue as a note, never as a reason not to issue: under the French franchise without a VAT number, since EN 16931's exempt category asks the seller's VAT or tax number (BR-E-02) and the French rule that says which number to give instead, BR-FR-CO-16 of AFNOR's XP Z12-012, is still to be read (design question 1); and an invoice whose rules lack your VAT or tax number, the client's VAT number (a reverse charge, a delivery to another member state), an identifier of yours, your country or the client's as a code, a line's unit or price the standard takes, or (a deposit for goods to another member state) the period it covers; lines outside the scope of VAT beside others, which EN 16931 keeps apart. The PDF is then a PDF/A-3 without attachment: a complete invoice on paper, as French law asks of small businesses until 1 September 2027.
- **The EPC QR code** (`epc.rs`; EPC069-12 version 3.1): on euro invoices with an IBAN whose key holds, never on a credit note: version `002`, UTF-8, `SCT`, the BIC when given, the account holder's name (else yours, at most 70 characters), the IBAN, the amount due, the invoice's number as the remittance; error correction M, version 13 at most, 331 bytes at most; drawn as squares (qrcodegen's modules, a run of dark ones one rectangle), 25 mm with its quiet zone, at the right of the payment details, "Scan to pay by transfer" under it. `[invoice] qr = false` leaves it out. An IBAN whose key fails is said before issue.
- **Kept** (`kept.rs`): written in the same step as the record and its number, under the journal's lock. The record is the commit: the file is written first under a name the sharing leaves alone (`<number>.pdf.new`), takes its name once the record is written, and is taken away when a step after it fails. An issue stopped halfway (a crash, a phone closing the app) never blocks the next number: before numbering, the device that numbers gives its name to a waiting file whose record came with its SHA-256, and sets aside in `invoices/leftovers/` any other waiting file and any kept file no record holds, said once in the status line (`kept::tidy`). The record names its file (`file`, which must be its number's, `<number>.pdf`, else nothing is handed out) with its SHA-256 (`file_sha256`). It travels with the records, in the sharing's part for drafts and invoices ([database.md](database.md)). **Print again**, issuing in the window or the terminal, and `sioul invoices export --pdf` copy it byte for byte where invoices go (`[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices`), so that a PDF reader saving into its copy never changes the file kept; a copy there already the same is left as it is. A kept file gone or changed is made again from the frozen record (the QR code on or off, as it was) and kept again when this Sioul makes the bytes issued, a changed one first set aside in `leftovers/`; when it cannot (another version of Sioul made them), nothing is handed out, and the sentence says where the record and the file are (`kept::issued`). When the copy where invoices go cannot be written, the invoice is issued all the same, and the status line says so ("Print again once that folder is there"). Format-1 records keep no file: their page is printed through Qt as before.
- **Checked in CI** (`.github/workflows/e-invoices.yml`, on pushes to main touching what goes into an invoice's file, and by hand, as the other workflows run): sioul-core's ignored test `invoice::samples::write_fixtures` writes one invoice per preset and VAT scheme, a credit note, a deposit invoice and the final one, an account whose IBAN's key fails, three pages of lines, a line longer than a page (`valid/`), one made wrong on purpose with the rule it breaks beside it (its totals a cent off, BR-CO-15, `invalid/`), and the French franchise without a VAT number (`without-xml/`). Mustang CLI 2.26.0 (its jar's SHA-256 pinned) reads each with veraPDF, the EN 16931 schematron and France's rules of XP Z12-012 inside, and `tools/einvoice/validate.sh` expects each part of its report valid and no word from the French rules on a good fixture, the broken one invalid for its own rule, and the PDF/A-3b of the third valid; it lists every warning. The same runs offline with Mustang's jar and Java (the script's comment says how). No invoice goes to an online validator.

### Countries: presets as data
- **One file per country**, `crates/sioul-core/data/invoice-presets/*.toml`, built into Sioul (`presets.rs`): France (`fr`), Germany (`de`), Belgium (`be`), the United Kingdom (`uk`), Switzerland (`ch`), and `elsewhere` for any country (no legal claims: every identifier and line your own). Spain (`es`) and Italy (`it`) are listed as not supported, each with why (certified, chained invoicing software, VERI\*FACTU; the state's exchange system, SdI): issuing under them is refused with that sentence, rather than a template offered that would be illegal there. No country has a code path of its own.
- **What a preset says**: its labels in English and French; its legal forms (and the mark a form prints after the name), its income regimes; the identifier kinds its invoices carry and how each is checked; what is required or recommended before issue (of the issuer or the client; for business, consumer or public clients; where the client is, at home, in another EU member state or abroad; for goods or services; under which scheme and legal form; from or until a day), with the sentence said when it is missing; its VAT schemes, the franchise's category, reason code and dated mention, its dated rates, exempt lines, the reverse charge; its legal lines with their EN 16931 subject codes, each with its source in the file's comments; its late-payment terms, its payment terms, its activities, thresholds and contribution rates, each dated (the books read them from phase 4).
- **Three settings apart**: the legal form (France: an entrepreneur individuel, micro-entrepreneur included, or EURL, SASU, SARL, SAS, SA; the other presets: a sole trader or a company), the income regime for the books (France: micro or réel), and the VAT scheme, dated (`none`, not registered; `franchise`, the small-business exemption with its mention; `liable`), since none implies another: an EI micro-entrepreneur liable for VAT prints « EI » and VAT per rate; a company under the franchise prints the franchise's mention, its form and its capital. Sioul prints « EI » after a French entrepreneur individuel's name (C. com. R526-27), whatever the VAT, unless the name says it already, at its start, at its end, or in words (`life::marked_name`). A French company's capital ("SASU au capital de 1 000 €") and its RCS city are required.
- **VAT by the scheme of the day of issue** (`life::default_tax`): not registered, `O`; the franchise, `E` with its mention (France: "TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI", reason code `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE`), and no VAT ever shown (art. 293 E CGI); liable, `S` at the preset's standard rate, your own rate, category or written exemption kept on a line. A service to a business in another member state takes the reverse charge whatever the scheme, `AE` with `VATEX-EU-AE`, "Autoliquidation" or "Reverse charge", and asks for both VAT numbers. The rates shipped (France 20, 10, 5.5 and 2.1 %; Germany 19 and 7 %; Belgium 21, 12 and 6 %; the United Kingdom 20, 5 and 0 %; Switzerland 8.1, 2.6 and 3.8 %) were not part of the research, but for Switzerland's 8.1 %: they are the rates as known in mid-2026, each file says so, and they are to be checked.
- **France's lines**: for business and public clients only, "Escompte pour paiement anticipé : néant.", the late-payment penalties at the rate kept with the invoice (by default the ECB's main refinancing rate in force on 1 January or 1 July, plus 10 points: 12.40 % for an invoice of the second half of 2026), and the €40 recovery indemnity (C. com. ## Budgets at their pace
A budget's verdict no longer compares a projection made only of what is scheduled with its target, which said "short" on the 3rd of every month. What is known in advance (presets, planned lines) counts in full; the rest, what simply happens, is weighed against the part of the period gone: ten days into thirty, a third of what the period still needs should have come in (or no more than a third of what it may spend should be spent). Within the one tolerance of every verdict, 5 % of what the period moves, it is on track ([accounting.md](accounting.md), "On track, without assuming money flows evenly").

The end of the period is estimated:
- with fewer than three past periods: the difference of today carried to the end (no extrapolation of three days into a month);
- with three or more: what is known, plus the median past period's unscheduled movements for the part left; the worst and best past periods give a range (the 10th and 90th percentiles from ten periods on).

A budget opens on its ledger (lines and recurring movements of the period, newest first), its balance by day, week, month or year (planned movements dotted), and "Add a movement": once, or each month or year (`[[preset]]`, written in place, comments kept).
