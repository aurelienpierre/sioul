# Projects, time and invoices

A project is any matter you follow, one list for all of them (`sioul-projects.toml` in your notes folder, [notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md)): the umbrella over its tasks (REFID), events, mail (its routes), notes, time and budget lines. Projects were once "cases" beside "projects"; they are one thing, with one name, and "for a client" is a choice in its form. What was written under the old name still reads ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#its-first-name)). Made, renamed, given its routes and taken out from the Projects page (its form, "Its mail"); taken out, its tasks, notes, mail and time stay where they are. The Porch's settings only choose which projects have a lane there (Porch ⚙, "Projects shown here"). A project **for a client** (`kind = "project"`) has a client, an hourly rate and the budget its invoices are expected in, and its time can be billed.

```toml
[[project]]
id = "lumen"
title = "Studio Lumen — website"
kind = "project"
client = "Studio Lumen"        # or "sioul:contact/<UID>": its client profile, else the card's postal address
rate = 60.0                    # else [invoice] rate
budget = "work"                # invoices expected there until paid
area = "work"                  # what it is for: work, admin, leisure, joined by "+"
```

## What a project is for {#what-a-project-is-for}
A project is for work, your admin or leisure, any of them together, as an address, a budget, a site or a task is ([areas.md](areas.md)): the three switches used everywhere (`AreaSwitches.qml`) in its form, under "What it is for". Written in its record in the areas' words, `work`, `admin`, `leisure` joined by `+` (`area = "admin+leisure"`); `personal`, an older Sioul's one box "Yours, outside work", reads as admin and leisure; a word no Sioul reads is never written. Unsaid, it is work's, a client's project or not, and its form says so under the switches: "Nothing ticked: the Projects and Time pages count it as work." (`Project::area_of`, as `mailbox::area_of` and `Budget::area_of` say an address's and a budget's).

- **The pages**: the Projects page and the Time page show a project while its area meets their filter's ticks ([areas.md](areas.md#a-pages-filter-by-area)).
- **Its tasks**: a task with no area of its own takes its projects' (`areas::TaskAreas`, beside its categories'), when the project says one, or, unsaid, when it has a client (work's); an unsaid project without a client gives its tasks none, so that their categories decide, else your admin, as before projects had areas, and the plan lays them where it did (`Project::gives_its_tasks_an_area`).
- **Between your devices**: the field travels with the project's record (the part "projects", field by field), a plain string, which a Sioul from before the three switches reads as a word it does not know: unsaid there, its tasks by their categories, its page by its older rule, until that device is updated; its form there shows one box and, saved, writes "personal" or nothing (`share::tests::a_project_s_area_travels_with_its_record`).

## A project's page (Projects, Ctrl+8)
The list shows the projects for what its filter ticks, Mail's filter by area: work's, admin's or leisure's as the hours say on this device, everything one tick away, "As the hours say" to come back; the ticks are this device's, shared with the Time page, in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/projects-view.toml` ([areas.md](areas.md#a-pages-filter-by-area); `projects::page_rows`, `ProjectsPage.qml`). No cover and no "Show anyway": the filter says what shows, at any hour, asleep too. A project opened from elsewhere (a link, the Time page's "Make the invoice") opens whatever the ticks.
- **One line of time**: what is coming first (dates asked of its open tasks, events), then what happened, newest first (tasks done, mail by its routes or tied by hand, notes of its record or tied to it, time noted, invoices). Mail comes by its routes on sender and subject (matched again each time), by being tied to the project (when it arrived, or by hand), and by conversation: the whole of a conversation one of its messages belongs to ([notes-folder.md](notes-folder.md#routes)). Each message is checked as the Porch checks it, each time the page is shown (`porch::Gate`, which reads the file and asks `porch::admission`, the function the Porch's own lanes use): what the Porch sets aside (a blocked sender, forged, a borrowed name, spam no route protects, hostile) never shows, whether a route, a tie Sioul made or a conversation brings it, and a conversation is followed through the messages let in only, so a forged reply joins nothing; mail nothing authenticates comes by no route on its sender's address or domain, and says "whose address could not be verified"; a message you tied by hand stays, as you tied it. The agents' `list_projects` reads the same page.
- **Its tasks** as a board, a list or a calendar: the Tasks page, filtered to the project.
- **"Note time"**: a meeting, a call, work away from the timer; for a project alone or a task.
- **"Make the invoice"**, when billable time waits: the draft of that time, opened in Money ▸ Invoices' editor, where it is checked, changed and issued. A project with a draft already gets back the draft that holds its time (else its newest), and only time that no draft holds is added to it: each line of that time joins the draft's line of time of the same words and hourly price, its minutes and its sessions added to it, else it is a line of its own (`life::add_time`), so that the project's "to bill", which rounds each label's time once, and its draft agree to the cent (the second review of phase 3, R15). The time taken out of one of the project's drafts is left out (`Invoice::set_aside`): only that draft's own **Put back** gives it its lines again ([The editor](#the-editor)). The status line says what was added ("2 lines of time added to the draft (1 h 30)."). Since the sessions of every draft are left out, the same hours are never in two drafts, nor put back by **Make the invoice** in the draft they were taken out of (`life::untaken`, which `sioul invoices draft` and the agents' `draft_invoice` ask too, and `life::draft_of_project`; `projects::make_invoice`, `time_draft`; the review of phase 3, F9; "Invoices in the window", below).
- **Its client's profile**, for work for a client: one line ("Client profile: A business · France · 904 123 452"), or "No client profile yet: invoices bill it as a business, from its card.", with **Client profile…** (**Make its client profile…** when there is none: "Clients", below). Each of its invoices opens its pane in Money ▸ Invoices.

## Time (Ctrl+9)
The Projects page's filter, its ticks the same: time on a project whose area is not ticked folds, out of the bars, the projects' lines and the stretches; with no project, a stretch counts by its task's area, and time noted on nothing, or on a project gone, always shows (`timereport::area_of`). One project chosen shows whatever the ticks, the filter put away. When the filter folds all the period holds: "Nothing noted for what is ticked." (`projects::time`, `TimePage.qml`).

A week, a month or a year: a bar per day (per month over a year) stacked by project, each project's hours and what is left to bill, in hours and in money at its rate, then each stretch of time, newest first. The money left to bill is counted as the invoice made of that time counts it, each of its lines rounded to the cent, then added up (`life::time_amount`, the project's page too), so that the two never differ by a cent (the review of phase 3, F18); for the same reason, time added later to a draft joins its line of the same words and hourly price rather than making a second line rounded apart (`life::add_time`; the second review of phase 3, R15). While it shows, the page reads again when an invoice is issued, paid or otherwise changed, here or elsewhere (`invoices_changed`, `TimePage.qml`), so that the time billed and to bill follow.
- **Billed or not, task by task**: a task's form says "Billed": as its project says (work for a client is billed), its time is billed, or not billed (`X-SIOUL-BILLABLE` in the task, kept by CalDAV servers; greyed on Google Tasks).
- **As a spreadsheet**: the export button on the Time page writes a project's billable time as CSV: what (a line per task, or per note), hours, hourly rate, amount, then the total; this week, last week, this month, last month, all time, or from one day to another. In French, `;` between fields and a decimal comma, as French spreadsheets read them. Nothing else goes in: the full invoice is the invoice's (below). Time comes from the focus timer (a task's time counts for its project: the first of its projects that is for a client, else its first project) and from what is noted by hand (`$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/time/<month>.toml`, sessions with `project`, `unbilled`, `invoice`: the number that billed it, a mark made again from the issued records before each issue, "Invoices" below). Any stretch not billed, timed or noted by hand, changes with a click: its task, its project, its day, from when to when, what it was (`qml/TimeDialog.qml`; moved to another month, it is written there first); its right click takes it out. Billed time stays as it was billed.

## Invoices
Invoices, credit notes and deposit invoices you issue (`crates/sioul-core/src/invoice/`: `record.rs`, `life.rs`, `series.rs`, `totals.rs`, `presets.rs`, `ids.rs`, `html.rs`, `edit.rs`; the issued file in `pdf.rs`, `pdfa.rs`, `cii.rs`, `epc.rs` and `kept.rs`; client profiles in `clients.rs`; the window's part in `crates/sioul-app/src/invoices.rs` and `qml/InvoicesTab.qml`, `InvoiceEditor.qml`, `InvoicePaidDialog.qml`, `InvoiceCreditDialog.qml`, `InvoiceDepositDialog.qml`, `ClientProfileDialog.qml`, `InvoiceSettings.qml`). The invoicing's phase 1 (November 2026) built the model and its life, its phase 2 the issued file, its phase 3 the window (Money ▸ Invoices, the editor, Settings ▸ Invoices by country, client profiles in Contacts and Projects) and the record's own hash, and its phase 4 the books made from it ("The books", below), all described here. What the design rests on: [research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), findings 76 to 82 of [research.md](research.md).

- **One record, in EN 16931's terms** (`record.rs`, `format = 2`): each field is one of the European standard's business terms (BT-xx), so that one record prints its page in any language and becomes an electronic invoice without translation. Its kind is UNTDID 1001's code (BT-3): 380 an invoice, 381 a credit note, 386 a deposit invoice. It holds its series and number, its issue day, the service's day or period (`period_from`, `period_to`: the first and last days of the time billed, the days given, else the day of issue, and none on a deposit invoice, whose service is still to come; service-public.gouv.fr, sheet F31808, and ann. II CGI art. 242 nonies A I-10°), its due day and terms, its currency (ISO 4217) and language, its project, its client as chosen (`client_ref`), the issuer and the client frozen as they were at issue, its lines, its totals, the invoice it corrects (BG-3), the deposit invoices it deducts, the client's purchase order (BT-13), its notes with their subject codes (UNTDID 4451) and the late-payment rate it printed (in basis points); `file` and `file_sha256`, the issued file kept beside it and its SHA-256 ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)); `record_sha256` stays empty, since a record cannot hold the hash of its own bytes: that hash is kept with its number in the series journal of the device that issued it ("Changed outside Sioul", in [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window)). Its payments and the day it was sent are not in it: they are its life, kept apart ("Its life, outside the record", below).
- **Where it stands is read, never typed** (`Invoice::status`, `left`, `credited`, `left_to_credit`): draft, issued, sent, partly paid, paid, credited, from its number, the day it was sent, its payments and the credit notes that cite it. A credit note has a status of its own, "credits invoice 2026-005" (`Status::CreditNote`): it is never paid nor unpaid; while it has money to pay back, it reads "to pay back" (`Status::ToPayBack`, "credits invoice 2026-005 · €120.00 to pay back": "Credit notes", below). Partly paid needs a payment: an invoice credited in part and not paid is issued (or sent), with less left to pay. An invoice whose deposits cover it all, nothing left due, reads paid. Nothing is "overdue": what is unpaid is "not paid yet".
- **Lines** (BG-25): a description and details; a quantity in thousandths of a UN/ECE unit (Recommendation 20: `HUR`, `MIN`, `DAY`, `C62`, `MON`, `KMT`, `LS`); a unit price in cents for `base` units (BT-149); a discount; a VAT category (UNTDID 5305: `S`, `Z`, `E`, `AE`, `K`, `G`, `O`) and its rate in basis points; an exemption's reason; goods or a service; the amount; the time it bills, by session. Each line has an id of its own in its draft (`id`, ten letters and digits, given once by `life::line_ids` and kept at issue), by which the editor names it, never by its place ([The editor](#the-editor)); a line whose file gives it none (a draft of before line ids) is named as it is read, in memory, from the draft's id and the line's place, alike on every device, and that name is kept with the first real change (`invoice::read_draft`; the second review of phase 3, R3). **Lines from time** come as before, one per task (or per note, for time noted without a task), in the order first worked on, as minutes at the project's hourly price with a base of 60, so that 25 minutes at €60 an hour make €25.00 exactly, as format 1 computed it. **The time billed is what the issued records list** (`life::billed`, `with_billing`): a session an invoice lists, and no credit note freed since, is billed by it whatever its own mark says; the sessions' `invoice` marks are a cache, made again from the records before each issue (`life::reconcile_marks`), so that a crash between a record and its marks never lets the same time be billed twice.
- **Totals** (`totals.rs`), computed once in integer cents as EN 16931 checks them (BR-CO-10 to 17): each line to the cent, half away from zero (Factur-X §7.1.8); per category and rate its base and its tax; then without tax, the tax, with tax, the deposits deducted (BT-113) and the amount due (BT-115). No float in a sum: quantities are thousandths, rates basis points; a project's rate, still a float in `sioul-projects.toml`, becomes cents at that boundary.
- **Drafts** in `$XDG_DATA_HOME/sioul/invoices/drafts/<id>.toml`, beside the records, so that they travel with them: a draft has no number, and its id is when it was made and six letters (`20261116-143005-k3x9qa`), never a number: its file's name, letters, digits and dashes only, never what the file says (`draft_id_ok`). It changes freely (`life::change_draft`, which the editor calls with each change, `edit::save`: [The editor](#the-editor)), its one file read, changed and written in one step under the file's lock, the one the sharing takes to write it (`invoice::with_draft_lock`, `life::change_draft_read`; `draft_in` reads that one file, never the folder; the second review of phase 3, R4), and reading it never writes it (R3): `life::refresh` takes the issuer as the settings say it, each line's tax where it is due ("Where VAT is due", below), the legal lines the preset prints for this client, the late-payment rate, the due day and the totals, and, when nothing says the service's day (no time billed, no day given), the day of issue, shown on the draft and printed (`period_default`; issued, the issue's own day), never on a deposit invoice; your own notes stay; an invoice's cited deposits are cited again as they are now (`life::recite`, as at issue; R8). Its client is made again from its profile, or its contact's card, until it is issued (`life::refresh_client`: at issue, and in `sioul invoices show`), so that a profile changed meanwhile counts; a client written as a name alone, or whose profile and card are gone, stays as the draft has it, and a credit note keeps the credited invoice's client as it was. A sync app's conflicted copy of a draft ("<id> (conflicted copy …)") is listed as a draft of its own, its id made of its file's name, and says which draft it copies (`Invoice::copy_of`), never hidden; saved, it keeps that id, and its file of before goes. A draft keeps the time taken out of it (`set_aside`: the sessions of each line of time taken out), which **Make the invoice**, `sioul invoices draft` and the agents' `draft_invoice` never take while the draft is there (`life::untaken`), and which the editor's **Put back** gives the draft again ([The editor](#the-editor); R15); a deposit invoice made from a draft names it (`deposit_of`), which its **Final invoice…** opens again ("Deposits and final invoices", below). A draft written by a newer Sioul (a later `format`) is read where it reads, never written here: `change_draft` and `save_draft` refuse it ("…: written by a newer Sioul; update Sioul on this device to change it."), `issue` refuses it (`invoice-draft-newer`), the editor shows it to be read only, saying why (the review of phase 3, F13), and the window's **Discard** refuses it too, its row's menu not offering it, since the removal would travel and nothing here could write it back as it was (`invoices::discard`; the second review of phase 3, R13). `sioul invoices discard <draft>` refuses a newer Sioul's draft too, with the same sentence. A draft is made from a project's time not billed yet (`life::from_time`: a project's **Make the invoice**, `sioul invoices draft`, the agents' `draft_invoice`, each of the time `life::untaken` leaves: none that a draft holds, none set aside in one of the project's drafts), for a client (`life::for_client`: a contact's **Bill this client**), blank (**New**), as a credit note (`life::credit`: **Credit note…**), as a deposit invoice (`life::deposit`: **Deposit invoice…**) or as a final invoice deducting a deposit (`life::cite_deposits`: **Final invoice…**), each in [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window).
- **Checked before issue** (`life::checks`): what the preset asks and the draft lacks, in plain words and in the invoice's language; and what EN 16931 refuses in the issued file's data: a unit price below zero (BR-27: a discount or a credit note says less), a unit outside Sioul's Recommendation 20 codes (a word such as "hrs" or "jours" is read as its code, by the agents' `draft_invoice` too), a country that is none (your own, the client's, the delivery's: by its code or its name, in the one table of countries, `clients::country`), characters the font cannot print and right-to-left writing. Each line's tax must fit the VAT scheme in force on the day of issue (the review of phase 3, F3): a line that charges VAT under the franchise or with no scheme is refused, since VAT shown on an invoice is owed (CGI, art. 283-3; `invoice-check-line-vat-unowed`), and so is VAT at a rate without its rate (EN 16931, BR-S-05; `invoice-check-line-rate-missing`); a credit note keeps the tax its invoice was billed with. A VAT scheme's day that does not read as a day (`config::read_day`) counts for none, so it holds the issue back (`invoice-check-scheme-day`, F19), as do numbers asked to start again from 1 each year without the year in them (`invoice-check-reset-year`, F20). A cited deposit of another client or currency, or one with nothing left after its credit notes, is refused, and a deposit of this client not deducted yet is said as recommended ("Deposits and final invoices", below); a credit note on a deposit invoice that a live final invoice deducts is refused, with the way out ("Deposit invoice 2026-010 is deducted by invoice 2026-020: credit invoice 2026-020 instead, or cancel it first.", `invoice-check-credit-deposit-deducted`; the second review of phase 3, R7; "Credit notes", below). The editor says them before issue, each in a sentence under the totals, the required ones first, and **Issue** waits until none of those is left, and while a number typed in the page does not read (R1); what is recommended and not required (no Factur-X data, an IBAN whose key fails…) and the notes are said there too, and again in the status line as it issues ([The editor](#the-editor)). Required items refuse the issue; recommended ones are said only ("The client's SIREN: asked on invoices to French businesses from September 2027."). Identifiers are checked by their keys (`ids.rs`: a SIREN and a SIRET by Luhn, La Poste's own rule too; a VAT number by its own country, the two letters it starts with, never by the issuer's: a French one by its key, another member state's, the United Kingdom's and a Swiss UID by their form, an unknown prefix taken as written, `ids::vat_valid`). An identifier counts toward what is required only when the invoice prints it (`printed`, true unless set otherwise). « en cours d'attribution » as the SIRET, or an Other identifier saying « SIRET en cours d'attribution », stands for it while it is being given (service-public F31808), and prints « SIRET en cours d'attribution ». The checks take the issued records too: a credit note is checked against what is left to credit ("Credit notes", below). A table that issued invoices before may now be refused where its preset requires what it lacks (your SIREN; the address of a French business client, which every client without a profile now is; the country of a client whose address names none Sioul knows; your legal form, "Older tables" below): a change of behaviour, said here.
- **Issued all or nothing** (`life::issue`): refused, in words, on a device whose sharing is on but leaves out the `drafts` part, which carries the invoices ("Turn on Drafts and invoices in Sharing to issue invoices on this device, or issue them on the device that has it."), since the others' numbers may not be known there; refused, the draft taken out, when an issued record names that draft already (a crash between the record and the draft's removal: `invoice-draft-issued`), and refused for a sync app's copy of a draft issued since, kept for you to read (`invoice-draft-copy-issued`); refused for a draft a newer Sioul wrote (`invoice-draft-newer`); its client made again from its profile or card (`life::refresh_client`, through `Steps::clients`; a credit note's is the credited invoice's, copied as it was) and an invoice's deposits cited again as they are then, what is left of each after its credit notes (`life::recite`; a credit note keeps the amounts of the invoice it cancels, R7), then checked; refused when some of its time was billed meanwhile, as the records say it (the sessions' marks made again from them first). Then, under the numbering's lock: numbered, its issuer frozen as the settings say it that day and its client as made then, its file made and kept as `invoices/<number>.pdf` (never over a file already there), the record kept as `invoices/<number>.toml` naming that file with its SHA-256 (never over another file: of two numbers that make one file name, "F:1" and "F/1", the second is refused), this device's series journal written, with the SHA-256 of the record's bytes as they were written beside its number (`Journal::records`, since phase 3); its time marked (a credit note's cancelled time freed); for an invoice in euros, the ledger's money, a planned line in the project's budget, tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>` and due on its due day (an invoice in another currency is expected in no budget, and no bank line in euros ever joins it); for a credit note, the credited invoice's planned line lowered by its amount instead, taken out when nothing is left of it (`budget::lower_planned`; a line already paid stays as it is); the draft taken out. A step that fails undoes those before it, a lowered line put back too (`budget::restore_lowered`), and its number taken out of this device's journal with its record's hash, under the numbering's lock, only while the journal still says it (`life::forget_number`): a number another process gave meanwhile stays, and the gap is said. In the window, **Issue** is the editor's alone: **Make the invoice** on the Projects page makes the draft and opens it there, and issues nothing (`crates/sioul-app/src/projects.rs`, `make_invoice`; until 17 November 2026 it issued in the same click, and a draft it could not issue was taken out); a refused issue keeps the draft, the reason said beside **Issue** and in the status line. The window asks the issue for the draft as the editor read it (`life::issue_as_read`, with the editor's revision, the file's lock held from the look to the issue; the second review of phase 3, R6): when the draft's file moved since (another device's change brought by the sharing, the command line), nothing is numbered (`NotIssued::Moved`), the editor reads the draft again and says so ("This draft changed elsewhere since it was read: it is read again here; look at it, then issue.", `invoice-draft-moved`), so that what is numbered is what was shown; `sioul invoices issue` reads the draft's revision and issues in one step.
- **Numbers** (`series.rs`): a pattern and its reset make a sequence. A pattern holds `{YYYY}`, `{YY}`, `{MM}`, and one counter, `{N}` or `{N:k}` (k digits at least), the rest as written; `reset = "yearly"` (then the year is in the pattern) or `"never"`. `[invoice] pattern` when set, else the older `prefix`: empty, `{YYYY}-{N:3}` from 1 again each January (2026-001); a prefix of your own and three digits, going on from the last whatever the year (F-041, F-042). A `reset` written applies to that usual pattern too (`life::pattern_of`), so that the reset Settings ▸ Invoices shows is the one the numbering follows; from 1 each year is refused, in words, for a prefix without the year (`Pattern::with_reset`, `invoice-check-reset-year`; the review of phase 3, F20). Each number is one more than the largest of the series journal, the records (those that no longer read too) and every device's claim (`series::next`). A counter that never starts again in a number with its year (`reset = "never"` with `{YYYY}` or `{YY}`) goes on from the last number issued, never from the largest of every year (the second review of phase 3, R12): the counter of the latest record by its day, and never below this year's own largest (its records', the journal's and the claims' for this year's yearly sequence), so that no number is given twice; "never from 1 again" chosen after 2025-041 and 2026-016 gives 2026-017, not 2026-042, or 2027-017 when the next invoice is issued the year after. Without the year in the number, as before, one more than the largest (F-041, F-042). The journal keeps each sequence's last number and its day, each device in its own file, `invoices/series/<device>.toml`, which no other device writes (so the sync never makes a conflicted copy of it, and an older Sioul, which lists only the folder's top files, never takes it for an invoice), read as the largest of every device's (the single `invoices/series.toml` of phase 1's first builds is read too); written in the same step as the record, under the numbering's lock: a record deleted by hand no longer frees its number, and a number of the journal's range with no record here is said ("2026-004 has no record on this device"; when the sequence's numbers hold a month, or a year it never restarts on, by its counter, "F2026{MM}-{N:3}: number 4 has no record on this device", `series::missing`), never given again. The lease's claim carries each sequence's last number too (`sioul_sync::lease::Claim::series`, from the journals at each renewal, right after each issue, the window's as the command line's, and when the window closes, `lease::close_with`; [database.md](database.md#leases-one-computer-at-a-time)), so that a device whose records have not come yet never gives one of them again. The window refuses to number when sharing is set up but its key cannot be read (a keyring locked: the others' claims cannot be read either), as the command line does ("Your sharing folder cannot be written now…", `invoice-unchecked`). Records list in their natural order, 2026-999 before 2026-1000.
- **Credit notes** (`life::credit`): a draft citing the invoice's number and day (BG-3), with all its lines (to cancel it) or some (to correct them), each line keeping the tax it was billed with, its exemption's words too (`Line::own_tax`), should the scheme have changed since; in the invoices' series, unless `credit_pattern` gives them one of their own; no payment terms; a business's credit note carries the payment lines too (escompte, penalties, the €40 indemnity), as France's rules ask of every invoice (BR-FR-05). Never beyond what is left to credit: a credit note for more than the invoice's amount less the credit notes issued for it is refused before issue, naming them (`Invoice::left_to_credit`, a required check), so that an invoice is never credited twice. A deposit invoice that a live final invoice deducts is credited through that invoice: a credit note on the deposit itself is refused before issue by a required check that says the way out ("Deposit invoice 2026-010 is deducted by invoice 2026-020: credit invoice 2026-020 instead, or cancel it first.", `invoice-check-credit-deposit-deducted`, from `life::deducted_by`), since the final invoice would go on deducting an amount the deposit no longer bills, and could no longer be cancelled whole (the second review of phase 3, R7); in the window **Credit note…** on such a deposit answers that sentence at once and makes no draft (`invoices::credit`), while a draft made from the command line waits at **Issue**. Issued, it settles what it credits: an invoice credited whole reads "credited", one credited in part has the rest left to pay. It reads "credits invoice 2026-005", never paid nor unpaid. When the invoice it credits was paid beyond what it now asks (its amount less every credit note), the credit note has that money to pay back (`Invoice::pay_back_share`: what was paid beyond, shared among the invoice's credit notes in their numbers' order, each up to its own amount; `to_pay_back`, less what it paid back): it reads "to pay back €120.00" (`Status::ToPayBack`) and takes **Paid back**, a box on its row in the project's page (`InvoiceRow::pay_back`, `pay_rest`), or amounts paid back (**Paid back…** in its pane, with the day, the means and the amount: [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window); `sioul invoices pay <credit note> --amount`; both `add_payment`), never more than is left to pay back; with nothing to pay back, its row has no box, and a payment on it is refused in words (`invoice-nothing-to-pay-back`). Money paid back is a negative receipt on its day (`life::received`, "Payments", below). It frees for billing again the time of each line it cancels whole, and all of the invoice's time when it credits the whole invoice (a format-1 invoice, whose lines hold no time of their own: all its time when credited whole; for a line credited alone, the sessions of that line's name among the time noted, which `life::credit` takes). Issuing it lowers the credited invoice's planned budget line by its amount, and takes the line out when nothing is left of it (above, "Issued all or nothing"), so that the client's next transfer is never paired with a credited invoice.
- **Deposits and final invoices** (`life::deposit`, `cite_deposits`, `deposits_open`, `deposit_left`, `same_client`; in the window, **Deposit invoice…** and **Final invoice…**: [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window)): a deposit invoice (386) bills an amount up front, excluding tax, and names the draft it was made from (`deposit_of`); a final invoice cites the deposit invoices it deducts, by number, day and what is left of each with tax after its credit notes (`deposit_left`: its amount with tax less what its credit notes credit), and its amount due is what is left. A deposit invoice prints no service day: the service is still to come. A final invoice deducts only deposits of its own client (`same_client`: the reference the client was chosen by, else the first line of its name) and its own currency: a cited deposit of another client or currency is refused by a required check (`invoice-check-deposit-other`), and so is one its credit notes gave back whole (`invoice-check-deposit-credited`); a change of the draft's client or currency unticks such deposits, and the status line says so ("Deposit invoice 2026-010 is no longer deducted: it bills another client or another currency.", `edit::Said::DepositsLeft`; the review of phase 3, F4). A draft invoice's cited deposits are cited again as they are now at each change of the draft (`life::change_draft_read`), each time the editor shows it (`invoices::draft_view`) and at issue (`life::recite`), so that the editor, the preview and the issue deduct the same amount (the second review of phase 3, R8); `recite` is for invoices only, a credit note keeping the amounts of the invoice it cancels (R7). A deposit is deducted by one final invoice at a time: a final invoice citing a deposit that another final invoice, not cancelled whole, deducts already is refused by a required check (`invoice-check-deposit-taken`); a credit note that cancels a final invoice whole deducts its deposits as the invoice did, and so gives them back, to be deducted again; while a live final invoice deducts a deposit, a credit note on the deposit itself is refused ("Credit notes", above). The editor offers a final invoice the issued deposit invoices it may deduct (`life::deposits_open`: of its client, in its currency, something left of them after their credit notes, deducted by no live final invoice), each to tick; a draft invoice that leaves one of them unticked says so, recommended and not required ("Deposit invoice 2026-010 of this client is not deducted yet: tick it under Deposits if this invoice is the final one.", `invoice-check-deposit-undeducted`; F10). No command or agent's tool makes either.
- **Payments**, as read (`Payment`: `{id, date, amount, means, line, fee}`; `line` the key of the bank line it came from, or that was found for it when it was said by hand, `movements::line_key`; `fee` what a processor kept of it), each counted once, whatever the devices did ("Its life, outside the record", below). A payment with an amount is never more than is left to pay, "Paid" aside (`life::add_payment` refuses it, in words); on a credit note, it is money paid back ("Credit notes", above). **Paid**, on the Projects page or the invoice's card in the agenda, says "Paid" on that day, by transfer (`life::pay_rest`, a `[[settled]]` entry): it covers what the other payments leave, read each time, so that Paid said on two devices, or before or after the bank's line comes, counts once; its budget line follows ("Budget", below); **Undo** takes it back; **Not paid** takes back everything said by hand (payments with an amount, "Paid", a format-1 record's own `paid`), never what the bank's lines paid (`life::unpay`). **Paid…**, in an invoice's pane in Money ▸ Invoices, says a payment with its day, its means and its amount (`life::add_payment`; [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window)). **From the bank**: a bank line that the matching joins to the planned lines of invoices, when an export is taken in, pays them (`movements::acts::resolve`, `life::take_bank_line`): one invoice, or several when one transfer pays them together, split across them, the oldest first, each up to what the bank's lines and the payments said in cash left it, by transfer, and what remains is said as paid beyond what they asked; when read, a payment said by hand with room for it takes the line in as its evidence ("Its life, outside the record", below). What the client paid counts, on the day it was paid: when a processor's export says them, the movement's gross on its day paid at the processor (`Movement::gross_or_net`, `paid_day`), its fees being the payment's fee; when only the net is known, a line through a processor (PayPal, Stripe: the account's kind, else the line's label) short of what is owed by at most the processor's usual fee (its published rule, `Processor::about_fee`) settles the invoices, the difference said as its fee in the status line ("€2.15 kept by the processor, as its fee.", `invoice-paid-fee`) and kept with the payment, never written into the money store. Nothing is written on a credit note, on an invoice with nothing left to pay, nor on an invoice in another currency than the bank line's, which is then not kept as joined to it either. Once per bank line and invoice, whatever the device: the same line taken in again, here or on another device, writes nothing more, and a line taken back from an invoice never pays it again. The window says it in the status line with Undo, which takes those payments back and keeps the bank line apart from the invoices' line from then on (`movements::acts::not_same`), and nothing asks to confirm it ([accounting.md](accounting.md#payments-on-your-invoices)). **Not the same payment**, in a movement's menu on the Budgets page, does both for a line found wrong later (`acts::not_same_paid`, which takes back what that line paid on any invoice, by its key: `life::take_back_line`); an agent's `same_movement` with `same = false` keeps the money store apart only ([mcp.md](mcp.md)). Payments add up: one smaller than what is left makes the invoice partly paid. `life::received` and `received_without_tax` give the turnover received from one day to another, excluding VAT, by the nature of what was sold, money paid back on a credit note being a negative receipt on its day, for phase 4's books.
- **Its life, outside the record** (`invoice::lives`): an issued record is never written again, so that its file (and phase 2's kept PDF, with their hashes) stays as it was issued. What happens to it later is written in `invoices/life/<device>.toml` (`format = 1`), each device's own file, which no other device writes, in entries that never name one another, so that nothing dangles when one is taken back: `[[payment]]`, money received with its amount (id, invoice, date, amount, means, line, fee), either said by hand, its id made of what it says (`hand:<invoice>/<day>/<amount>/<means>/<n>`, `n` its rank among payments alike said on this device), or a bank line's share for an invoice, its id made of the line's key, the same on every device that takes the line in (`bank:<line>/<invoice>`; `movements::line_key`: its account, day, amount and a digest of its label); `[[settled]]`, "Paid" said on a day (`settled:<invoice>/<n>`), its amount read each time as what the payments with an amount leave; `[[removed]]`, an entry taken back by its id (an Undo, "Not paid", "Not the same payment"), whichever device wrote it; `[[sent]]` (`invoice`, `date`), the day it was sent, written when a mail that carried it to its client leaves Sioul (`invoices::sent_with`: "Write the mail", in [Invoices in the window](#invoices-in-the-window); `life::mark_sent`, once per device; read as the first day any device said); and, since phase 3, `[[declared]]`, a word on a declaration, said made or not made, dated, the latest counting ("Declared", in "The books", below). The books add `[[booked]]` and `[[told]]` ("The books", below). Reading merges every device's file by union, each id once, a removal winning (`lives::Lives::load`; `all_in` gives each record its life), so that Paid said on two devices, the same export taken in on two, and the same payment typed on two before they exchange each count once; a bank line split across invoices on two devices at once counts as the first file split it. Then each invoice's payments are read (`Lives::apply`): the bank's first, each taken in by a payment said by hand with room for it, of its very amount first, else the nearest in days, never one said in cash, which keeps its own day and amount (the line is its evidence, never a second payment), what is left of the line beyond them counting as its own; then "Paid", a format-1 record's own word among them, covers what is left of what the invoice asked on the first day it was said (its amount less the credit notes issued by then), on that day. "Paid" taken back on one device while another takes the bank's line in leaves the bank's payment. Since each file has one writer, the sync never makes a conflicted copy of a record or of its life. A life file or a series journal of a newer format is read where it can be, never written over (`change_own` and `Journal::save` refuse, asking to update Sioul on this device), and said (`Lives::newer`, `Journal::newer`, in `sioul invoices list`); a record or a draft of a newer format is read as format 2 where it reads so, never written, and said (`Invoice::newer`; `invoice::newer_in`, in Money ▸ Invoices' list too): such a draft opens in the editor to be read only ("Drafts", above). A second file holding a number another holds is one record when it is the same invoice (an older Sioul's Paid aside, which counts if either says it); otherwise two devices gave that number at once (`invoice::twice_in`, `Twice`): the file its number names is the record read, the other is kept, and both are said, by `sioul invoices list` ("2026-014 was given twice, to Studio Lumen and to Atelier Exemple (2026-014 (conflicted copy).toml): issue a credit note for one, then invoice it again.") and by one calm row for all such numbers on the Porch's Money lane, in work's hours, until it is dismissed or none is left (`notices::invoices_twice`, `money:invoices-twice/<numbers>`). Life writers take the device, the sharing's own id (`Numbering::device`, `add_payment`, `take_back`, `pay_rest`, `unpay`, `take_bank_line`, `take_back_line`). Records, their issued files, drafts, life files and journals are written whole through Sioul's shared writer (`filelock::replace`: a hidden file renamed over the old one, tried again on Windows while another program holds it a moment).
- **Format-1 records** (before November 2026, no `format` key) read as issued invoices of kind 380 with time lines (`record::V1`): their SIRET an identifier, their VAT line the franchise's mention (category `E`), or no VAT (`O`) without one. They are never written: their own `paid` reads as one more "Paid", said on their due day (`older:<number>`), which covers only what the other payments leave, which "Not paid" or an Undo takes back with a removal entry, and which an older Sioul ticking Paid changes; their payments and the day they were sent go into the life files as any record's. They print as they always did, in the window's language. A record that no longer reads still holds its number, and is never written over. An older Sioul never reads a format-2 record as its own (`record::tests::an_older_sioul_never_reads_a_format_2_record_as_its_own`), so that its Paid never writes one back without its lines, its taxes and its client; to it, such a record is a number it never gives again, which is why every device that shares invoices is updated together (`packaging/release-notes.md`).
- **Its own language and currency**: an invoice keeps its language (`en` or `fr`: the client profile's, else `[invoice] language`, else the window's) and its currency (the profile's, the settings', the preset's, else euros), prints in them whatever the window speaks, and shows its amounts in its currency in every view (the project's page, the agenda's dated card, `sioul invoices`, the agents), where a euro sign showed before whatever the currency.
- **The page** (`html.rs`) prints the issuer with its mark and identifiers, the client with its identifiers and delivery address, each party's country when the two differ, by its name in the invoice's language ("Belgique" on an invoice in French, `Address::text`), the nature of the operation, the date or period of the service, each line's quantity, unit and unit price, the VAT per rate whenever the invoice carries VAT (liable, or a credit note of an invoice from when you were, whatever your scheme on its day), the totals, the deposits deducted, the legal lines and how to pay: a draft's preview (the editor's **Preview**, in Qt's rich text; `sioul invoices export --html`), and the issued file says the same words ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)). A format-1 record keeps no file: the window prints its page to an A4 PDF through Qt's text document and PDF writer, as it always did, in `[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices` (Factures in French).
- **Budget**: the amount due of an invoice in euros, the ledger's money, is expected in the project's budget, a planned line on the due day (the terms: the client profile's, the settings', the preset's, else 30 days), tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>` (an invoice in another currency is expected in no budget, since the ledger does not convert yet); a credit note lowers the credited invoice's line, or takes it out ("Credit notes", above); ticking "Paid" dates it today and makes it real, with "Undo" for ten seconds; unticked, it is planned again on the day it had, its due day when that is not known (`budget::unsettle_line`), where it was in the ledger, so that Paid then not paid leaves the budget as it was. **Paid…** that pays the invoice in full makes the line real on the day said (`budget::settle_line`). Its Undo plans the line again as it was, its day and planned, when the line still holds what Paid… wrote, real on the payment's day, whatever day that was (`budget::unsettle_paid`, given that day), then takes the payment back; when the line no longer holds it, something having changed it since, nothing is undone, the payment included, and the status line says it is too late ("Too late to undo: it is done."; the review of phase 3, F1). A partial payment leaves the line planned, and its Undo takes the payment back.
- **From the command line** (`crates/sioul-cli/src/invoices.rs`): `sioul invoices [list] [--client <words>] [--year <year>] [--status <status>]` (drafts first, a sync app's copy of a draft said so, then the invoices, newest first, each with where it stands; `--status` draft, issued, sent, partly-paid, paid, credited, credit-note or to-pay-back; a record that no longer reads, a number with no record here, a number given twice, and a file written by a newer Sioul, said), `show <number or draft>` (its lines, totals and payments, its client and whether it is billed as a business, a person or a public body, "a business, as no client profile says otherwise; a profile with kind = "consumer" bills a person" when no profile says it, a draft's client as its profile or card says it now, the service's day or period, and for a draft what it still lacks), `export [--year <year>]` (every invoice as CSV, in French with `;` and decimal commas, each text cell through the time spreadsheet's guard, `timereport::csv_cell`, so that a client's name never runs as a formula) `export --html <number>` (its page to print) or `export --pdf <number>` (its issued file, copied where invoices go, made again first when gone or changed and this Sioul makes the same bytes; its path), `draft <project>` (a draft of the project's time not billed yet, never time a draft holds already nor time taken out of one of the project's drafts, `life::untaken`; nothing numbered), `issue <draft>` (you at the keyboard, under the window's rules: one device numbers at a time, never one whose sharing leaves invoices out or whose sharing key cannot be read; with sharing on, only when this computer's window may number, as the window itself asks it (`sioul_sync::lease::may_number`: this computer's claim alive, renewed within two minutes and never closed, the keeper's, settled, no other computer's closed claim unread, the others heard from lately), else "Open Sioul on this computer first…" (`invoice-cli-open-sioul`) or the window's own words, since a claim of the command line's own would lapse unseen when it ends; right after issuing, it renews that claim with the journal's last numbers (`claim_numbers`), never reopening one the window closed meanwhile, whose numbers `lease::close_with` raises instead; the draft read and issued in one step, `life::issue_as_read` with the revision just read; the draft's client made again from its profile or card; its notes said, its file copied where invoices go and its path said, a copy that cannot be written said apart, the invoice issued all the same), `credit <number> [--line <n>]…` (a draft credit note, all lines or those given, never beyond what is left to credit), `discard <draft>` (a draft taken out: nothing was numbered), `pay <number> [--day <day>] [--amount <amount>] [--means transfer|card|cash|cheque|direct-debit|other] [--received <amount>]` (a payment received, or, on a credit note, money paid back; what is left when no amount is given, never more; for an invoice in another currency than the books', `--received` says what reached the person in the books' currency), `received <number> <amount> [--day <day>]` (the same, said afterwards: "The books", below).
- **Agents**: `invoices` reads drafts and invoices, `draft_invoice` writes drafts only, when you ask, a project's time as `sioul invoices draft` takes it (`life::untaken`); neither issues, sends nor records a payment: their drafts are listed in Money ▸ Invoices, where you check and issue them; `books` reads the books ([mcp.md](mcp.md)).

### Invoices in the window
Money ▸ Invoices, the Budgets page's fifth tab, **Invoices** (`qml/InvoicesTab.qml`, made the first time it shows; `crates/sioul-app/src/invoices.rs`; the invoicing's phase 3, 17 November 2026), holds every draft and every invoice, the editor and each issued invoice's pane. A project's **Make the invoice** and a contact's **Bill this client** open their draft in the editor there; an invoice's row on its project's page opens its pane, and so does its link (in Ties, a note, a task) when the agenda has no card for it, paid or a credit note (`main.qml`: `openInvoiceDraft`, `openInvoice`). Drafts and the client profiles are a change kind of their own (`changes::Kind::Invoices`: `invoice::save_draft`, `restore_draft`, `remove_draft` and `clients::save`, `change`, `remove` here; `notes/sioul-clients.toml` from the sharing), and the issued records, their life files and the journals another (`changes::Kind::Issued`: `invoice::write_new`, `remove_issued`, `lives::change_own`, `Journal::save`; the second review of phase 3, R15); the invoices' folder holds both, so `data/invoices/` arriving from the sharing says both. Both are told by the signal `invoices_changed` to Money ▸ Invoices, its editor, the Projects page and, while it shows, the Time page: a draft saved as it is typed says `Invoices` alone, reads no store again on the window's thread (what the pages read is kept, `work::loaded_known`), leaves the tie index as it is and never plans the day again; `Issued` reads no store again either, but empties the tie index, which reads the issued invoices (`changes::heard`), so that an invoice issued or paid outside this window shows in a project's Ties and on a shown Time page without waiting for another change; what arrives from elsewhere also makes the Porch, the budgets and the accounts again, since the Porch says the declarations and the invoices not paid yet (`changes::remade`; the review of phase 3, F7).

- **The list** (`invoices::list`): the drafts first, the newest first, then the invoices, credit notes and deposit invoices by their day, the newest first. With none: "No invoice yet. Kind regards," and your name; a credit note's without a day), then the account's signature, as every draft written for you has it (`Draft::add_signature`; the review of phase 3, F17); the kept file attached, `invoices/<number>.pdf`, the bytes issued, made again first when gone or changed (`kept::issued`; refused in words when it cannot be); the draft tied to `sioul:invoice/<number>`, and naming the invoice it was written to carry (`Draft::invoice`, its number; empty for every other mail). Nothing leaves until you send it. When a mail tied to an invoice leaves Sioul (sent; sent but not filed in Sent; sent to some of its recipients), the invoice is marked sent that day only when that mail carried it to its client (`invoices::sent_with`, `delivered`; F5; the second review of phase 3, R11): the invoice's kept PDF among its attachments, by its SHA-256 (a format-1 invoice, its printed copy, by its path), and, among the recipients its server took (To, Cc and Bcc, less `SyncError::PartlySent`'s `missed`), either a known address of the client, as **Write the mail** resolves it (`invoice_addresses`: the record's, its profile's, its contact's card's), or, for the mail **Write the mail** drafted for this invoice, at least one recipient that is not one of your own accounts' addresses. So an address typed in the invoice's own mail counts, for a client with no address known too, and your own copy alone does not; a mail merely tied to the invoice (a reminder with the file, an agent's draft) still needs a known address of the client; and a mail without the file marks nothing, nor does a send whose server refused every such address, nor a draft made or saved. Marked on the send path before the draft goes (`mail.rs`, `send_now`, `send_signed_now`; `life::mark_sent`, `[[sent]]`). A format-1 invoice, which kept no file, attaches its printed copy where invoices go, else says to press **Print again** first ("This invoice, from before November 2026, kept no file: press Print again first, then Write the mail (…).").
- **Print again** gives the kept file back, as before ([The issued file](#the-issued-file)), copied where invoices go and opened in the PDF reader.
- **Changed outside Sioul** (`invoice::outside`, `series::Journal::records`): an issued record is never written again (its life lives apart), so at issue the SHA-256 of its bytes as written is kept with its number in the series journal of the device that issued it (`records`, number → hex, left out of a journal that has none: an older Sioul reads past it; written by that device alone, one device issuing a number; read as every device's, `Journal::load`). The pane says a record whose bytes no longer give it ("This invoice's record was changed outside Sioul since it was issued (…/2026-014.toml): an issued invoice is never changed; a credit note answers it."), and a kept file whose SHA-256 is not the record's `file_sha256`, or a record that names a file other than its number's (`kept::state`, Changed or Misnamed: "This invoice's file was changed outside Sioul since it was issued (…/2026-014.pdf): Print again gives back the file issued, made again from its record."); its row says "changed outside Sioul". A kept file not on this device yet is not changed; a record issued before phase 3 has no hash, and only its file is judged; a format-1 record is never judged. Said calmly, in words, never a badge; nothing is changed back by itself. A file's SHA-256 is read again only when its size or its modification time changed (`kept::sha256_of`, kept for the process), so that the list, which asks it of every invoice at each read, does not hash every kept PDF again (the review of phase 3, F7).
- **On a phone** ([android.md](android.md)): the Budgets page's five tab names stay in one row, which scrolls sideways when it is wider than the screen (in French, Aperçu, Budgets, Mouvements, Courriels, Factures), the tab shown kept in sight, rather than names cut; a row's menu on a long press; the editor and the dialogs in one column, every row within its width. **Back**, Android's and the title bar's arrow (the Budgets page's `canGoBack` and `back`), closes what the page opened and gives its list again: the editor, what was typed written first (a save that failed keeps it open once: [The editor](#the-editor)); an invoice's pane; a budget's ledger; the declaration view (the review of phase 3, F15).
- **Amounts typed** (`invoices::read_amount_in`, `read_amount`, `read_number`; the review of phase 3, F8; the second, R1 and R14): a line's quantity, price, discount and rate in the editor, **Paid…**'s amount and **Deposit invoice…**'s amount or share are read by the window's language: in English a comma groups thousands and a dot marks decimals ("1,500.50"; "1,50" is no amount); in French a space, a no-break or a narrow space groups three digits and a comma marks decimals (« 1 500,50 »), a dot alone read as decimals ("12.50", "1.5"); but a dot followed by exactly three digits with no comma after ("1.500", "12.500": thousands to many hands, decimals to Sioul) is refused, never read as the smaller number, and so is a space group that is not three digits ("12 5", "1 50"); a currency sign is left aside. What a number reads as is said beside its field as it is typed: an amount with its currency ("= €1,500.00"; beside a line's price and discount, under Paid…'s and the deposit's amount), a quantity and a rate without one ("= 2.5"; `read_number`). Text that does not read says so ("Not an amount: write 1500, or 1,500.50."), an emptied number that is needed (a quantity, a price, a rate; an emptied discount is none) asks for one ("Type a number."), and neither is sent ([The editor](#the-editor)).

### The editor
`qml/InvoiceEditor.qml` and `crates/sioul-core/src/invoice/edit.rs` (`invoices::draft`, `change`, `put_back`, `set_client_traits`, `issue`, `discard`, `leave_draft`, `after_exchange`): a draft, shown as it would be issued today (its client made again from its profile or card, the settings of today, its deposits as they are now, its totals and checks: `invoices::draft_view`, `life::refresh_client`, `recite`, `refresh`; showing it writes nothing), saved as it is typed: there is no Save. A line's fields, the notes, the purchase order, the terms and the currency are sent 600 ms after the typing stops, a choice at once (the review of phase 3, F11). What was typed and not sent yet is written before the draft is left (**Keep as draft**, a phone's Back, another draft opened), before **Issue** and **Deposit invoice…**, and before Sioul quits or is put away on a phone (`InvoiceEditor`'s `flush` and `leave`; `InvoicesTab.flush` and `BudgetsPage.flushInvoices`, called from `main.qml` on quitting and when Android suspends or hides the window).

Each change names only what it changes (`edit::Change`): a field, or a line by its id with only the fields changed (`LineEdit`), a line without an id being a new one, and the lines taken out by their ids (`removed`); in the window's units, made integers at the boundary (`edit::cents`, `thousandths`, basis points); with the draft's revision as the editor read it (`invoice::draft_revision`: the SHA-256 of the draft's file, cut to 16 digits). `edit::save` merges it into the draft as its file is on this device, field by field and line by line, through `life::change_draft_read` (the review of phase 3, F2): the file is read, changed and written in one step under its lock, the one the sharing takes to write it, the revision compared inside (`invoice::with_draft_lock`; the second review of phase 3, R4), so that what already reached this device's file meanwhile (a change the sharing brought, the command line, a later **Make the invoice**) is kept, and the status line says so ("This draft changed elsewhere meanwhile: those changes are taken in, yours with them."); a line changed here but taken out elsewhere drops the change, and says so ("A line you changed was taken out elsewhere meanwhile: your change to it is dropped."). The editor then keeps what was just typed as typed and takes the rest as the draft now says it; while nothing typed waits, a change heard from elsewhere (`invoices_changed`) reads the open draft again at once when its revision moved, and so does the Invoices tab coming back into view, since a tab out of sight hears nothing (`InvoicesTab`'s `onVisibleChanged`; R6); either says so ("This draft changed elsewhere: it is shown as it is now."). Reading a draft never writes it: a line with no id in its file (a draft of before line ids) is named in memory as it is read, from the draft's id and the line's place (`invoice::read_draft`: ten digits of the SHA-256 of `<draft id>/<place>`), alike at every read and on every device, and kept with the first real change (R3; until then, opening such a draft wrote it, with ids drawn at random on each device). A line of time keeps its minutes and its sessions under its id, whatever moves around it: only its words and its hourly price change; taken out, its time is set aside in the draft (`Invoice::set_aside`), never put back by **Make the invoice**, and given back by the draft's own **Put back** ("Lines", below). A draft written by a newer Sioul opens to be read only, its fields and buttons off, saying why ("This draft was written by a newer Sioul: it opens to be read only. Update Sioul on this device to change it."; F13). A draft **New** or **Bill this client** just made in this window, left exactly as it was made (its file unchanged: nothing chosen, nothing typed), is taken out without a word when the editor leaves it; a draft changed in any way, or made before, always stays (`invoices::leave_draft`, `FRESH`; F17).

A save that fails (`send`) puts what was typed back to wait, with what was typed since, and says the reason in the status line, where it is seen on a phone too; **Back** then keeps the editor open once, saying that a second Back leaves without those changes ("Not saved: … Press Back once more to leave without these changes."; `leave`, `InvoicesTab.back`), and a later save that holds settles it. When the draft is gone, discarded or issued elsewhere (`gone`, `invoices::gone_words`), nothing can be tried again: the status line says why ("This draft was issued already, as 2026-014: it is taken out.", or "This draft is no longer there."), and **Back** leaves at once, saying it again. Only **Back** waits so: another draft opened, Quit and a phone putting Sioul away say the reason and go on (the second review of phase 3, R5).

Between devices, a draft travels as one whole file (the second review of phase 3, R2): the sharing carries each file of the invoices' folder as one entry, the later word winning (`sioul-sync`'s `Shape::Whole` for `data/invoices/`), so when two devices each change the same draft before they exchange, the later file replaces the other whole, and what the other device changed is not in it. The sharing keeps the replaced file among the earlier versions of the device that lost ([database.md](database.md#earlier-versions)), from where it is put back whole. The merge by line and by field, above, covers only what had already reached this device's file: another program's change, or one the sharing had already brought. What is said: when an exchange replaces a draft that this window changed since the last exchange, the status line names it ("The draft for Studio Lumen was changed on another device before your changes here went out: its version replaced yours, kept among the earlier versions."; `invoices::changed_here`, which the editor's saves, **Put back**, **Final invoice…** and **Make the invoice** call with the revision they wrote; `after_exchange`, called by `share::exchange_now` once an exchange wrote into `data/invoices/`, and `replaced_among`, which names the drafts whose file is then another). Its limits: those drafts are tracked in memory, per run of the window, and only as the window itself wrote them, so nothing is said when Sioul was closed and started again before the exchange; a draft the exchange took out (issued or discarded elsewhere) is not named; and nothing is said on the device whose version won. To carry a draft as entries, its fields and each line's fields each their own, as `config.toml` and the client profiles travel, the sharing lacks four things: a gate for the devices whose Sioul does not know the store (as the client profiles wait until every device's Sioul knows their file); deleting an emptied entries-file in a folder store (a draft issued or discarded goes as a file); a per-file report of a file replaced while it was changed here (which the window works out from the drafts' revisions meanwhile); and per-field entries inside an element of a keyed list (a draft's lines, keyed by `id`).

- **The client**: typed, then chosen among the client profiles first and the contacts with a name or an organisation (twelve at most, `invoices::client_choices`), or "Bill "…" as written"; under its name, its kind, its country ("country not said" when none) and its email. Another client brings its profile's language, currency, terms and purchase order (`life::for_client`); a deposit ticked that bills another client or currency is then unticked, and said ("Deposits and final invoices", above). **Kind and country…** sets the client's kind (A business, A person, A public body) and country, written in its profile, those two keys alone, the rest of it as written (`clients::change`), or in a profile made for its contact, or its name, when it has none (`invoices::set_client_traits`): a draft's client is made again from its profile until it is issued, so a kind or a country kept in the draft alone would be undone at issue; a country Sioul does not know is refused in words. A credit note keeps the credited invoice's client, and shows no field.
- **The days**: "Issued on 17 November 2026, when you press Issue.": the issue's day is the day **Issue** is pressed, never chosen, since the numbers follow the days: an invoice numbered today never bears an earlier day than the one numbered before it. **Service**, its first and last days; both empty, the issue's day, said under them ("The service's day is the issue's, unless you say another day or period.", `period_default`); none on a deposit invoice.
- **Lines**: those from time folded under one row ("2 lines of time"), each with its hours as noted and its **Price an hour**; the others in full: **What it is**, a quantity, a unit (Hours, Days, Units, Months, Kilometres, Lump sum: Recommendation 20's `HUR`, `DAY`, `C62`, `MON`, `KMT`, `LS`; minutes, `MIN`, are time's own unit, priced by the hour, never offered for another line, and a line in minutes shows them by their name), a **Unit price** and the line's amount; the quantity, the price, the discount and a rate are read by the window's language, what each reads as said beside its field as it is typed, a quantity and a rate without a currency ("= 2.5"; "Amounts typed", above), and each is sent once it reads. One that does not read, or an emptied one that is needed (a quantity, a price, a rate: "Type a number."), is said beside its field in the warm colour, stays as typed whatever other save lands, and is not sent (`setLine`, `mark`, `unread`; the second review of phase 3, R1); while any is so, **Issue** is held back, with why under it ("A number above does not read, so it was not saved: correct it, then issue."). When lines come or go, the page is made again from the draft; a text that did not read stays as typed on the lines still there, and still holds Issue back (the label beside its field goes with the page made again: the sentence under Issue remains). **More**, under each: **Details, under the line**, a **Discount**, A service or Goods, **Your activity for it** when the settings name more than one, its tax as the preset places it ("Its tax: VAT 20%", or its category's words), and **Its own tax, not your country's rule** (`Line::own_tax`): a category (VAT at a rate, VAT at zero, Exempt from VAT, Reverse charge, Delivery to another EU country, Export outside the EU, Outside the scope of VAT), a rate among the preset's of today, and **Why it bears no VAT here, in words**. VAT at a rate chosen on a line with no rate takes the preset's standard rate, which the list then shows as it is; under Elsewhere, whose preset has no rates, the rate is typed (**Rate, %**; `edit::edit_line`; the review of phase 3, F3). A unit, a category or a rate its list does not hold (written by hand, or by a newer Sioul) shows as itself, after the list's own, never as its first entry (F17). **+ A line** adds one (none on a credit note); **×** takes one out, a line of time leaving its time aside. Under the lines, a quiet row then says how much time was taken out of the draft ("1 h 30 of time taken out of this draft, not billed here.", from `set_aside`) with **Put back** (`invoices::put_back`), which gives the draft that time's lines again, each joined to the draft's line of the same words and hourly price (`life::from_time`, `add_time`), what was billed or deleted since left out, and empties `set_aside` (R15).
- **Deposits it deducts**, on an invoice: the issued deposit invoices it may deduct (`life::deposits_open`: of its client, in its currency, something left of them after their credit notes, deducted by no live final invoice), each with its number, its day and what is left of it, and, when that is less than it billed, why ("of €300.00, its credit notes gave the rest back"; R8), ticked when cited; what a cited deposit deducts is made again as it is now each time the draft is shown or changed, so that the totals, the preview and the issue agree; one cited that no longer fits stays listed, ticked, and a required check says why ("Deposits and final invoices", above).
- **Terms, notes, language and currency**, folded, saved as they are typed, as the lines: **Payable within, in days**, **at the end of that month**, and the day it makes ("Payable by 17 December 2026"); **The client's purchase order**; the invoice's language (English or French) and its currency (a credit note keeps its invoice's); **Your notes, printed under the lines**, a line each; the legal lines the preset prints for this client, shown as they print (Settings ▸ Invoices changes them).
- **The totals** (`invoices::totals_view`), in the invoice's currency: the total, or, when it bears VAT, the total without VAT, the VAT on each rate and the total with VAT; the deposits deducted; the amount due.
- **The checks** (`life::checks`), under the totals: each required item in a sentence, in the warm colour, **Issue** waiting until none is left, a line is there and every number typed reads; the recommended ones and the notes in quieter words under them.
- **Preview**, folded: the page as it prints (`invoice::page`, Qt's rich text), in the invoice's language.
- **Keep as draft** (the editor's Back, an arrow on a phone): the list again, what was typed written first, a draft left with nothing in it taken out; the draft is kept already, and a save that failed keeps the editor open once (above). **Issue** (`invoices::issue`): what `life::issue` does ("Issued all or nothing", above), what was typed written first, asked with the revision the editor read (`life::issue_as_read`): when the draft's file moved since, nothing is issued, the editor reads it again and says so beside **Issue** and in the status line ("This draft changed elsewhere since it was read: it is read again here; look at it, then issue."; R6); refused in words, the draft kept, the reason said beside **Issue** and in the status line, so that it is in sight on a phone too (the review of phase 3, F14), and while invoices are numbered on another computer, with **Make invoices on this device** under it ([accounting.md](accounting.md#invoices-on-one-device)); issued, its file copied where invoices go and opened in the PDF reader, its pane opened, the status line saying what the issue found to say (a file set aside, the checks' notes, a copy not written). **Deposit invoice…**, on an invoice ("Deposits", above). **Discard** (`invoices::discard`): "Draft invoice discarded.", with **Undo**, which writes its file back byte for byte, whatever Sioul wrote it (`invoice::draft_bytes`, `restore_draft`: nothing of it read nor made again); a draft a newer Sioul wrote is not discarded here (`invoice-draft-newer`; R13); refused, the reason said beside it and in the status line too.

### The issued file
Issued, an invoice is a file, written once and kept beside its record ([research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), §15; finding 80 of [research.md](research.md)).

- **A PDF/A-3b, written in Rust** (`pdf.rs`, krilla 0.8.2): A4, 18 mm margins; Liberation Sans regular and bold, embedded and subset (`crates/sioul-core/data/fonts/`: version 2.1.5, byte for byte as released, SIL Open Font License 1.1, its licence beside and in every package with `NOTICES.md`); its colours in sRGB, with sRGB's output intent; its creation day the day of issue, so that the same record makes the same bytes. It says what the page says (`html::current`), word for word, in the invoice's language and currency: a test holds what the layout draws to the page's words, and every word inside its page (`pdf::tests::the_pdf_says_what_the_page_says`). A long invoice goes on as many pages as it takes, the table's head again on each, each page saying which it is; a line longer than a page goes on to the next under the head again, its figures on its first part; a column's head too wide for its column goes on two lines, and a total wider than its column reaches left, its label ending before it; a test holds every word drawn to no other on its line. A character the font lacks (a script other than Latin, Greek or Cyrillic) holds the issue back, said before (`pdf::unprintable`), since a PDF/A never draws a missing glyph; right-to-left writing (Hebrew, Arabic) too (`pdf::right_to_left`), which would print backwards without Unicode's bidirectional ordering; a non-breaking hyphen prints as a hyphen, a narrow no-break space as a no-break space.
- **Its Factur-X XML** (`cii.rs`): Factur-X 1.09.2 / ZUGFeRD 2.5.2 in the EN 16931 profile (`urn:cen.eu:en16931:2017`), UN/CEFACT CII D22B written by Sioul's own serializer on quick-xml, element by element in the schema's order, from the record's own terms, nothing computed again. Attached as `factur-x.xml` (`text/xml`, the relationship *Alternative*: the PDF and the XML say the same invoice, the one relationship Germany accepts), listed in the catalog's `/AF`. The VAT categories: `S` with its rate; `E` with its reason (the French franchise: `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE` and its mention); `AE`, the reverse charge, with both VAT numbers; `O`, not registered, with `VATEX-EU-O`, no rate and neither side's VAT number; `K`, goods to a business in another member state, with `VATEX-EU-IC`, the delivery country and day; `G`, an export, with `VATEX-EU-G`; `Z`. The identifiers: a French SIREN (a SIRET's first nine digits) as the legal registration (scheme 0002), the VAT number (`VA`), a Steuernummer, a UTR or a tax number elsewhere as a tax registration (`FC`), another identifier as the seller's own when it has none of these; France's business process (BT-23: `B1` goods, `S1` services, `M1` both); the account to pay into, the one the QR code names (the first whose IBAN's key holds, else the first): a SEPA transfer (58) when its key holds and the invoice is in euros, else a credit transfer (30) with the number as written (`ProprietaryID`, after the holder's name, in the schema's order), the invoice's number as the payment's reference; the day the supply was completed (BT-72): the last day of the period billed, else the day of issue, none on a deposit invoice, which bills what is not supplied yet (its period, when it says one, as BG-14); the invoices it cites (BG-3, unbounded in the profile's schema): the one a credit note corrects, the deposit invoices a final one deducts; the countries as ISO 3166-1 codes (`clients::country`, the one table of countries, `data/countries.tsv`: UK as GB, EL as GR, a name in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese or its own languages as its code); the units as UN/ECE Recommendation 20's (`units.rs`).
- **Its XMP** (`pdfa.rs`): the Factur-X properties (`fx:DocumentType` INVOICE, `fx:DocumentFileName`, `fx:Version` 1.0, `fx:ConformanceLevel` EN 16931) and their schema, among the PDF/A extension schemas krilla declares (one `pdfaExtension:schemas` only: a property twice, and no reader takes the XMP). krilla 0.8.2 takes no XMP property of one's own (its issue #209), so the metadata stream krilla wrote is written again in place, its length with it, the cross-reference offsets after it moved by as much: one object changes, the file stays one revision. lopdf, which the design named for this, would rewrite the whole file and bring some twenty crates (ciphers, hashes, a random generator) for one stream; this takes none, and a test reads the table back.
- **Without its XML** (`cii::without`), said before issue as a note, never as a reason not to issue: under the French franchise without a VAT number, since EN 16931's exempt category asks the seller's VAT or tax number (BR-E-02) and the French rule that says which number to give instead, BR-FR-CO-16 of AFNOR's XP Z12-012, is still to be read (design question 1); and an invoice whose rules lack your VAT or tax number, the client's VAT number (a reverse charge, a delivery to another member state), an identifier of yours, your country or the client's as a code, a line's unit or price the standard takes, or (a deposit for goods to another member state) the period it covers; lines outside the scope of VAT beside others, which EN 16931 keeps apart. The PDF is then a PDF/A-3 without attachment: a complete invoice on paper, as French law asks of small businesses until 1 September 2027.
- **The EPC QR code** (`epc.rs`; EPC069-12 version 3.1): on euro invoices with an IBAN whose key holds, never on a credit note: version `002`, UTF-8, `SCT`, the BIC when given, the account holder's name (else yours, at most 70 characters), the IBAN, the amount due, the invoice's number as the remittance; error correction M, version 13 at most, 331 bytes at most; drawn as squares (qrcodegen's modules, a run of dark ones one rectangle), 25 mm with its quiet zone, at the right of the payment details, "Scan to pay by transfer" under it. `[invoice] qr = false` leaves it out. An IBAN whose key fails is said before issue.
- **Kept** (`kept.rs`): written in the same step as the record and its number, under the numbering's lock. The record is the commit: the file is written first under a name the sharing leaves alone (`<number>.pdf.new`), takes its name once the record is written, and is taken away when a step after it fails. An issue stopped halfway (a crash, a phone closing the app) never blocks the next number: before numbering, the device that numbers gives its name to a waiting file whose record came with its SHA-256, and sets aside in `invoices/leftovers/` any other waiting file and any kept file no record holds, said once in the status line (`kept::tidy`). The record names its file (`file`, which must be its number's, `<number>.pdf`, else nothing is handed out) with its SHA-256 (`file_sha256`). It travels with the records, in the sharing's part for drafts and invoices ([database.md](database.md)). **Print again**, issuing in the window or the terminal, and `sioul invoices export --pdf` copy it byte for byte where invoices go (`[invoice] folder`, else `~/Documents/Invoices`), so that a PDF reader saving into its copy never changes the file kept; a copy there already the same is left as it is. A kept file gone or changed is made again from the frozen record (the QR code on or off, as it was) and kept again when this Sioul makes the bytes issued, a changed one first set aside in `leftovers/`; when it cannot (another version of Sioul made them), nothing is handed out, and the sentence says where the record and the file are (`kept::issued`). When the copy where invoices go cannot be written, the invoice is issued all the same, and the status line says so ("Print again once that folder is there"). Format-1 records keep no file: their page is printed through Qt as before.
- **Checked in CI** (`.github/workflows/e-invoices.yml`, on pushes to main touching what goes into an invoice's file, and by hand, as the other workflows run): sioul-core's ignored test `invoice::samples::write_fixtures` writes one invoice per preset and VAT scheme, a credit note, a deposit invoice and the final one, an account whose IBAN's key fails, three pages of lines, a line longer than a page (`valid/`), one made wrong on purpose with the rule it breaks beside it (its totals a cent off, BR-CO-15, `invalid/`), and the French franchise without a VAT number (`without-xml/`). Mustang CLI 2.26.0 (its jar's SHA-256 pinned) reads each with veraPDF, the EN 16931 schematron and France's rules of XP Z12-012 inside, and `tools/einvoice/validate.sh` expects each part of its report valid and no word from the French rules on a good fixture, the broken one invalid for its own rule, and the PDF/A-3b of the third valid; it lists every warning. The same runs offline with Mustang's jar and Java (the script's comment says how). No invoice goes to an online validator.

### Countries: presets as data
- **One file per country**, `crates/sioul-core/data/invoice-presets/*.toml`, built into Sioul (`presets.rs`): France (`fr`), Germany (`de`), Belgium (`be`), the United Kingdom (`uk`), Switzerland (`ch`), and `elsewhere` for any country (no legal claims: every identifier and line your own). Spain (`es`) and Italy (`it`) are listed as not supported, each with why (certified, chained invoicing software, VERI\*FACTU; the state's exchange system, SdI): issuing under them is refused with that sentence, rather than a template offered that would be illegal there. No country has a code path of its own.
- **What a preset says**: its labels in English and French; its legal forms (and the mark a form prints after the name), its income regimes; the identifier kinds its invoices carry and how each is checked; what is required or recommended before issue (of the issuer or the client; for business, consumer or public clients; where the client is, at home, in another EU member state or abroad; for goods or services; under which scheme and legal form; from or until a day), with the sentence said when it is missing; its VAT schemes, the franchise's category, reason code and dated mention, its dated rates, exempt lines; where a line's VAT is due (`[[tax.place]]`, below); its legal lines with their EN 16931 subject codes, each with its source in the file's comments; its late-payment terms, its payment terms, its activities, thresholds and contribution rates, each dated, and the rules of its declarations of turnover and of its declaration of services to businesses in other member states (France's `[declaration]` and `[des]`), which the books read ("The books", below).

Germany and Belgium: a service to a business in another member state takes the reverse charge under either scheme, "Reverse charge" ("Autoliquidation" on an invoice in French), Germany asking both VAT numbers under the small-business exemption too (Directive 2006/112/EC art. 226(3)-(4)); liable, their intra-EU deliveries of goods and their exports are placed too, and so are their services to a business outside the EU under either scheme, and their goods to one under the franchise too (`abroad-service`, `abroad-goods-franchise`: the franchise's mention would not say why such a line bears no VAT), as are, in the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a liable issuer's services to a foreign business and exports. Where the research gives no words for a placement, a required check says why and asks the line's own. With no rule: not registered, `O`; the franchise, `E` with its mention (France: "TVA non applicable, article 293 B du CGI", reason code `VATEX-FR-FRANCHISE`), and no VAT ever shown (art. 293 E CGI); liable, `S` at the preset's standard rate (a service to a consumer abroad too). A line's own tax (`Line::own_tax`, with its exemption's words and reason code: yours, or a credit note's, kept from the invoice it credits) stays as set. The client's zone comes from its country, by VAT territories (`presets::zone`, `vat_territory`: Monaco's is France's; "Clients", below): an address whose last line is a postal one is domestic; a client with no address at all, or whose address's last line names no country Sioul knows, is in an unknown zone, taken as domestic to place its lines, and a required check asks its country whenever a placement rule could apply to that client and its lines (`invoice-check-client-country`). The rates shipped (France 20, 10, 5.5 and 2.1 %; Germany 19 and 7 %; Belgium 21, 12 and 6 %; the United Kingdom 20, 5 and 0 %; Switzerland 8.1, 2.6 and 3.8 %) were not part of the research, but for Switzerland's 8.1 %: they are the rates as known in mid-2026, each file says so, and they are to be checked.
- **France's lines**: for business clients only, "Escompte pour paiement anticipé : néant.", the late-payment penalties at the rate kept with the invoice (by default the ECB's main refinancing rate in force on 1 January or 1 July, plus 10 points: 12.40 % for an invoice of the second half of 2026), and the €40 recovery indemnity (C. com. ### The books
What the law asks a business to keep and to say of the money it receives, made from the payments of the invoices it issued and, for what no invoice describes, from the work budgets that declare ([accounting.md](accounting.md#the-declaration)): the receipts book, the declarations of turnover, the VAT summary, the thresholds watched and the declaration of services to businesses in other member states (`crates/sioul-core/src/books/`: `mod.rs`, `declarations.rs`, `vat.rs`, `thresholds.rs`, `des.rs`; the window's part in `crates/sioul-app/src/books.rs` and `ledger.rs`, the command line's in `crates/sioul-cli/src/books.rs`). Built in the invoicing's phase 4 (November 2026), with the declarations said made and the refund rule in its phase 3. Money counts as the law counts it: received, on its day (finding 79). Rates, thresholds and the rules of declarations are each preset's dated data (`presets.rs`: `Contribution`, `Threshold`, `DeclarationRule`, `DesRule`), so that a release updates them, and each is overridable in `[invoice]` (below). The books prepare and estimate: nothing is declared, filed or paid from Sioul. Nothing says "late": a day to declare by is a day, before or after it. What they rest on: findings 79, 81 and 82 of [research.md](research.md), §6 to §8 and §10 of [research/invoicing.md](research/invoicing.md), and §2.1 of [research/money-ledger.md](research/money-ledger.md) for refunds.

- **Payments read in one place** (`books::received_of`): each issued record's own payments, as `invoice::all_in` gives them their life ("Its life, outside the record", above: a bank line's share, keyed by the line; a payment said by hand; "Paid", one entry worth what the others left on its day; through a processor, what the client paid on the processor's day, its fee apart), each signed: what an invoice or a deposit invoice received, positive; a credit note's entries, money paid back, negative (refunds, as **Paid back**, **Paid back…** and `sioul invoices pay <credit note> --amount` write them). The receipts book, the VAT summary and the franchise's count read payments through it alone, never the life files, and a payment's turnover excluding VAT through `invoice::life::received_without_tax` (its share of the record's total without tax, a credit note's below zero). The declared turnover is what the client paid: a processor's fee is never taken off (CGI art. 102 ter, finding 62). An entry whose day does not read is left out.
- **The receipts book** (`books::rows`, `book`; C. com. L123-28; CGI art. 50-0, 102 ter and 286; BOI-BIC-DECLA-30-30 §20–30): one row per payment received, in the order of the days, each on the day it was received: the client (the invoice's, never the bank's words), the nature of what was sold (goods, services or both, as the invoice's lines say), the invoice's number, the means of payment in words (bank transfer, card, cash, cheque, direct debit, other; "not said" when the payment says none), cash said apart, what was received, and the turnover excluding VAT, divided as the record's lines are by activity ("Activity and nature", below). A refund paid back on a credit note is a row of its own, negative, on its day: it lowers the book's totals of its own period, never the period of the payment it gives back; the declarations count it where "A refund and its declaration", below, places each of its parts (the row keeps where each part counts, `Row::counted`: a `Counted` per part, the day it counts on, `on`, or the declaration it corrects, `corrects`, and its share excluding VAT, `share`; a receipt counts whole on its own day, `Counted::whole`). Totals of each quarter the days asked touch (from the quarter's first day until the last day asked) and of each year (from its 1 January), VAT included, excluding it, sales and services apart: "Fourth quarter 2026: €2,080 received."; under VAT, "Fourth quarter 2026: €600 received with VAT, €500 excluding it."; a total stopped before its period's end is named until its last day ("Fourth quarter 2026, until 31 October 2026: €2,080 received.", `Total::until`).
- **Activity and nature** (`books::line_activity`, `Split`; the review of phase 4, P4-13): each line of a record is declared in an activity, apart from its nature. Its nature (goods or services) says when its VAT is due and where; its activity says its contribution rates and the threshold it counts toward, as sales or as services. The micro regime's sales are goods, food sold to take away or eaten on the spot, and lodging (C. com. L123-28, after CGI art. 50-0; service-public F32353), so a meal served or a night's lodging is a service for VAT and a sale for URSSAF and the thresholds. A line's activity is its own (`activity` on the line, `goods-bic`, `services-bic`, `bnc` or `cipav`), else the issuer's when it has one only, else the first activity said of its nature, else the first said. A record's payments divide by its lines' activities, each part its share of the record's amount without VAT (`Split::divide`); a receipt no invoice describes goes to the first activity said.
- **Every euro once, with the budgets that declare** (`receipts::declares`; the review of phase 4, P4-3 and P4-12): a receipt no invoice describes still comes from such a budget's movements, as the receipts register read them (`receipts::register_rows`: at what the client paid, on the day it was paid at its processor; its client the movement's words, its means the processor's name, a transfer, or "written by hand"). Every euro of such a movement counts once (`Moved`): a movement whose bank line an invoice's payment came from (the payment's `line`, the line's key, `movements::line_key`: its account, day, amount and a digest of its label, the same on every device) stands for that payment, up to its amount (a carried payment of the same amount first, as in a processor's payout); a payment through a processor is its gross and the bank's line its net, so the line is that payment whole, never counted beside it; what it brought beyond the payments it stood for (an overpayment, a payout's other payments) is a receipt of its own in its budget, noted "beyond invoice 2026-031", no invoice describing it; a payment said by hand that no line was found for, whose amount a movement tied to the same invoice shows, is that movement, and a format-1 record's own "paid", which had no day, takes its line's day; a movement the matching tied to an invoice with no payment written stays, with the invoice's client and nature. A receipt no invoice describes is cash when its words say so ("cash", « espèces », « liquide »: `cash_said`), an invoice's payment by its means. ## Budgets at their pace
A budget's verdict no longer compares a projection made only of what is scheduled with its target, which said "short" on the 3rd of every month. What is known in advance (presets, planned lines) counts in full; the rest, what simply happens, is weighed against the part of the period gone: ten days into thirty, a third of what the period still needs should have come in (or no more than a third of what it may spend should be spent). Within the one tolerance of every verdict, 5 % of what the period moves, it is on track ([accounting.md](accounting.md), "On track, without assuming money flows evenly").

The end of the period is estimated:
- with fewer than three past periods: the difference of today carried to the end (no extrapolation of three days into a month);
- with three or more: what is known, plus the median past period's unscheduled movements for the part left; the worst and best past periods give a range (the 10th and 90th percentiles from ten periods on).

A budget opens on its ledger (lines and recurring movements of the period, newest first), its balance by day, week, month or year (planned movements dotted), and "Add a movement": once, or each month or year (`[[preset]]`, written in place, comments kept).
