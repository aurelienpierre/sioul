# Areas and hours: what comes when

Two questions decide what Sioul brings forward, and they are separate:
- **Who may reach you, and when**: five states, the same for mail, calls and other apps' messages (strangers, in none of your address books and on no list; the blocked, never; and for the people in your address books safe, neutral, restricted), your Always through people, and the one matrix of what reaches you, a row per state on each channel ([attention.md](attention.md)). That is about people.
- **What a thing is for, and whether now is for it**: areas and the five times, this page. That is about sources (an address, a site, a chat) and tasks.

They meet in one place, mail: everyone's mail comes at the times ticked for their row, and is told only while the address it came to is for what now is for (the notifications' decision 2 of 13 November 2026). Your safe senders' mail to an address for another time is shown on the Porch at their times and told once the address's hours begin; the others' (neutral, restricted, strangers) waits off the Porch until then. Your Always through people's mail comes to any address, and so does everyone's while you are Reachable. An address with nothing ticked has no closed hours for your safe senders: their mail to it is told at their times, as before; the others' waits for work's hours, as unsaid has always meant. Calls and other apps' messages follow their own matrices alone (but a mail app's notification on a phone, which follows the mail's rule). The codes and links you just asked a site for, and what you send yourself, are shown at once, whatever the time; a code is told at once too, but while you sleep.

This is a health matter before it is a filter. Work that reaches the evening keeps people from recovering, and detaching from work after hours is what recovery needs most (Sonnentag & Fritz 2007, 2015). The risk is highest for people who work from home or for themselves, for whom nothing else marks the end of the day. Admin left to spread over every evening weighs too, even when nobody is working.

## Areas: three switches
A source or a task is for **work**, for **your own admin** (bills, letters, offices, health errands), or for **leisure** (friends, family, chats, what you enjoy). These are switches, not a choice of one: an address for everything personal is for admin and leisure; a freelancer's bank account, used for both, is for work and admin; a chat used for work and for friends is for both.

`sioul_core::areas::Area`, written in files as `work`, `admin`, `leisure`, joined by `+` (`admin+leisure`). The older words still read: `personal` is admin and leisure, `mixed` is work and admin.

| Thing | Where its area is set | Unsaid |
|---|---|---|
| A mail address | Accounts, on its card: "What this address is for" (`[[account]] area`) | work: an address you did not classify never reaches your evenings |
| A budget | its form ("Edit"): "For" (`[[budget]] area`) | your admin; the older `personal` reads as leisure |
| A site | the site's ⋮ ▸ For (`area`) | by its type: secure mailboxes and client areas (offices, banks, suppliers) are admin; chats, social networks and dating, leisure; video calls and other sites, admin and leisure |
| A task | the task's panel, "For" (`X-SIOUL-AREA`) | by its tags and projects: work's (`[quiet] work`, GitHub), leisure's (joy, family, friends…), both personal ones (health), its projects' areas (a client's project unsaid, work's), else admin |
| A supply | its card: "What it is for" (`[[supply]] area`; [supplies.md](supplies.md)) | every time: a supply you never classified is never hidden |
| A project | its form: "What it is for" (`[[project]] area`; [projects.md](projects.md#what-a-project-is-for)) | a client's project, work; the older `personal`, admin and leisure, and all three beside a client; no client, nothing of its own: its tasks keep their categories, the Projects and Time pages take its open tasks' areas (`Project::area_of`, `area_shown`) |
| An agenda (a calendar or a task list) | Agenda ⚙ ▸ Your agendas, its line: "For" (`[agenda.calendars."<account>/<id>"] area`; [client.md](client.md#several-agendas)) | work when its account says work alone, else your admin and leisure: a work agenda follows the work hours, a personal one reaches you all day unless you say outside work hours |
| An app's notifications, on a phone | the phone's card in Settings ▸ Devices, under the app: "For" ([android.md](android.md#notifications-from-other-apps)) | any time: the matrix decides alone ([attention.md](attention.md)) |
| A device | Settings ▸ Devices, on its card: "For", said from any of your devices (`[device."<id>"] area`; [database.md](database.md#devices)), and its cells in the grid under the devices ([below](#what-each-device-is-for)) | everything: a device you never classified is for work, admin and leisure |

Mail addresses are the one place nothing is mixed for you: asking what each address is for is what separates a business address from a personal one.

**What a device is for narrows what comes forward on it**: each rule on this page that says what a thing's area brings now is read against the device's own line once you said something of it, and stays as written here until then ([What each device is for](#what-each-device-is-for)).

## Five times
What now is for is one of five times (`sioul_core::areas::Time`, decided by `sioul_core::quiet::mode`):

| Time | What it is | Set where |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | working hours; "A little longer", "Work … more" and "Work until tonight" too | Settings ▸ Hours |
| **Admin** | hours for your own admin | Settings ▸ Hours |
| **Meals** | each meal, from getting it ready to its end | Health: meals, and the day's changes ([health.md](health.md)) |
| **Sleep** | the night, from winding down to waking; each nap with its minutes to come back | Health: the night, naps |
| **Leisure** | every other time: evenings, days without hours, time off, a day closed early ("Work done for today"), Free time | nothing to set |

**Which comes first**, when they overlap: the pause, then sleep, then Free time, then meals, then the hours ([pauses.md](pauses.md)). The pause (« En pause ») holds everything Sioul shows, whatever the time, as sleep does (`Reason::Paused`), with its own column in the matrices of who may reach you; Free time (« Temps libre ») is leisure whatever the hour, until you come back or the night begins, and the working time it took moves the end of today's work later, within limits (`Reason::FreeTime`, `Reason::Extended`). A meal inside working hours is a meal while it lasts. "Work now" may last over a night: the night stays sleep. "Work done for today" is leisure until work comes back; "A little longer" and "Work now" are work. Time off is leisure. Work and admin hours at once bring what each of them brings, all together (`Time::Several`), until the first of them closes.

**The hour before bed is sleep's.** Health keeps the wind-down free, and the programmes it comes from make that hour free of what keeps the mind going (Harvey et al. 2021): a work message or a notification is exactly that. The status line says it apart: "Winding down: nothing disturbs until 07:00." The night's own notice at its time, the start of the wind-down, still comes: it is the one that says the night begins, and it offers to close the day ([reviews.md](reviews.md)). From the wind-down, the status line offers nothing more.

**A block taken out for one day** (off that day, on the Health page) is no meal or sleep that day; one made quiet that day (no notice) still is.

**No working or admin hours at all**: everything comes, as before any were set (`Time::Any`); meals and sleep keep their time when Health sets them. Leisure lets only your safe senders through: someone who has not set hours yet would see no other mail. Mail, calls and other apps' messages read such a time as working and admin hours together: the more open of a row's two cells (`attention::Now::of`, [attention.md](attention.md), Q14).

**No night set**: nights are leisure, and nothing keeps notifications away while you sleep. The Porch asks for it in a card, "Set my night" (Settings ▸ Hours, at the meals and night) or "Leave as is", which stops asking.

### What each time brings

| Time | Comes forward | Waits |
|---|---|---|
| Work | work; admin too, until admin has hours of its own; a call to an office, always (offices keep business hours) | leisure |
| Admin | admin; work too, while work has no hours | leisure |
| Leisure | leisure, and nothing else | work and admin |
| Meals | leisure: a meal is a break | work and admin, tasks for them |
| Sleep | leisure, if you open Sioul; no notification of any kind but doses | Tasks ▸ Now behind one sentence; every notification |
| No hours set at all | everything, as before any were set | nothing |

What waits, waits in the calm views alone ([below](#calm-views-and-full-views)): the full views show everything at any hour.

`sioul_core::areas::brought` is this table, and `areas::in_view` the whole rule for areas by the hours: a thing's area meets what the time brings (on a device you said something of, its cell for that time: [below](#what-each-device-is-for)); the attention model's `Attention::mail` is the rule for mail ([attention.md](attention.md)); the status line says what now is and until when: "Admin time until 19:00: offices, bills, letters.", "Meal until 13:00.", "Leisure until 22:00: what you enjoy.", "Sleep: nothing disturbs until 07:00.", and for the two pauses "Free time: only your safe senders, doses and codes reach you. Work comes back when you do." and "Paused."

### "Work now", gone {#work-now}
"Work now" went with the three states (decision 10 of 18 October 2026, [do-not-disturb.md](do-not-disturb.md#the-three-states)): Reachable took its part for people and the Porch, and the status line's menu its part for work, "Work half an hour more" to "Work four hours more" and **Work until tonight** (`Overrides::work_until` at the night's start, as Health knows it; offered only when a night is known). Nothing ends any more when Sioul starts or closes. A `work_now` an older Sioul wrote is still read as work until its end (`quiet::end_of_next_workday`, `Overrides::work_now`, `Reason::WorkNow`), and "Back to the usual hours", in the status line's menu, clears it. Meals and sleep still come first.

### When no hours are set
The Porch asks, in a card, for the hours not set yet (work, your admin), with "Set my hours", which opens Settings at them, and "Leave as is", which stops asking.

## Hours: work and admin
Settings ▸ Hours has two weeks: **working hours** and **hours for your admin**. Each day of each can be on or off, from a start to an end (`[[window]]`, with `kind = "admin"`; working hours have no kind). One sentence says that leisure is every other time, meals and sleep coming from Health, with a button to them. Time off (your vacations, and the holidays that count: [below](#vacations-and-holidays)) and a day closed early are leisure; "A little longer" is work.

**Leisure is not set.** Leisure is every time not given to work, admin, a meal or sleep; "Free time" is now the name of a pause you take ([pauses.md](pauses.md)). Hours an older Sioul set as leisure (`kind = "leisure"`, its "free time") stay in the file, untouched, read without a word and left aside (`Config::week_hours`); saving the other weeks keeps them.

### Vacations and holidays {#vacations-and-holidays}
Settings ▸ Hours ▸ **Vacations and holidays** (18 November 2026, in place of "Days off"; `sioul_core::vacations`, `VacationsCalendars.qml`; design-calendars §2). A day off is leisure all day (`Reason::TimeOff`, with its word), the plan lays nothing on it, "two working days before" skips it, work's reminders wait until work reopens after it, the agenda shades it and draws it at the head of its day, the pauses and the day's review have no end of work that day.
- **One function answers "is this a day off, and why"** (`vacations::DaysOff::on`), for every reader that knew days off: the time now (`quiet::mode`, `next_work`, `after_today`, `end_of_next_workday`, `frames`), the plan's closed days (`quiet::Situation`), the reminders' working days (`reminders`), the agenda's items (`timeline::days_off`), the reach of calls and messages (`reach::Clock`), notices, the agendas' filter (`agendas::Hours`), the pauses, the reviews, the home card, `sioul` and the MCP. A test reads the sources and fails when anything reads the list `[[time_off]]` itself (`vacations::tests::nothing_reads_the_days_off_list_but_this`).
- **Its sources**: the periods you type, `[[time_off]]` as before (`from`, `until`, `label`, `id`), a kind added (`kind`: `sick`, `other`; unsaid, a vacation), which an older Sioul passes over; then the whole days of each subscribed calendar of holidays that counts as your days off (`subscribed::Subscription::days_off`): **public holidays count by default, school holidays do not**, each with its switch. A typed period comes first when both cover a day.
- **What you typed stays as it is**: the same entries in the same place, written in place with their ids. **An entry that does not read** (no day, or no date) **is passed over** (`config::time_off_entries`), never the whole configuration, which stopped loading before; Settings keeps it in the file as it is when the list is saved.
- **Each device knows the holidays from its own copy** of the calendar: until it has read it once, it does not know them, and its line in Vacations and holidays says so; the calendar's events never travel between devices (calendars by their address, [client.md](client.md#calendars-by-their-address)).
- **Which countries' holidays are offered ready-made** (23 November 2026; the form "Subscribe to a calendar…", [client.md](client.md#calendars-by-their-address)): France's (public holidays by territory, Etalab's; school holidays by zone, the Ministry of Education's) and the United Kingdom's (the Government's bank holidays, one calendar per nation), your country's first. **Only a state's own calendar, published under an open licence, is offered.** The files Thunderbird publishes per country are built from a commercial provider's data without a licence that allows passing them on, and mix regional days and plain observances with the holidays: they are not offered. Germany, Belgium, Switzerland and the other countries wait for a source of that kind, or for their holidays worked out from each country's rules, which Sioul does not do; meanwhile any calendar of holidays may be subscribed to by its address and said to hold public or school holidays. The list is data (`crates/sioul-core/data/holiday-calendars.toml`), checked before each release (`tools/check-holidays.py`).
- **School holidays** that count (when you say so) take their days as the calendar shapes them: the Ministry's begin after class (the day the event starts is a class day) and end before the morning classes resume.
- **In the agenda**, a counted calendar's whole days are drawn as days off, in place of its own events, so that they show once; its timed events show as any event.
- **The books do not count public holidays yet** (the review's F26, a departure from the design): their due days (`books::working`, `nth_working`, `declarations::due_of`) still count Monday to Friday from their own settings, which a holidays calendar does not reach without threading its days through each of their callers; the day they say stays on the safe side, a day or two before the law's, never after.
- **In the availability you share, a day off is busy all day** (the design's decision 3; `availability::days_off_spans`, laid into the busy times' shared record, `availability::lay_days_off`): each day the one function says is a day off, from its midnight to the next, said "Busy" whatever its kind, days in a row one block. It is busy for others alone: do-not-disturb's busy stretches (`busy::stretches`) know nothing of it, so a day off never holds what reaches you by itself. Only a calendar of blocks Sioul keeps (a mirror) carries it: a server's own way (Google's free/busy, Nextcloud's) knows only the events of its calendars.

### Mail
Who may reach you when is the one matrix of [attention.md](attention.md): for mail, a row per state, strangers included, and Always through above them. The address it came to narrows it (`Attention::decide`, step 8): mail is told only while its address is for what now is for, the same rule that folds the address on the Mail page (`areas::in_view`; [below](#a-pages-filter-by-area)). Your safe senders' mail to an address for another time is shown on the Porch at their row's times (◑), and told with "The Porch opens" once the address's hours begin, when their row tells them there at some time of your week (●; a row that only shows them there, Quieter's leisure, decides alone: nothing waits for good); the others' (neutral, restricted, strangers) waits off the Porch until then. The exceptions: your Always through people's mail, told to any address at their row's times; Reachable, which lets every address's mail through; and an address whose card says nothing of what it is for (`mailbox::said_area` none, `attention::Event::for_address`): work's hours for the others, as always, but no closed hours for your safe senders, whose mail to it is told at their times, as before 13 November 2026 (review of phase 2, M8: most people leave their one personal address unsaid, and Saturday's mail from a friend waited for Monday). A mail app's notification on a phone takes the same rule, by the account its address belongs to: the address itself, its user name, the address with a "+tag" left aside, else the one account of its domain (an alias); an address none of your accounts holds has no area, its row alone deciding. When a row and an address never meet in your week (a stranger writing to an address for leisure, strangers coming only in work and admin time; a restricted sender writing to your personal address), their row alone decides, so that no mail waits for good. Until 13 November 2026 your safe senders' mail was told to any address: a colleague you marked safe rang in the evening on your work address (decision 2 of the notifications' redesign changed it, [notifications.md](notifications.md#mail)). While admin has no hours of its own, working hours take its cells too; while work has none, admin hours take work's, as areas are lent. Budgets follow the areas, by what each is for (your admin when unsaid).

### Sites
The sites for now are listed; the others fold under one line, "Other hours: 3", opened with a click. A site's notifications wait until a gathered notification falls in its hours (see [sites.md](sites.md)). A site in real time, and a call, come at once, but only while its area fits now, and never while you sleep.

### Sleep
While you sleep (`Mode::sleeps`): the Porch shows codes and what your lists let through now, a message about a project among the people you know, no money and no paper letters; every address rests on the Mail page, and its filter ticks nothing ([below](#a-pages-filter-by-area)); only leisure sites are listed; Tasks ▸ Now waits behind the status line's sentence and **Show anyway**, a thought noted for later in the line under the tabs, the page's other tabs in reach and showing everything; Projects and Time tick leisure alone in their filter, everything one tick away. **Almost nothing notifies**, as usual (the matrix's sleep column, [attention.md](attention.md)): no site, no new mail (your safe senders' is shown, not told), no message, no call but your Always through people's, no reminder before a date (but an event's own reminders when the event itself is in the night: you chose it, [reminders.md](reminders.md)), no pause to move, no meal or nap notice. The codes you just asked for are not told: only their card waits on the Porch, and no notification comes at waking either, the code being over by then (decision 5 of the notifications' redesign, 13 November 2026); **At once** in Codes × Sleep tells them anyway. What waits comes at waking: reminders while they still make sense, new mail that waited ("The Porch opens: …"), sites' notifications at the next gathered time. Dose reminders come all the same: you set their times, and a dose at 05:00 is meant to wake you. Settings ▸ What reaches you ▸ Sioul's own notices, the doses' sleep cell: At once (the default) or Later; later, a dose's reminder comes at waking, and so does the question on doses due while Sioul was closed. Sioul never silences a dose without that choice.

### Tasks
Tasks ▸ Now keeps what fits (`quiet::QuietTasks`): what is for leisure in leisure and during a meal, none during sleep; the Agenda's What comes lists the steps of the same tasks, those of a task hidden now named "Work" on a coming day and left out today. The Tasks page's other tabs and the Agenda's Day and Week show every task whatever the hour ([below](#calm-views-and-full-views)). The next step is never one that waits for something still open, and never a call to an office that is closed now. A task needing an office uses its office's own opening hours when it has them ([tasks.md](tasks.md)). The plan gives tasks room in working and admin hours; leisure has no hours, so what is only for leisure takes no room and waits for none.

## Calm views and full views {#calm-views-and-full-views}
Each page shows one kind of thing. Its calm view comes first and follows the hours; its full views, one tab away, are for managing, and hide nothing by the hour: having to fight a page for a whole view weighs more than the view itself, and opening the agenda in the evening without tomorrow's plan in it is the more stressful (the settings rework, 18 November 2026).

| Page | Calm view: follows the hours | Full views: hide nothing by the hour |
|---|---|---|
| Tasks | Now (`quiet::QuietTasks`; asleep, behind its cover) | The day, List, Board, Gantt, Search: every task, its lists left out by your word alone (`agendas::hidden_by_hand`) |
| Supplies | For now: the supplies of what now is for (`areafilter::hours_say`), one line each; how many are kept for other times, one quiet line | All supplies: every supply, its stock, threshold and prices, the archived folded ([supplies.md](supplies.md)) |
| Agenda | What comes: the agendas the hours admit, today's steps of a task hidden now left out, a coming day's named "Work" | Day and Week: every step, today's as the coming days' (`work::today_laid_out` lays every task), named as itself; the Month, its appointments and dates, no step, as always; in all three, the agenda filter by hand alone (`FilterNow::hidden_by_hand`, `shown_as` full): a set chosen by its button shows alone there too (`hidden_by_hand_with_set`), a set a time brings does not apply there; the steps by area, by hand (below) |

Money ▸ Overview, by Budgets' filter, is a calm view too. Mail, Budgets, Projects and Time are lists with a filter by area ([below](#a-pages-filter-by-area)): ticked as the hours say, everything one tick away. Show's own switch for steps (the Agenda's Show menu, per device) is yours, in every view. The plan is the same in every view: a work task gets its time in work hours (`plan::Settings::usable`); the views differ in what they show of it. **Today is laid twice** (`dayview::today`): from every task, for the Day and the Week to draw; from the tasks the calm views keep now on this device, as it was always laid, whose slots of time for you are the ones in force, written for every process (notifications, and calls where a cell holds them, read them: [capacity.md](capacity.md)), and drawn in the Day in place of its own (`dayview::with_slots_of`; `dayview::tests::the_slots_of_time_for_you_are_those_of_the_tasks_kept`).

## A page's filter by area {#a-pages-filter-by-area}
Mail's **All addresses**, the Budgets, and the Projects and Time pages show what the hours admit, as a filter you may change at once. Code: `sioul_core::areafilter` (the hours' ticks, yours, when yours end, the button's words), `sioul_core::mailbox::Filter` and `hours_say` (Mail's), `crates/sioul-app/src/mail.rs` (`mailbox_filter`, kept on this device), `crates/sioul-app/src/payments.rs` (`budget_filter`), `crates/sioul-app/src/projects.rs` (`filter`, `page_rows`, `time`), `qml/AreaFilter.qml` (the component).
- **Three boxes**, Work, Admin and Leisure, behind one button that says what shows ("Admin · Leisure"; "Nothing ticked"), so that no state is hidden ([research.md](research.md), finding 5). Ticked as the hours say, since work cues in the evening undo recovery (finding 21) (`areafilter::hours_say`: each area alone that comes forward now on this device, `Mode::brings`): work in working hours, and admin with it while admin has no hours of its own; admin in admin hours, and work while work has none; leisure in leisure and at meals; everything when no hours are set. Mail ticks nothing while you sleep or pause (`mailbox::hours_say`), as every address rested then.
- **Your ticks hold until the hours change**, everything adjustable (finding 10), nothing to set back by hand: until the time they were ticked in ends (`Mode::until`), or the hours say otherwise before it (a day closed early, a pause), and a day at most (`areafilter::LONGEST`), so that a weekend of leisure or a time with no end known never keeps them for good. Then the hours' ticks come back, and the window's minute forgets yours (`settle`), so that a later time saying the same never brings them back. **As the hours say** forgets them at once; ticking what the hours say is the same.
- **The addresses** are switches beside it, every one on unless switched off. What shows is the mail of the addresses switched on whose area meets the ticks (`Filter::shows`); a message two addresses hold shows when one of them does, with both marks. An address whose area is unsaid counts as work, as everywhere (`mailbox::area_of`).
- **Nothing else explains it**: no line per resting address; one "?" in the filter says it in short. When it shows no address, the list says nothing at all: the filter's own state says why.
- **Kept on this device** with the mailbox's chip (`$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/mailbox-view.toml`: `off`, the addresses switched off; `[areas]`, your ticks while they hold, the hours' ticks they were made under and their end), never shared. An older Sioul reads the chip and leaves the rest; its "one address alone" (`account`) is read by none.
- **On a phone**, the same in the folders' sheet, each address a box with its whole address, within 412 points.
- **Budgets** ([accounting.md](accounting.md)): by each budget's area (`Budget::area_of`), leisure ticked at meals and at night so that the page is never empty; kept in `budgets-view.toml`.
- **The Agenda's Day and Week, and Tasks ▸ The day**: the plan's steps by their task's area, as the plan reads it (`TaskAreas::of`); a step, its task's time block and its done mark drawn while its area is ticked, nothing standing in for one left out (`agenda::step_left_out`). A full view: **every area ticked** unless you say, and **your ticks kept** on this device in `agenda-view.toml` (`step_areas`) until you change them, never brought back by the hours; **Everything** ticks the three, **As the hours say** ticks what now is for on this device (`areafilter::hours_say`), as your word. It filters steps only: events follow the agendas' filter, meals, the night and doses Show, and with Show ▸ Steps off it is hidden. What comes and Now have none.
- **Projects and Time**, one filter for both, both showing projects: a project shows while its area (`Project::area_shown`: a client's project unsaid is work's; one with neither a word nor a client, its open tasks' areas, else whatever the ticks) meets the ticks; "No project for what is ticked." when none does; on the Time page, time on a project not ticked folds, a stretch with no project counting by its task's area and time noted on nothing always showing (`timereport::StretchAreas`: each project's area read once per reading of the page, the page read off the window's thread), the total said with the whole beside it, a partial line said partial, what is left to bill never folded; one project chosen shows whatever the ticks, the filter put away. Ticked as the hours say (`areafilter::hours_say`: leisure alone at night, so that what is yours stays in reach); kept on this device in `$XDG_STATE_HOME/sioul/projects-view.toml` (`[areas]`), never shared. Neither page has a cover or a "Show anyway" ([projects.md](projects.md)).
- **Agents** keep their own rules (`mcp.md`): the filter is the window's only.

## What each device is for {#what-each-device-is-for}
A third question joins the two above: **on which device**. A computer for work, a phone for your own admin and for leisure, a tablet on the sofa: Settings ▸ Devices says what each is for, from any of them, and each device then brings forward only what it is for, at the hours for it. Decided on 17 November 2026: the three areas are the device's too; calls and texts stay out of it; a device folds what is not for it and still holds the data.

**The rule.** Each time brings a set of areas (`areas::brought`: the table of "What each time brings"; `areas::in_view` is "the thing's area meets that set"). A device has five **cells**, one per time: the areas that come forward on it then (`areas::Line::cell`). A cell is what the time brings, kept to what the device is for; a cell you changed is the whole answer for that device at that time. A thing comes forward on a device when its area meets that device's cell for now (`areas::Here::brings`, asked through `quiet::Mode::brings` and `attention::Now::brings`).

| | Work | Admin | Leisure, meals | Sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A computer for work | work | nothing | nothing | nothing |
| A phone for admin and leisure | admin, while admin has no hours of its own | admin | leisure | leisure, if opened |
| A device nothing is said of | as the hours say | as the hours say | as the hours say | as the hours say |

- **A cell you changed** may hold an area the time does not bring (friends reaching a phone during work: `work+leisure` there), or nothing at all (`none`). "As its areas say" puts it back. It is drawn apart in the grid.
- **Work and admin hours at once**: both cells together. **No hours set at all**: no time is known to read a cell for, so the cell is what the device is for.
- **With nothing said, nothing changes.** A device whose line says nothing (no area, no cell) narrows nothing: every answer is the hours' own, whatever the other devices' lines say. A thing with no area keeps the hours' answer on every device. Nothing is narrowed anywhere until you say what a device is for.
- **An area no device is for is narrowed by none** (`Here::unheld`). When no device that counts brings an area at any time of the week, each device brings it as the hours say: a phone lost, forgotten or silent for a week never makes what was for it vanish from the others. A device alone therefore narrows nothing. Settings ▸ Devices says it under the grid: "No device is for leisure: until one is, that comes on every device, as the hours say." Only the times your week has count: a cell in force at a time your week lacks (admin's, while admin has no hours of its own; meals', while Health sets none) holds nothing, and what a time lends counts (admin in work's cell while admin has no hours, work in admin's while work has none). A device counts as the doses count it (`sioul_core::health::counts`: not said off, still sharing, heard within a week); one whose Sioul is older counts as bringing nothing ("Which device tells", below).
- **Kept in the shared settings**: `[device."<id>"] area = "work"`, and under it `[device."<id>".at] work = "work+leisure"`, one key per cell you changed (`work`, `admin`, `leisure`, `meals`, `sleep`; `none` for nothing). An older Sioul keeps the table as it is. **Each device says its own cells in its sealed entry** (`devices::Entry::cells`), as it reads its own line of the settings in its own week: what the others act on is what each device says of itself, never the settings, which a device may not share, or not yet. Its card in the grid says so when the two differ: "Its own settings say otherwise for now: it follows those until yours reach it." A line you change is written with when you changed it (`changed`, `device_said::Said::changed`): a device that is not running, whose line you changed after it last said its own, and whose entry says it shares the part "settings", is read by your line, since it will follow it once it runs (review of phase 2, N3). On a device whose own settings do not travel, a sentence under the grid says it may tell again what another device told.

**Shown.** What is not for this device now folds exactly as what is not for this time folds, and the same doors open it:

| Where | What folds | Its door |
|---|---|---|
| Mail | the filter's ticks (`areafilter::hours_say`): an address whose area is not ticked | the filter's three boxes |
| Sites | under "Other hours" | a click on that line |
| Tasks | the Now view (`quiet::QuietTasks`) | The day, List, Board, Gantt and Search, which show every task whatever the hour or the device |
| The Agenda | What comes: the steps of a task not for now ("Work"), the agendas the hours leave out | the Day and the Week, which show every step, and with the Month every agenda but those you turned off |
| The Porch | mail to an address this device is not for: your safe senders' shown, not told; the others' off the Porch; sites' news, money, the agenda's conflicts | **Show all mail now**; each thing's own page |
| Budgets, Projects, Time | the filter's ticks | the filter's three boxes |
| Another app's notifications with a "For", on a phone | its automatons, held while the phone is for that area at no time; people's messages come as the hours say, since no other device can tell them for the phone (a device never lets through what the hours hold) | Sioul's list of what it holds |

Where the device is the reason and not the hour, the words say so: the status line ("Leisure until 22:00: what you enjoy. This device is for work: leisure waits on your other devices."; where you changed the cell, "On this device, at this time, leisure waits on your other devices: you set it so in Settings ▸ Devices."), the filter's "?", and an address's resting line (`areafilter::device_line`, `Mode::elsewhere`).

### Opened elsewhere {#opened-elsewhere}
What is opened through a door on a device that is not for it is not settled by it, so that it is not forgotten on the device it is for (research.md, finding 85: mail read on the phone was forgotten at the office). A device is not for a thing when it keeps it back now, or is for its area at no time of the week (`quiet::Mode::for_another`); an address is read as the fold reads it, one nobody classified as work's (`mailbox::area_of`, `mail::read_here_only`); nothing said of a device, it is for everything, as before.

- **A message** opened there is **read here only**: never marked read on its server, so it stays new everywhere else; on this device it shows read (`view::read_here`, this device's own list, `read-here.txt` in its state folder, sixty days). The reader says "Read here only: this device is not for its address, so it stays new on the device that is." and **Mark as read** marks it read everywhere (`\Seen`).
- **Done for now** pressed after **Show all mail now** closes the Porch on what this device is for: an address that is another device's stays open on the device it is for.
- A site's news goes when the site is opened on that computer, a task when it is done, as before: neither is shared by being shown.

### Which device tells {#which-device-tells}
One device tells, as before devices: the one used last keeps the "notices" lease ([attention.md](attention.md#which-device-tells)). It tells what comes forward on it now, by its own cells. What does not waits, shown where it belongs, and is told with "The Porch opens" once a device it comes forward on is the one used, which then keeps the notices. It waits for another device only if that device counts, says in its own entry that it brings the thing's area at some time of the week, and says it acts on the keeper's own cells (`devices::Entry::known`); otherwise the keeper tells it. Every other device claims a thing without a word only when it is sure the keeper tells it (the told log, or the keeper's own cells, in the same hours, read from its entry as the folder holds it and only when that is the very copy this device acts on, or a keeper that narrows nothing, or, for an older Sioul, a thing that arrived more than the two clocks' margin after it took the notices); otherwise it keeps it, and tells it once it keeps the notices itself. Never lost comes before never doubled: a device whose settings do not travel, and a time boundary the told log has not crossed yet, may tell a thing twice. A device whose Sioul is older tells everything while it keeps the notices, as before, and counts as bringing nothing: while another device keeps them, it claims every letter without a word, as before devices, so nothing is ever left waiting for it.

### The rest, the same on every device
**What a device's areas never keep back**: doses and everything of Health (their own lease, untouched; the dose path reads the hours alone, `hours::hours_mode_at`, the notices' own moment included, `tell::moment`); the codes you just asked for; alarms and an event's own reminders, and the time to leave for it; your Always through people's mail and messages; everything while you are Reachable (sites and their gathered news, dates, the books' thresholds, other apps' automatons and those set "at once": Reachable comes before every device step, `areas::tests::reachable_comes_before_every_device_step`); **Show all mail now**; your safe senders' mail to an address nobody classified. Phone calls and texts keep their own matrix, and a call in a site rings where it rings, as a phone's call does.

**A task's reminder** comes on the devices for its task's own area (`reminders::Holds::areas`, `attention::Event::device`), at the time its `work` flag says, as before: an admin task's date, out of view when work starts, comes then on the laptop for admin, never on the computer for work. A device for that area at no time of the week holds it while another computer is; a phone tells no date reminder, so it holds none for later.

**The plan stays yours.** The plan gives a task room in the hours of its area on every device alike (`plan::Room::usable`), and "this row and this address never meet in your week" reads the week alone (`Attention::meets_at`): neither reads a device. Agents (`sioul mcp`) read the hours alone too.

**The grid**, in Settings ▸ Devices: under the devices, a cell per device and per time your week has, in words ("Work", "Admin · Leisure", "Nothing"). A cell opens three boxes and "As its areas say". In a wide window the cells line up as one matrix, devices by times; on a phone each card says its cells one line per time. One sentence says now for this device: "Now, leisure until 22:00: leisure comes here." A device whose Sioul is older says "Its Sioul is older: everything still comes there." `sioul devices` prints the same, and `sioul devices for` and `sioul devices at` set a device's areas and a cell.

**What it does not do.** A device that folds what is not for it still fetches and holds it: on a device shared with others, folding is not privacy. A work phone still rings for calls outside work. A device has no hours of its own. Each is left for later.

Why: people already give their devices a role, those who keep work apart more than others, and what goes with less distress is control over the line, whichever way one draws it ([research.md](research.md), findings 83 to 85). So nothing is narrowed until you say so, every cell can be changed and put back, what is folded says why and opens in one gesture, and what must reach you reaches every device.

## What is not decided for you
- **A bank account used for both.** It is for work and admin: it comes in work time and in admin time, not in leisure. Sioul does not split its movements between business and personal. Your accountant decides that, and in France a micro-entrepreneur needs a separate account once turnover passes €10,000 two years running. Keeping the business account apart keeps its alerts out of your evenings.
- **Admin hours inside office hours.** If your admin hours are all in the evening, calls to offices still come in working hours, when offices answer. Give admin a slot during the day (Tuesday 14:00–16:00) if you would rather keep work time for work.
- **No hours at all.** Nothing changes until you set some. Setting only admin hours keeps work in them (work comes in admin hours while it has none of its own); outside them, leisure.
